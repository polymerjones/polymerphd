# Topic Reference 01 — Evidence, Attributions and Confidence Levels

This file exists because the source collection does something unusual for health content: **it grades its own claims.**

Nearly every video in the corpus ends by separating what it presents as established from what it presents as inference, hypothesis or extrapolation. That separation is part of the source material, and it is preserved here rather than flattened. When this package states something firmly, it is because the source did. When it hedges, that hedge came from the source too.

# 3. How the collection grades itself

Every video ends with a confidence statement. Reading across all 190, the grading follows a consistent four-tier pattern.

## Tier 1 — Presented as established

Anatomy, measured physical constants, replicated clinical findings with named cohorts, and physics that can be calculated rather than observed. The collection states these without hedging, and so does this package.

Examples: intradiscal pressures by posture; the 2 cm minimum toe clearance; cervical load by flexion angle; the 200 ms reactive window; nitric oxide's vasodilation pathway; the 20-second oxytocin latency; illuminance values indoors versus out; the 12–16 hour autophagy threshold; caffeine's receptor-blocking mechanism.

## Tier 2 — Measured outcome, inferred mechanism

The collection is careful about this distinction, and flags it repeatedly. **The result happened; the explanation for why is the video's reasoning.**

`CtM-hB2oe4Q` states it most plainly: *"the measured outcomes are real, but several of the proposed mechanisms are inferences."*

Three are worth naming, because they demonstrate the standard the rest of the corpus is measured against.

**`RYBagD7_RQM` (gardening without gloves)** carries **four separate self-labelled inferences**, each isolated from the firm material:
1. **Default mode network suppression during soil contact** — the principle is established across sensory modalities; the soil-specific application is the video's inference, not imaged.
2. **Transdermal mineral absorption** — physics established, gradients favourable, **human measurements sparse, evidence preliminary.**
3. and 4. Similar in kind.

# 5. Where the collection disagrees with itself

Of the 190 videos, **187 explicitly note points of tension with other videos in the collection.** Under the canonical-knowledge rule, these are preserved rather than resolved — but most of them turn out not to be genuine contradictions once the mechanisms are laid side by side.

The resolutions are documented in `03_BIOLOGICAL_MECHANISMS.md`. In summary:

| Apparent conflict | Resolution |
|---|---|
| **Cortisol as anti-inflammatory vs pro-inflammatory** | Acute versus chronic. Chronic exposure downregulates the receptor, inverting the effect |
| **Chronically elevated cortisol vs depleted baseline** | Two different configurations. Most videos describe elevation; `wrc2SDiMsOU` describes long-standing trauma producing a **depleted baseline with amplified reactivity.** Both stated |
| **Muscle efficiency ~25% vs ATP hydrolysis efficiency ~40%** | Different measurements of different things. Both correct |
| **Two limits on shivering** | Glycogen exhaustion at 3–4 h; enzymatic stalling below 30 °C. Different limits, both real |
| **Gait speed vs gait variability** | **Speed predicts mortality; variability predicts falls.** Different variables |
| **Wind vs canopy stillness** | Different benefits requiring incompatible conditions. Have both at different times |
| **Birdsong vs moving water as the active agent** | Different outcomes through different channels — acoustic-limbic versus pulmonary-immune chemical |
| **Phytoncide emission: ~5 kg vs ~10 kg per hectare per day** | **Stated as a range.** `Adh1myK3_CE` and `gyOTlBlFEuo` say ~5; `6XmOWCCPh-c` says ~10 |
| **Weekly vs monthly forest visits** | Both follow correctly from the decay curve — the weekly schedule targets the 7-day window, the monthly the 30-day |
| **Sitting interval: 15–20 min vs 45 min** | Different tissues with different time constants. The shorter interval satisfies both |

**The grounding/zeta potential case is the one genuine confidence conflict.** `Adh1myK3_CE` and `6XmOWCCPh-c` explicitly label it their weakest evidence; `gyOTlBlFEuo` states it without hedging. **This package carries the hedged framing**, per the rule favouring the more detailed and more context-specific source.

# 6. What the collection does not claim

Reading across 190 videos, several boundaries hold consistently and are worth stating because they define what this package is for.

- **It does not diagnose.** Symptom-to-mechanism mapping is offered as explanation, not identification.
- **It routes medication decisions to prescribers**, every time, without exception. See `topic_reference_09_MEDICATION_INTERACTIONS.md`.
- **It does not claim practices replace treatment.** The recurring formulation is that a practice reaches the same target through a different route — with the drug's route left intact.
- **It calibrates magnitude.** `hxc0opwYlyA` on facial-feedback chemistry: *"modest — not equivalent to a dose of medication."*
- **It states what a single intervention cannot do.** `hCW0TehxqEI`: sleeplessness has several causes, this addresses the one you can change in 60 seconds, **and 60 seconds does not reverse all of them.**
- **It refuses to resolve genuine trade-offs.** `OUln7rLXg3M` on one-meal-a-day: *"The physics does not advocate… The trade-off is the physics. The decision is yours."*
- **It names its own limits.** `sBNDNOU1VH8`: physics can describe everything love does to the body, and **cannot explain why any of it feels like anything at all.**

# 7. How to read confidence in this package

When a statement in these files appears **without qualification**, the source stated it without qualification.

When you see phrases like *"presented as,"* *"the video's inference,"* *"stated as a range,"* *"flagged as the weakest evidence,"* or *"plausible but unmeasured"* — **that language came from the source material**, and it is there deliberately.

Every claim in this package traces to a note in the working corpus, and every note carries `[mm:ss]` anchors back to the video it came from. The video IDs cited throughout resolve through `09_SOURCE_CATALOG.md` to title, URL, upload date and duration.

**Nothing in this package originates outside the transcripts.**
