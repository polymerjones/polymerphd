# Combined Glossary

## First Aid

# 07 — Plain English Glossary

Every technical term introduced across this knowledge package, defined the way the source material
defines it. Terms are alphabetical.

---

## A

**AED** — an automated external defibrillator, used interchangeably with "defibrillator" in the
source material: the device that analyzes heart rhythm and advises whether a shock is needed.

## C

**Cold water shock** — the sudden loss of the ability to control breathing on entering cold water.
Fighting the sensation, rather than floating through it, is what the source material describes as
the actual mechanism of drowning.
