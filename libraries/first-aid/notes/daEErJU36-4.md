# Heart Attack vs Cardiac Arrest: The Difference Could Save a Life

## Central claim

**A heart attack and a cardiac arrest are different emergencies needing different responses: a
heart attack is a circulation problem where the person is usually conscious and talking and needs
to be sat down, kept calm, and given their own medication while emergency services are called; a
cardiac arrest is an electrical problem where the person is unresponsive and not breathing, and
needs immediate CPR and a defibrillator.** `[00:00]` "A heart attack is a circulation problem. It's
a blocked artery that stops blood reaching the heart muscle... Whereas a cardiac arrest is an
electrical problem. The heart stops pumping all together."

## Distinguishing the two at a glance

| | Heart attack | Cardiac arrest |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanism (as stated) | Circulation problem — blocked artery | Electrical problem — heart stops pumping |
| Consciousness | Usually conscious, breathing, talking | Unresponsive |
| Common signs named | Chest pain or pain spreading to arm/jaw, sweaty/clammy, feeling sick | Not breathing normally |
| Immediate action (as stated) | Sit down, keep calm, call EMS, give own prescribed medication | Call EMS now, start CPR now, get a defibrillator |

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

Heart attack: chest pain or pain radiating through the arm or jaw, sweating/clamminess, feeling
sick, while conscious and breathing. Cardiac arrest: unresponsive, not breathing normally.
