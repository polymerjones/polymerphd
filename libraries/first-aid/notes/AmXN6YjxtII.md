# Child Collapses & Not Breathing — What To Do (CPR + AED)

## Central claim

**When a child collapses and is not responding, the source's protocol is: send someone for a
defibrillator, check breathing for up to 10 seconds, give five rescue breaths if not breathing,
then begin one-handed compressions to one-third body depth in a 15-compressions-to-2-breaths
cycle, continuing until the AED arrives and its prompts can be followed.** `[00:00]` "You look,
listen, and feel for 10 seconds. If they're not breathing, you're going to give five rescue
breaths. Then you're going to start the compressions using one hand pushing down 1/3 of body
depth. You're going to do 15 compressions and then two breaths."

## AED use and pad placement

`[00:00]` Pad choice: pediatric pads if available, adult pads if not. Adult-pad placement is
specified precisely — "the first one is going to the left of the midline of the chest," and "the
second one is going to go straight behind that on their back." `[01:01]` The device analyzes and
announces "shock advised or no shock advised"; if advised, the responder pushes the shock button,
allows the shock, "and then carry on with your CPR."

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

A child who is unresponsive and not breathing normally — checked by the look/listen/feel method
for up to 10 seconds.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **AED** — used interchangeably with "defibrillator" in the source: "the defibrillator, otherwise
  known as an AED." `[00:00]`
