# CPR in a Bed? Yes – New CPR Guidelines Explained!

## Central claim

**Per the source's stated 2025 guideline update, CPR should be started immediately on a bed rather
than delaying to move the person to a hard floor first, compensating for the soft mattress by
pushing slightly deeper.** `[00:00]` "The new guidelines state it's better to start CPR on the bed
than to delay... don't drag them off, take the pillows away, kneel beside them, and start
compressions straight away."

## What changed, and why

`[00:00]` The prior practice, as described: "we used to be told that a soft mattress stops you
pushing deep enough, so we had to move them to a hard floor first." The stated reason for the
change: "moving someone wastes precious seconds, and every second without CPR lowers the chance of
survival." The source frames the trade-off directly: **"starting now beats starting perfectly."**
