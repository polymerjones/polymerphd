# The #1 First Aid Skill Isn't CPR

## Central claim

**Asked what the most important first-aid skill is, the speaker says it is not CPR, AED use, or
treating severe bleeding — it is recognizing that something is seriously wrong and calling
emergency medical services early.** `[00:00]` "Surprisingly, my answer is not CPR, using the AED,
or even treating severe bleed. It's recognizing when something is seriously wrong, and calling the
emergency medical services early."

## Why people delay, and the source's fix

`[00:00]` The stated reason people fail at this is social, not medical: "too many people wait
because they don't want to make a fuss." The source's stated fix is simple and repeated twice —
"trust your instincts" and call for help — rather than any diagnostic checklist for what counts as
"seriously wrong."
