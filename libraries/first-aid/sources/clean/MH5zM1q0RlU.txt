[00:00] If someone's struggling in the water, your instinct is to go in after them. Please don't. People drown trying to rescue people. You become the second casualty. Call 999 and if you're at the coast, ask for the coast guard. If you're inland or river or lake or canal, ask for the fire rescue. Then shout at the casualty and tell them to float. Head back, ears in the water, relax. It costs you nothing and it buys them minutes. Throw them anything buoyant. A ring, a body board, an empty bottle, a cool bag or reach. Reach with a towel, a branch or a rope. Lay flat, stay low and keep your weight back so you don't get pulled in. Reach, throw, do not go. And if you're the one who falls in, float. Cold water shock takes your breathing away and fighting it is what drowns people. Send this to whoever you're going to the water with. Stay safe.
