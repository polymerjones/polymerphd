[00:00] If someone is found collapsed in a bed, check if they're breathing for up to 10 seconds. If they're not breathing normally, call 999 straight away. Now, here's what's changed with the 2025 guidelines. You can now keep them on the bed. We used to be told that a soft mattress stops you pushing deep enough, so we had to move them to a hard floor first. But moving someone wastes precious seconds, and every second without CPR lowers the chance of survival. So, the new guidelines state it's better to start CPR on the bed than to delay. So, don't drag them off, take the pillows away, kneel beside them, and start compressions straight away. Just pushing a little bit deeper to make up for the soft mattress.
