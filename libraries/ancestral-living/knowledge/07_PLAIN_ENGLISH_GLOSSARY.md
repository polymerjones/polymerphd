# 07 — Plain English Glossary

Every technical term introduced across this knowledge package, defined the way the source material
defines it. Terms are alphabetical.

---

## C

**Constraints-led approach** — using an object, template, or step-by-step breakdown ("scaffolding")
to shape how a student solves a movement problem, rather than the object being the point of the
exercise.

## D

**Differential learning** — deliberately varying a movement slightly on each repetition (e.g.
moving a hand's position) to expose a learner to more variation rather than one fixed pattern.

## E

**Emulation** — the term used for the peer-engagement effect of training natural movement in a
large group rather than alone.

## N

**Natural method** — the roughly hundred-year-old French physical education tradition based on
Georges Hébert's work, distinct from and older than MovNat.

## P

**Points of support** — the parts of the body in contact with the ground or another surface at a
given moment; how these change as the body moves between positions is used to teach body
awareness.
