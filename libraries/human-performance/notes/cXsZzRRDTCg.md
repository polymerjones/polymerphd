# The Productivity Apps I Trust With My Life

## Central claim

**Switching from mainstream productivity tools (Gmail, Chrome, Google Drive) to more private
equivalents turned out to be simpler, not harder, and increased his productivity — because tools
that don't monetize attention tend to be built to just work well rather than to pull users back
in.** `[01:00]` "What I found was that a more private setup and a more simpler setup were basically
the same thing... my productivity went up significantly more." Later, stated as a general
principle: `[17:22]` "When a tool's business model isn't your attention, it tends to be a better
tool because it's just trying to work well, not keep you sucked into the app all the time."

## The stack, with stated tradeoffs

`[03:05]`-`[15:19]`:

| Tool | Role | Privacy claim | Stated tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Proton** (Mail, Drive, Calendar, Pass, VPN, and new Docs/Sheets/Zoom-alternative) | Core suite, replacing Google Workspace | Switzerland-based; end-to-end encryption where the company itself holds no decryption keys | New Docs/Sheets apps are "a little bit more simplistic" than Google's equivalents |
| **Notesnook** | Notes and to-do lists | End-to-end encrypted, open source | None stated; praised for a plain, uncluttered editor |
| **Brave browser** | Web browsing | Chromium-based (runs Chrome extensions), blocks ads/trackers by default | None stated |
| **Recall AI** | "Second brain" / saved-content search and summarization | **Not private** — content is processed on Recall's servers by AI | States directly: "I would not store sensitive information there" |
| **Obsidian** (being tested, not yet adopted) | Alternative second-brain, fully local | Notes live on-device; no server unless a sync plugin is added | More setup required; less automatic organization than Recall |
| **Claude (AI)** | Reasoning/writing/research assistant | **Not private** — conversations processed on Anthropic's servers, not end-to-end encrypted | Named alternatives (Proton's Lumo, Venice AI) are more private but "don't work for my workflow near as well" |

**He is explicit about not treating "private" as all-or-nothing:** `[16:22]` "I'm not just telling
you to build a fully private setup... you need to figure out what your threat model is." He also
flags his own commercial relationships directly rather than only in a description disclaimer:
Proton is not a sponsor but he has a disclosed affiliate discount link alongside a non-affiliate
one; he has no financial relationship with Recall, Notesnook, Brave, Obsidian, or Claude.

## A stated rejection of productivity-system complexity

`[08:13]` — describes having previously taken "a $2,000 course on the PARA method," concluding
**"it doesn't last... I have come to believe that that is the mask of productivity... Productivity
is from actually doing things, not optimizing your notes."** His actual to-do system: a single
running checklist in Notesnook, unchanged for over a year.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **End-to-end encryption**, defined precisely as he uses it: encryption that happens on-device
  before data leaves it, such that even the service provider holds no key to decrypt it — contrasted
  with services that claim "end-to-end encryption" but retain decryption capability `[03:05]`.
- **Second brain** — a searchable, cross-linked personal knowledge base built from saved articles/
  videos/notes; he states skepticism that most people need one `[11:15]`.
