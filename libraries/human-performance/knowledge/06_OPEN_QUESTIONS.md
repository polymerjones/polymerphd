# 06 — Open Questions

What this corpus doesn't yet resolve, or leaves genuinely unsettled — either because the sources
themselves flag uncertainty, or because two sources point in different directions.

## Flagged as uncertain by the sources themselves

- **Whether specific mortality/risk comparisons across different causes are apples-to-apples** —
  more than one video compares a lifestyle risk factor (loneliness, chronic stress) to smoking in
  magnitude, while explicitly cautioning that the comparison is illustrative, not a precise
  equivalence.
- **How much of a cited sex-based injury-risk gap (ACL tears) is pure biology versus differences in
  training history, coaching, and resource access** — named directly as a live scientific debate in
  the source covering it, not resolved one way or the other.
- **Whether population-level "Blue Zones"-style longevity research holds up** — one source uses the
  concept of long-lived, socially tight-knit communities as an illustration while explicitly stating
  the broader research on why certain regions produce so many long-lived people is still debated
  among scientists.
- **The precise dose-response relationship for several emerging findings** — high-dose creatine for
  acute sleep deprivation, and a proposed link between creatine and Alzheimer's progression, are
  both named as promising but based on small, early-stage studies rather than settled evidence.

## Real tensions between sources in this library

- See `05_CROSS_SOURCE_COMPARISON.md` for the coffee/mineral-depletion-versus-mortality-benefit
  tension, and the explore-small-failures-versus-protect-few-priorities framing, neither of which
  this collection resolves definitively.

## Gaps in current coverage

- Neither creator's videos in this collection address female-specific hormonal cycles (beyond
  training-intensity planning) or pregnancy/postpartum recovery in depth.
- Nutrition coverage in this library leans heavily on one dietary philosophy (whole-food,
  primal/ancestral eating); this library does not yet contain a source arguing a substantially
  different nutritional framework for direct comparison.
- Supplementation coverage is currently limited to creatine and a brief mention of a magnesium/
  melatonin/valerian sleep stack; most other popular supplements in this space are not yet covered.

This file will grow as more videos are added and as genuine disagreements or unresolved questions
surface across new notes.
