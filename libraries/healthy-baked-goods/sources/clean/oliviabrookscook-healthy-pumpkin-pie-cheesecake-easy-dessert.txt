[¶1] Healthy Pumpkin Pie Cheesecake 🎃 Easy Dessert!
Recipe👇
Ingredients:
8 oz (225 g) cream cheese, softened
1/2 cup pumpkin puree
1 large egg
1/4 cup maple syrup
1/2 cup Greek yogurt
1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
1 tsp vanilla extract

[¶2] Instructions:
Preheat oven to 160°C (325°F). Line a 7-inch springform pan with parchment.
Beat cream cheese until smooth. Add pumpkin puree, Greek yogurt, maple syrup, egg, pumpkin pie spice, and vanilla. Mix just until smooth.
Pour into the pan and bake for 40–45 minutes, until the edges are set and the center has a slight jiggle. Cool completely, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours before slicing.
