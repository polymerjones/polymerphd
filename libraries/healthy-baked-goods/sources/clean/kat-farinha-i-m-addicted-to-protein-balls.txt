[¶1] I’m addicted to protein balls :)

[¶2] 16 balls — 4 servings of 4 balls

[¶3] 1 cup canned pumpkin purée, blotted dry
⅓ cup almond butter
4 oz beef isolate protein powder
½ cup blanched almond flour
¼ cup monk fruit sweetener
2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
1 tsp vanilla · ½ tsp sea salt
White chocolate chips
½ TB Coconut oil

[¶4] Method
Blot the pumpkin between paper towels until it’s visibly thicker. Non-negotiable. Wet pumpkin will not roll.
Stir the pumpkin with the almond butter, vanilla, and monk fruit until smooth.
Add the protein powder, almond flour, coconut flour, spice, and salt. Mix into a thick dough.
Roll into 16 balls.
Rest 10 minutes in the fridge. 
Melt coconut oil with white chocolate chips.
Take the dough out of the fridge and cover with white chocolate, return to the fridge for at least 10 mins.

[¶5] Per ball: 101 cal · 8g protein · 5.5g fat · 5.6g carbs · 2.5g fiber
