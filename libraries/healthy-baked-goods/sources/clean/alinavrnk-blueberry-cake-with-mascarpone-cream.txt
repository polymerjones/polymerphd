[¶1] 💜 Blueberry Cake with Mascarpone Cream 🫐

[¶2] Super berry-packed, moist, and topped with a silky mascarpone cream. The perfect little blueberry treat! 💜

[¶3] Ingredients

[¶4] For the cake

[¶5] * 3 eggs, room temperature
* 140 g (⅔ cup) neutral vegetable oil
* 130 g (⅔ cup) granulated sugar
* 230 g (1 cup) Greek yogurt, room temperature
* 2 tsp baking powder
* 250 g (2 cups) all-purpose flour
* 150 g (1¼ cups) blueberries
* Pinch of salt

[¶6] For the blueberry jam

[¶7] * 50 g (⅓ cup) blueberries
* 20 g (1½ tbsp) granulated sugar
* 1 tsp cornstarch

[¶8] For the mascarpone cream

[¶9] * 250 g (1 cup) mascarpone
* 130 g (½ cup + 1 tbsp) heavy cream, chilled
* 30 g (2½ tbsp) powdered sugar, optional
* 1–2 tbsp blueberry jam

[¶10] Method

[¶11] Batter 🫐
1. Preheat the oven to 175°C (350°F). Line a loaf pan with parchment paper.
2. In one bowl, whisk together the eggs, oil, sugar, and Greek yogurt.
3. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking powder, and salt.
4. Add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and gently mix with a spatula until just combined.
5. Toss the blueberries with 1 tsp of flour, then gently fold them into the batter.
6. Pour the batter into the prepared pan and bake for 50–55 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
7. Transfer the cake to a wire rack and let it cool completely.

[¶12] 🫐 jam
1. Place the blueberries and sugar in a small saucepan. Cook over medium heat until the berries start bubbling and breaking down.
2. Blend until smooth.
3. Mix the cornstarch with a small amount of water to make a slurry. Add it to the blueberry mixture and cook for another 2–3 minutes, stirring constantly. Let the jam cool completely.

[¶13] Mascarpone cream
1. Whip the chilled heavy cream to soft peaks.
2. Add the mascarpone, powdered sugar, and 1–2 tbsp of blueberry jam. Whip until smooth and creamy.

[¶14] Once the cake has completely cooled, spread the mascarpone cream over the top.
Drizzle with the remaining blueberry jam and finish with fresh blueberries. 🫐

[¶15] Slice, serve, and enjoy this incredibly moist, berry-filled cake! 💙
