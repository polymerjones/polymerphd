[¶1] Pumpkin Coffee Cake 🎃☕️🍁

[¶2] Soft, buttery pumpkin cake topped with a pumpkin streusel, and finished with the dreamiest maple glaze. 🤎

[¶3] For the Pumpkin Cake:

[¶4] * ¾ cup unsalted butter, softened
* 1 cup light brown sugar, packed
* 2 egg yolks, room temperature
* 2 tsp vanilla extract
* ½ cup pumpkin purée
* 1¾ cups all-purpose flour
* 1 tbsp pumpkin pie spice
* 2 tsp cornstarch
* ½ tsp baking soda
* ½ tsp baking powder
* ½ tsp salt

[¶5] Pumpkin Streusel:

[¶6] * 1 1/4 cup flour 
* 1/2 cup dark brown sugar, packed
* 1/4 cup granulated sugar
* 2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
* 1/2 cup melted butter
* Pinch of kosher salt
* Splash of vanilla extract

[¶7] Cinnamon Sugar Topping:

[¶8] * ¼ cup granulated sugar
* ½ tsp pumpkin pie spice

[¶9] Maple Glaze:

[¶10] * 1 cup powdered sugar
* 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
* 1 tbsp whole milk
* Pinch of kosher salt
* ½ tsp vanilla extract

[¶11] Instructions:

[¶12] Preheat oven to 350°F and grease or line your baking pan.

[¶13] Cream together the softened butter and brown sugar until light and fluffy. Mix in the egg yolks and vanilla, then add the pumpkin purée.

[¶14] In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, pumpkin pie spice, cornstarch, baking soda, baking powder and salt. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until just combined.

[¶15] For the streusel, combine the flour, brown sugar, granulated sugar, pumpkin pie spice and salt. Stir in the melted butter and a splash of vanilla until crumbly.

[¶16] Spread the pumpkin cake batter evenly into your prepared pan, then generously sprinkle the streusel over the top.

[¶17] Bake at 350°F until the center is set and a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs. About 20-25 min. Allow the cake to cool completely.

[¶18] Once cooled, sprinkle the pumpkin pie spices & sugar over the top of the cake.

[¶19] Whisk together the powdered sugar, maple syrup, milk, vanilla and a pinch of salt until smooth. Finish by drizzling the maple glaze over the cinnamon sugar topping. 🍁✨

[¶20] The perfect cozy fall coffee cake. Save this one for pumpkin season! 🎃🤎
