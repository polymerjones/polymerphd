[¶1] 🍋🫐 Lemon Blueberry Cake with Cream Cheese Frosting & Blueberry Drizzle

[¶2] Lemon Blueberry Cake

[¶3] Ingredients

[¶4] * 200g unsalted butter, softened
* 180g caster sugar
* 3 large eggs
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* Zest of 1 lemon
* 1–2 tbsp fresh lemon juice
* 60g (¼ cup) buttermilk
* 250g all-purpose flour
* 1 tsp baking powder
* ½ tsp baking soda
* 170g fresh blueberries
* 1 tbsp flour (for coating the blueberries)

[¶5] Homemade Buttermilk

[¶6] * 60ml milk
* 1 tsp lemon juice

[¶7] Mix together and let stand for 5–10 minutes.

[¶8] Method

[¶9] 1. Preheat oven to 170°C. Grease and line an 8×8-inch baking pan.
2. Toss the blueberries with 1 tbsp flour.
3. Beat the butter and sugar until light and fluffy.
4. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition.
5. Mix in the vanilla, lemon zest, and lemon juice.
6. Whisk together the flour, baking powder, and baking soda.
7. Add the dry ingredients alternately with the buttermilk. Mix just until combined.
8. Fold in the blueberries.
9. Pour into the prepared pan and bake for 40–50 minutes, or until a skewer comes out clean.
10. Cool completely before frosting.

[¶10] ⸻

[¶11] Cream Cheese Frosting

[¶12] Ingredients

[¶13] * 125g unsalted butter, softened
* 125g cream cheese, softened
* 250g icing sugar, sifted
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* 1 tsp lemon juice (optional)

[¶14] Method

[¶15] 1. Beat the butter until creamy.
2. Add the cream cheese and beat until smooth.
3. Gradually beat in the icing sugar.
4. Mix in the vanilla and lemon juice until light and fluffy.

[¶16] ⸻

[¶17] Blueberry Drizzle

[¶18] Ingredients

[¶19] * 85g blueberries
* 25g sugar
* ½ tbsp lemon juice
* 1 tbsp water
* ½ tsp cornstarch
* ½ tbsp water (to make a slurry)

[¶20] Method

[¶21] 1. Combine the blueberries, sugar, lemon juice, and 1 tbsp water in a saucepan.
2. Cook over medium heat for 5–7 minutes until the blueberries soften.
3. Mix the cornstarch with ½ tbsp water and stir it into the sauce.
4. Cook for 1–2 minutes until thickened.
5. Cool completely before using.

[¶22] ⸻

[¶23] To Assemble

[¶24] * Spread the cream cheese frosting over the cooled cake.
* Drizzle the cooled blueberry sauce over the frosting.
* Swirl gently with a spoon or knife.
* Garnish with fresh blueberries and lemon zest if desired.
