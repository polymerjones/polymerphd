[¶1] Full Recipe:

[¶2] 2 cups of flour 
1/2 teaspoon baking powder 
1 1/4 cup of sweet potato puree 
4 tablespoons of melted butter 
1/3-1/2 cup grated cheese

[¶3] Bake at 350 degrees Fahrenheit for 15-20 minutes or until golden brown.

[¶4] You can add any spices you might want! A dash of cinnamon would be so lovely for fall!

[¶5] I toss mine in melted butter + garlic + parsley flakes for added flavor but not necessary! You can also just toss in melted butter + salt for kids who might not want the green bits 🌿
