[¶1] 🍓 Strawberry Cheesecake Stuffed Cookies
Soft, buttery strawberry cookies made with real freeze-dried strawberries and swirled with homemade fresh strawberry jam, then filled with a sweet and creamy cream cheese center. Every bite is bursting with strawberry flavor.🍓

[¶2] Ingredients
🍪 Cookie Dough
½ cup unsalted butter, softened
⅔ cup granulated sugar
1 teaspoon vanilla extract
1 cold egg
1½ cups all-purpose flour
½ teaspoon cornstarch
½ teaspoon baking soda
½ teaspoon baking powder
¼ teaspoon salt
½ cup freeze-dried strawberries

[¶3] 🍓 Strawberry Jam:
12 oz fresh strawberries, chopped 
¼ cup granulated sugar
✨Note: If strawberries aren’t sweet you may add more sugar✨
Cream Cheese Filling:
4 oz cream cheese, softened
2 tablespoons granulated sugar
1/2 tsp vanilla extract

[¶4] Instructions
Cream Cheese Filling:
Add the softened cream cheese and sugar to a small bowl.
Mix until smooth and well combined.
Line a tray with parchment paper.
Scoop small spoonfuls of the cream cheese mixture onto the tray, leaving space between each portion.
Place the tray in the freezer for at least 1 hour, or until the cream cheese portions are firm and frozen.

[¶5] 🍓For the Strawberry Jam:
Add the chopped strawberries and sugar to a small saucepan.
Cook over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the strawberries release their juices and begin to break down.
Continue cooking until the mixture thickens into a jam, about 10–15 minutes.
Remove from the heat and let it cool completely.
