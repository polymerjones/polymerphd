[¶1] Apple Cheesecake 🍏✨

[¶2] Light, fluffy, naturally sweet, and packed with protein, this simple apple cheesecake is the perfect healthier dessert. Made with Greek yogurt and apple purée, it has a soft, creamy texture without added sugar.

[¶3] Nutrition (per serving, makes 4): 125 kcal | Protein: 9g | Carbs: 17g | Fat: 3g

[¶4] 🛒 Ingredients

[¶5] * 1 cup (250 g) unsweetened apple purée or applesauce
* 1 cup (250 g) 2% Greek yogurt
* 2 large eggs
* ¼ cup (30 g) cornstarch
* Granulated sweetener, to taste
* 1 tsp vanilla extract
* ½ tsp cinnamon
* Pinch of salt

[¶6] 🍴 Instructions

[¶7] 1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (180°C). Line a small baking dish or cake pan with parchment paper.
2. Add the apple purée, Greek yogurt, eggs, cornstarch, vanilla, cinnamon, salt, and sweetener to a bowl.
3. Whisk until completely smooth and creamy.
4. Pour the mixture into the prepared pan and smooth the top.
5. Bake for 45–50 minutes, until the center is set but still slightly soft.
6. Let the cheesecake cool completely, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight for the best texture.
7. Slice and serve chilled. Add fresh apple slices or a sprinkle of cinnamon if desired.

[¶8] 🍏 A simple, protein-rich dessert that feels indulgent without being overloaded with sugar.
