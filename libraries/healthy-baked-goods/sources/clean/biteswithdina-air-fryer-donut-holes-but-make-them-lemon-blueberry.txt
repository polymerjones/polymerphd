[¶1] Air fryer donut holes, but make them lemon blueberry 🍋🫐
Soft on the inside, bursting with blueberries, and dipped in a sweet vanilla glaze. These are made with cottage cheese and are such an easy little treat when you’re craving something sweet!

[¶2] Ingredients:
¾ cup Good Culture 2% cottage cheese, blended
1 cup all-purpose flour
1 egg
1 tbsp sugar-free maple syrup
Zest of 1 lemon
2 tsp baking powder
¼ tsp baking soda
¼ tsp salt
⅓ cup frozen wild blueberries

[¶3] Vanilla glaze:
½ cup powdered sugar
¼ tsp vanilla extract
1–2 tbsp milk

[¶4] Directions:

[¶5] 1. Whisk together the blended cottage cheese, egg, sugar-free maple syrup and lemon zest.
2. Add the flour, baking powder, baking soda and salt. Mix until just combined.
3. Gently fold in the frozen blueberries.
4. Scoop and roll into about 12 donut holes. If the dough is too sticky, add flour 1 tbsp at a time until workable.
5. Preheat the air fryer to 350°F and lightly spray the basket.
6. Air fry for 6–8 minutes, until lightly golden and cooked through.
7. Let cool, whisk together the glaze ingredients, then dip and enjoy! 🍋

[¶6] Makes about 12

[¶7] Approx. 77 calories | 3g protein | 14g carbs | 1g fat each

[¶8] No air fryer? Bake at 350°F for 12–15 minutes!
