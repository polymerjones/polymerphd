[¶1] It’s officially that time 🎃 My Pumpkin Pie Truffles are BACK!

[¶2] I’ve been making these for years and they continue to be one of the most-loved fall recipes on my website. They’re creamy, perfectly spiced and genuinely taste like pumpkin pie in one little bite.

[¶3] They’re also gluten-free, made with just a handful of simple ingredients and require zero baking, which makes them perfect for those September days when you’re craving all the fall flavors but it’s still way too warm outside.

[¶4] Comment TRUFFLES and I’ll send you the recipe! 🤎

[¶5] https://lovelydelites.com/pumpkin-pie-truffles/

[¶6] 1/2 cup almond butter (may sub for cashew butter or oat butter for nut free option)
1/3 cup pumpkin puree (not pumpkin pie filling)
3 Tbsp maple syrup
1/3 cup coconut flour
1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
1 tsp cinnamon
1 cup white or dark chocolate
1-2 tsp coconut oil

[¶7] Mix almond butter, pumpkin puree and maple syrup until smooth. Stir in coconut flour, pumpkin pie spice and cinnamon, then scoop and roll into balls. Dip each truffle in melted white chocolate with a little coconut oil, then freeze for about 15 minutes until set. Enjoy!
