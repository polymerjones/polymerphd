[¶1] September is almost here, and with it come the brrr months. 🍂 There’s something about this time of year that makes a cozy little treat feel especially fitting.

[¶2] Healthy Pumpkin Spice Cheesecake Bites!

[¶3] They taste like a little indulgence, but you’re also getting protein and calcium from the Greek yogurt, along with fiber, potassium and vitamin A from the pumpkin.

[¶4] Greek yogurt also contains casein, a slower-digesting protein, making these a nice little evening treat, especially after an active day or workout when your muscles are recovering.

[¶5] They’re creamy, have that graham cracker crunch, and taste like cheesecake without feeling quite as heavy. And this recipe is easy to make your own too. You could try chocolate, peanut butter, berries or lemon depending on what you’re craving.

[¶6] Here’s the recipe:

[¶7] Ingredients
• 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
• ½ cup pumpkin purée
• 2 tbsp maple syrup or honey
• 1 tsp vanilla extract
• 1 tsp pumpkin pie spice
• ⅓ cup brown sugar
• 1 cup graham cracker crumbs
• 2 tbsp melted coconut oil or butter
• Optional: chopped pecans or walnuts

[¶8] How to Make

[¶9] 1. Mix the Greek yogurt, pumpkin purée, maple syrup, vanilla, brown sugar and pumpkin pie spice until smooth.
2. In a separate bowl, mix the graham cracker crumbs with the melted coconut oil or butter.
3. Spoon the crumb mixture into silicone muffin molds and press down.
4. Add the pumpkin cheesecake mixture on top.
5. Freeze for 2 to 3 hours, or until firm.
6. Let sit for a few minutes before enjoying.

[¶10] Optional: Drizzle with a little maple syrup and sprinkle with crushed pecans or walnuts for an extra little touch of fall.
