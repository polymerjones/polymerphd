[¶1] RECIPE BELOW👇

[¶2] These sweet potato donuts are rich, chocolatey, and taste like real donuts. They’re super easy to make and you only need 7 ingredients to make them!

[¶3] Save this the next time you’re craving donuts and share with a friend who needs to try this! Follow for more healthy dessert recipes that taste just like your favorite treats!

[¶4] Remix credit @chefbosquet (recipe modified from original chefbosquet version)

[¶5] Ingredients

[¶6] ¾ cup (180g) roasted sweet potato purée, cooled
½ medium very ripe banana (about 50–60g)
1 large egg
3 tbsp maple syrup
2 tbsp milk of choice
2 tbsp melted coconut oil or avocado oil
1 tsp vanilla extract
¾ cup (75g) oat flour
¼ cup (25g) unsweetened cocoa powder
1½ tsp baking powder
¼ tsp baking soda
¼ tsp salt

[¶7] Chocolate coating

[¶8] 70g dark chocolate
1 tsp coconut oil

[¶9] Instructions

[¶10] Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C) and generously grease a donut pan.
Add the sweet potato, banana, egg, maple syrup, milk, oil, and vanilla to a blender. Blend until completely smooth.
Add the oat flour, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Pulse just until combined. Avoid continuing to blend once the flour is incorporated.
Pipe or spoon the batter into 8 donut cavities, filling each about ¾ full.
Bake for 12–15 minutes, until the tops spring back lightly when touched and a toothpick comes out with a few moist crumbs.
Cool in the pan for 5–10 minutes before carefully removing. Let cool completely before dipping.
Melt the chocolate with the coconut oil until smooth. Dip the tops of the cooled donuts into the chocolate and allow the coating to set.

[¶11] **Estimated nutrition per donut:**
~175 calories | 4g protein | 24g carbs | 8g fat | 4g fiber | 9g sugar*

[¶12] *Estimated using the full 100g dark chocolate coating and assuming 8 donuts. Nutrition will vary depending on the chocolate used and how much coating ends up on each donut.
