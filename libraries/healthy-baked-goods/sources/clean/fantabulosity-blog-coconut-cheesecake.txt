[¶1] Coconut Cheesecake 🥥🍰✨

[¶2] ▢ 4 Tablespoons salted butter, melted
▢ 1½ cups crushed graham crackers
▢ 3 (8-ounce) blocks cream cheese, room temperature
▢ 3 large eggs
▢ ½ cup coconut milk
▢ 1 cup granulated sugar
▢ 1 Tablespoon cornstarch
▢ Cool Whip, optional for topping
▢ Shredded coconut, optional for topping

[¶3] This Coconut Cheesecake is rich, creamy, and full of coconut flavor without being overly complicated.

[¶4] The coconut milk gives the cheesecake a subtle tropical flavor, while the graham cracker crust keeps it classic. Top it with whipped topping and shredded coconut for an easy finish that makes it look extra special.

[¶5] It’s perfect for holidays, spring gatherings, potlucks, or anytime you’re craving a creamy coconut dessert.
