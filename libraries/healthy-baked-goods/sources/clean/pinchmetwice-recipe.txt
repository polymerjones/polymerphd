[¶1] RECIPE 👇

[¶2] Ingredients:
2 (11 oz) boxes of Nilla Wafers
1/2 cup of unsalted butter, melted
1/4 cup of brown sugar

[¶3] 2 (8 oz packs) of cream cheese, softened
3/4 cup of sugar
2 cups (1 pint) of heavy whipping cream, cold
1 tsp of vanilla extract

[¶4] 1 (3.4 oz) box of instant vanilla pudding mix
1 cup of milk
4 oz of Cool Whip
3-4 bananas

[¶5] Crust:
Crush 1 (11oz) box of Nilla wafers 
In a bowl stir together crushed wafers, brown sugar, and melted butter
Spray a 9 inch springform pan with cooking spray
Press mixture into the bottom and about half inch up the sides of a springform pan
Place in refrigerator until firm

[¶6] Cheesecake Mixture:
In a bowl beat softened cream cheese until fluffy
Add in sugar, then beat until smooth
Add in cold heavy whipping cream and vanilla extract
Beat mixture for 3-4 minutes until thick (see video on my YouTube channel)
Place in refrigerator

[¶7] Banana Pudding Mixture:
Mix instant vanilla pudding and milk until it is creamy and has no lumps
Stir in Cool Whip

[¶8] Add the banana pudding to the cheesecake mixture and stir until combined
Pour half the banana pudding cheesecake mixture into your prepared crust
Add a layer of vanilla wafers and sliced bananas 
Pour remaining banana pudding cheesecake mixture on top and smooth with a spatula

[¶9] ‼️REFRIGERATE OVERNIGHT‼️

[¶10] Decorate the top of cheesecake with vanilla wafer crumbs, cool whip, vanilla wafers and banana slices

[¶11] Remove cheesecake from springform pan before slicing. Serve cold.

[¶12] Cheesecake can be stored in refrigerator for 5 days. To freeze, wrap leftover cheesecake in cling wrap and foil and store in freezer for up to 3 months.
