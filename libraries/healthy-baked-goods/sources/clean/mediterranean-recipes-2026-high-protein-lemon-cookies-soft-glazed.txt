[¶1] High-Protein Lemon Cookies (Soft & Glazed)
Ingredients (Makes ~18 cookies)

[¶2] Cookie Dough

[¶3] 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese (or thick Greek yogurt)
1 large egg
½ cup vanilla protein powder (whey or plant-based; ~20–25 g protein per scoop)
¾ cup oat flour (or finely blended oats)
¼ cup granulated sweetener (sugar, coconut sugar, or erythritol)
2 tbsp lemon juice (fresh)
1 tbsp lemon zest
1 tsp baking powder
½ tsp vanilla extract
Pinch of salt

[¶4] Lemon Glaze (Optional but recommended)

[¶5] ½ cup powdered sweetener (powdered sugar or powdered erythritol)
1–2 tbsp lemon juice
Extra lemon zest for topping
👩‍🍳 Instructions
Preheat oven to 175°C / 350°F. Line a baking tray with parchment.
Blend cottage cheese until smooth (important for soft texture).
Mix wet ingredients: blended cottage cheese, egg, lemon juice, zest, vanilla.
Add dry ingredients: protein powder, oat flour, sweetener, baking powder, salt. Mix to a thick, scoopable dough.
Scoop cookies (about 2 tbsp each) onto tray. Gently flatten slightly.
Bake 10–13 minutes, until set and lightly golden underneath.
Cool completely.
Glaze: whisk glaze ingredients and drizzle over cooled cookies. Let set 5–10 minutes.
🧮 Nutrition (Approx. per cookie)
Calories: ~95–110
Protein: ~6–8 g
Carbs: ~10 g
Fat: ~2 g
(Depends on protein powder & sweetener used.)
