[¶1] 🤎These put the “oh!” in Pumpkin Oatmeal Cookies. 
RECIPE:
1 ¾ cups all-purpose flour 225 g
½ teaspoon baking soda
½ teaspoon baking powder
½ teaspoon salt
1 ½ teaspoons ground cinnamon
1 ½ teaspoons pumpkin pie spice
1 cup unsalted butter, softened 226 g
¾ cup packed light brown sugar 165 g
¾ cup 180 g granulated sugar 180 g
1 large egg yolk room temperature
¾ cup pumpkin puree blotted
1 ¾ teaspoons vanilla extract
2 ½ cups 245 g quick oats
INSTRUCTIONS:
Preheat the oven to 350°F. Line two baking sheets with parchment paper.

[¶2] Place the pumpkin puree between several layers of paper towels and gently press to remove excess moisture

[¶3] In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, cinnamon, and pumpkin pie spice.

[¶4] In the bowl of a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, beat the softened butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar on medium speed for 2 to 3 minutes, or until creamy.

[¶5] Add the egg yolk and vanilla extract and mix until combined.

[¶6] Add the blotted pumpkin puree and mix just until evenly incorporated.

[¶7] Add the flour mixture and mix on low until most of the flour has disappeared.

[¶8] Add the quick oats and mix just until evenly distributed.
Use a jumbo cookie scoop to portion the dough onto the prepared baking sheets, leaving about 2 inches between each cookie. Press the tops down very slightly only if needed.

[¶9] Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, or until the edges are set and the centers still look soft.

[¶10] While the cookies are still warm, use a round cookie cutter slightly larger than the cookies to gently shape them into even circles.

[¶11] Cool on the baking sheet for about 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack to cool completely.
