# Strawberry Cheesecake Cookies

## Central claim

**A sugar cookie stuffed with a frozen cream cheese disc and folded (not mixed) with a
heavily reduced homemade strawberry jam, so pockets of jam survive rather than dissolving
into the dough.** `[¶2]` "Soft & chewy sugar cookies, with lots of jammy strawberries
throughout, stuffed with a creamy vanilla cheesecake filling." The Instagram caption points
to `[¶3]` "inbloombakery.com" for the full recipe, fetched here as the second source.
`` `strawberry-cheesecake-cookies`[¶1] `` "By reducing the jam a lot, we're able to keep the
cookies extra chewy!"

## Ingredients

`` `strawberry-cheesecake-cookies`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card. Cheesecake filling:
cream cheese (6 oz/170g, cold), sugar (3 tbsp/38g), vanilla (½ tsp). Strawberry jam: fresh
strawberries, hulled and diced (12 oz/340g), sugar (¼ cup/50g). Cookies: all-purpose flour
(2¾ cups/344g, spooned and leveled), baking powder (½ tsp), baking soda (½ tsp), salt (½
tsp), granulated sugar (1 cup/200g, plus ¼ cup for rolling), unsalted butter (1 cup/227g,
very softened), egg (1, room temp), vanilla (2 tsp).

## Method

`` `strawberry-cheesecake-cookies`[¶1] `` Beat the cream cheese, sugar, and vanilla fluffy;
portion into 20 discs and freeze solid. Cook the strawberries and sugar ~45 minutes,
smashing halfway, until reduced to a thick "heaping ⅓ cup" jam; chill. Whisk the dry
ingredients. Cream the butter and sugar, beat in the egg and vanilla "until pale in color
and very fluffy, not just until it's combined," then mix in the dry ingredients. Layer the
dough and jam in quarters in the bowl and fold with a spatula just enough to leave "little
pockets of jam" rather than mixing it in fully. Scoop 20 portions, flatten slightly,
enclose a frozen cheesecake disc in each, roll in sugar, and bake 6 at a time at 350°F for
11–12 minutes — reshaping warm with a round cutter. Cool 10 minutes on the pan before
transferring to a rack.

## Source-stated confidence

The blog is explicit that store-bought jam is a tested-and-rejected substitution, not just
an untested guess: "Store bought jam is not as thick and could lead to the cookies
spreading too much, as well as not having as strong of a strawberry flavor" — a specific,
stated failure mode rather than a general preference.
