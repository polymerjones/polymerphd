# No-Bake Banana Pudding Cheesecake

## Central claim

**A no-bake springform cheesecake that layers a whipped cream-cheese filling with instant
vanilla pudding, bananas, and Nilla wafers — effectively banana pudding rebuilt as a sliced
cheesecake.** `[¶2]` "2 (11 oz) boxes of Nilla Wafers / 1/2 cup of unsalted butter, melted /
1/4 cup of brown sugar... [¶3] 2 (8 oz packs) of cream cheese, softened / 3/4 cup of sugar /
2 cups (1 pint) of heavy whipping cream, cold / 1 tsp of vanilla extract... [¶4] 1 (3.4 oz)
box of instant vanilla pudding mix / 1 cup of milk / 4 oz of Cool Whip / 3-4 bananas."

## Ingredients

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Crust | Nilla wafers (1 of the 2 boxes, crushed), unsalted butter (½ cup, melted), brown sugar (¼ cup) |
| Cheesecake layer | Cream cheese (2×8 oz packs, softened), sugar (¾ cup), heavy whipping cream (2 cups, cold), vanilla extract (1 tsp) |
| Banana pudding layer | Instant vanilla pudding mix (1×3.4 oz box), milk (1 cup), Cool Whip (4 oz), bananas (3–4) |

## Method

`[¶5]` Crush the wafers, stir in brown sugar and melted butter, press into a 9" springform
pan, and refrigerate until firm. `[¶6]` Beat the cream cheese until fluffy, add sugar, then
cold heavy cream and vanilla, and beat 3–4 minutes until thick — "see video on my YouTube
channel." `[¶7]` Mix the instant pudding and milk until smooth, stir in Cool Whip. `[¶8]`
Combine the banana pudding mixture into the cheesecake mixture, pour half into the crust,
layer with wafers and sliced bananas, then top with the remaining mixture. `[¶9]`
"REFRIGERATE OVERNIGHT." `[¶10]` Decorate with wafer crumbs, Cool Whip, whole wafers, and
banana slices before unmolding and slicing cold.
