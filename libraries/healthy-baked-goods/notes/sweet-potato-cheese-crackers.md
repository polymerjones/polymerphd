# Sweet Potato Cheese Crackers

## Central claim

**A 5-ingredient dough — flour, baking powder, sweet potato puree, butter, and grated
cheese — cut and baked into small crackers, framed by the creator as a toddler snack
rather than a general-audience recipe.** `[¶2]` "2 cups of flour / 1/2 teaspoon baking
powder / 1 1/4 cup of sweet potato puree / 4 tablespoons of melted butter / 1/3-1/2 cup
grated cheese." `` `sweet-potato-cheese-crackers-video`[0:00] `` — the video's own on-screen
caption calls it "The toddler cracker you'll want to make weekly."

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Flour | 2 cups |
| Baking powder | 1/2 teaspoon |
| Sweet potato puree | 1 1/4 cup |
| Butter, melted | 4 tablespoons |
| Grated cheese | 1/3–1/2 cup |

`[¶2]` Five ingredients as stated; the creator doesn't name a cheese variety or specify

## Method

`[¶3]` "Bake at 350 degrees Fahrenheit for 15-20 minutes or until golden brown." No mixing
order, dough consistency, or cutting instructions are stated in the caption.
`` `sweet-potato-cheese-crackers-video`[0:03] `` — the video itself shows the finished
crackers cut into small, fluted-edge (cracker-cutter or ravioli-cutter) shapes, tossed
together in a small tin rather than the mixing/cutting process itself.

## Flavor variations

`[¶4]` The creator offers an optional add-in: "You can add any spices you might want! A
dash of cinnamon would be so lovely for fall!" — stated as a suggestion, not something
she tried herself.

`[¶5]` Her own finishing toss, stated as her preference rather than a required step: "I
toss mine in melted butter + garlic + parsley flakes for added flavor but not necessary!
You can also just toss in melted butter + salt for kids who might not want the green
bits." The garlic/parsley toss and the plain butter-and-salt toss are presented as
equally valid, audience-dependent options — not one graded above the other.
