# Coconut Cream Brownies

## Central claim

**A fully eggless, three-layer brownie: a chocolate brownie base, a cooked coconut-cream
pudding layer folded with shredded coconut, and a whipped-cream topping.** `[¶1]` "Fudgy
chocolate brownies layered with dreamy coconut cream and topped with a light, fluffy whip
topping all bursting with real coconut flavor!" `[¶2]` "completely eggless and can be made
dairy-free!" The Instagram caption gates the step-by-step version behind a comment, but
gives the blog directly (`[¶4]` "go to my website www.pastrybysaloni.com and search for
coconut cream brownies"), fetched here as a second source.

## Ingredients

`` `coconut-cream-brownies-pastrybysaloni`[¶6] `` — the blog's own recipe card (metric):

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Brownie base | Dark or semi-sweet chocolate (200g), butter (90g), powdered sugar (125g), light brown sugar (110g), milk (120ml), vanilla extract (2 tsp), all-purpose flour (160g), cocoa powder (40g), baking powder (¼ tsp), salt (¼ tsp) |
| Coconut cream layer | Canned coconut milk (270ml + 60ml to loosen), cane/castor sugar (125g), cornstarch (24g), vanilla extract (2 tsp), butter (60g), unsweetened shredded coconut (150g) |
| Whipped topping | Cold whipping cream (180ml), vanilla extract (1 tsp), powdered sugar (40g), shredded coconut (30g, to sprinkle) |

## Method

`` `coconut-cream-brownies-pastrybysaloni`[¶6] `` Melt the butter and chocolate together,
stir in the sugars, vanilla, and warm milk, then fold in the flour, cocoa powder, baking
powder, and salt. Bake at 340°F (170°C) for 25–30 minutes; cool completely. For the coconut
layer: cook the first measure of coconut milk with sugar and cornstarch until thickened,
off heat stir in vanilla and butter, fold in the shredded coconut, chill, then loosen with
the reserved coconut milk until spreadable. Spread over the cooled brownie base. Whip the
cream, vanilla, and powdered sugar to stiff peaks, spread over the coconut layer, sprinkle
with shredded coconut, and chill at least an hour before slicing.
