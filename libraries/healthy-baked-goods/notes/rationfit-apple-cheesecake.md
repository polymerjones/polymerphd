# Apple Cheesecake (Greek Yogurt)

## Central claim

**A crustless, flourless baked cheesecake built entirely on Greek yogurt and apple purée
instead of cream cheese, thickened with cornstarch rather than a graham crust for
structure.** `[¶2]` "Made with Greek yogurt and apple purée, it has a soft, creamy texture
without added sugar." `[¶3]` "Nutrition (per serving, makes 4): 125 kcal | Protein: 9g |
Carbs: 17g | Fat: 3g."

## Ingredients

`[¶5]` Unsweetened apple purée or applesauce (1 cup/250g), 2% Greek yogurt (1 cup/250g),
eggs (2 large), cornstarch (¼ cup/30g), granulated sweetener (to taste), vanilla extract (1
tsp), cinnamon (½ tsp), salt (pinch).

## Method

`[¶7]` Preheat to 350°F (180°C) and line a small baking dish or cake pan with parchment.
Whisk the apple purée, yogurt, eggs, cornstarch, vanilla, cinnamon, salt, and sweetener
until completely smooth. Pour into the pan and smooth the top. Bake 45–50 minutes until the
center is set but still slightly soft. Cool completely, then refrigerate at least 4 hours
or overnight "for the best texture." Slice and serve chilled, optionally with fresh apple
slices or a sprinkle of cinnamon.

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

Framed explicitly as a lighter, protein-forward alternative to a traditional cheesecake
craving — `[¶2]` "the perfect healthier dessert" — `[¶8]` "A simple, protein-rich dessert
that feels indulgent without being overloaded with sugar." No added sugar is used; sweetener
is to taste.
