# Homemade Oatmeal Cream Pies

## Central claim

**A from-scratch version of the packaged Little Debbie oatmeal cream pie — molasses-spiced
quick-oat cookies sandwiching a whipped-butter marshmallow filling.** `[¶1]` "Soft, chewy
oatmeal cookies and fluffy marshmallow cream make these homemade oatmeal cream pies pure
childhood nostalgia." Unlike most creators in this library, this one gave the full recipe
away for free rather than gating it: `[¶2]` "Comment RECIPE and I'll send you the full
recipe or you can find it at" her own blog, fetched here as a second source.

## Ingredients

`` `oatmeal-cream-pies`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card:

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Oatmeal cookies | All-purpose flour (2 cups/250g), baking powder (1 tsp), baking soda (1 tsp), cinnamon (½ tsp), salt (½ tsp), unsalted butter (1 cup/227g), light brown sugar (¾ cup/165g), granulated sugar (¾ cup/150g), eggs (2 large), molasses (2 tbsp/40g), vanilla extract (2 tsp), quick oats (2 cups/162g) |
| Marshmallow cream filling | Unsalted butter (1 cup/227g), marshmallow fluff (1¼ cups/159g), powdered sugar (1¼ cups/150g), vanilla extract (2 tsp), salt (¼ tsp) |

## Method

`` `oatmeal-cream-pies`[¶1] `` Whisk the dry ingredients (flour, baking powder, baking soda,
cinnamon, salt). Cream the butter and both sugars until light and fluffy; beat in the eggs
one at a time, then the molasses and vanilla. Mix in the flour mixture and oats on low just
until combined. Scoop with a large (3 tbsp) cookie scoop, bake at 350°F (177°C) for 10–12
minutes until edges are golden, immediately reshape any misshapen cookies with a round
cutter, then cool fully. For the filling: cream the butter until pale and fluffy, mix in the
marshmallow fluff, then the powdered sugar, vanilla, and salt; whip on medium 2 minutes
until smooth. Pipe filling onto one cookie and sandwich with a second.
