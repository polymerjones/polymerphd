# Pumpkin Coffee Cake with Maple Glaze

## Central claim

**A pumpkin cake layered with its own pumpkin-spiced streusel baked into the top (not just
sprinkled on after), finished in three passes: streusel baked in, a cinnamon-sugar dusting,
then a maple glaze drizzled last.** `[¶2]` "Soft, buttery pumpkin cake topped with a pumpkin
streusel, and finished with the dreamiest maple glaze."

## Ingredients

`[¶4]` Cake: unsalted butter (¾ cup, softened), light brown sugar (1 cup, packed), egg
yolks (2, room temp), vanilla extract (2 tsp), pumpkin purée (½ cup), all-purpose flour
(1¾ cups), pumpkin pie spice (1 tbsp), cornstarch (2 tsp), baking soda (½ tsp), baking
powder (½ tsp), salt (½ tsp). `[¶6]` Streusel: flour (1¼ cups), dark brown sugar (½ cup,
packed), granulated sugar (¼ cup), pumpkin pie spice (2 tsp), melted butter (½ cup), salt
(pinch), vanilla (splash). `[¶8]` Cinnamon sugar topping: granulated sugar (¼ cup), pumpkin
pie spice (½ tsp). `[¶10]` Maple glaze: powdered sugar (1 cup), maple syrup (2 tbsp), whole
milk (1 tbsp), salt (pinch), vanilla (½ tsp).

## Method

`[¶12]`–`[¶19]` Preheat to 350°F. Cream the butter and brown sugar, mix in the egg yolks and
vanilla, then the pumpkin purée. Separately whisk the flour, pumpkin pie spice, cornstarch,
baking soda, baking powder, and salt; fold into the wet ingredients just until combined.
Combine the streusel ingredients (dry ingredients plus melted butter and vanilla) until
crumbly. Spread the cake batter in the pan and sprinkle the streusel generously over the
top. Bake at 350°F for about 20–25 minutes, until a toothpick has a few moist crumbs. Cool
completely, dust with the cinnamon-sugar blend, then whisk and drizzle the maple glaze over
the top.
