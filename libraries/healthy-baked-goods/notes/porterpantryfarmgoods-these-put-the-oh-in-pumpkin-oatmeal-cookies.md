# Pumpkin Oatmeal Cookies

## Central claim

**A pumpkin oatmeal cookie where the pumpkin purée is deliberately blotted dry before
mixing — the same moisture-control technique seen elsewhere in this library's pumpkin
recipes — and the warm cookies are reshaped with a cutter immediately out of the oven for a
uniform round shape.** `[¶1]` "These put the 'oh!' in Pumpkin Oatmeal Cookies." Recipe
credited by the poster to another creator: `[¶12]` "Recipe credit: @better_baker_club."

## Ingredients

`[¶1]` All-purpose flour (1¾ cups/225g), baking soda (½ tsp), baking powder (½ tsp), salt
(½ tsp), ground cinnamon (1½ tsp), pumpkin pie spice (1½ tsp), unsalted butter (1
cup/226g, softened), light brown sugar (¾ cup/165g, packed), granulated sugar (¾ cup/180g),
egg yolk (1 large, room temp), pumpkin purée, blotted (¾ cup), vanilla extract (1¾ tsp),
quick oats (2½ cups/245g).

## Method

`[¶1]` Preheat to 350°F, line two baking sheets. `[¶2]` Press the pumpkin purée between
paper towels to remove excess moisture. `[¶3]`–`[¶8]` Whisk the dry spice/flour mixture
separately. Cream the butter and both sugars 2–3 minutes until fluffy, mix in the egg yolk
and vanilla, then the blotted pumpkin. Add the flour mixture until mostly combined, then mix
in the oats just until distributed. Scoop with a jumbo scoop, spacing 2" apart, pressing
tops down only if needed. `[¶9]`–`[¶11]` Bake 8–10 minutes until edges are set and centers
still look soft. While still warm, shape into even circles with a round cutter slightly
larger than the cookies. Cool on the sheet 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.
