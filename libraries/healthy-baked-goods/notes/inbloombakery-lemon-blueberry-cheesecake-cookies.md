# Lemon Blueberry Cheesecake Cookies

## Central claim

**The lemon-blueberry variant of this creator's stuffed-cookie technique — lemon zest
rubbed into the sugar before creaming, a heavily reduced blueberry jam folded in for
pockets of flavor, and a frozen cream cheese disc baked inside.** `[¶3]` "They're soft and
chewy lemon sugar cookies, swirled with jammy blueberries and filled with creamy vanilla
cheesecake." No comment-gate is used on this particular post — the caption alone doesn't
name a recipe source, so the blog was located by searching this creator's other identically
structured recipes and is fetched here as the second source.

## Ingredients

`` `lemon-blueberry-cheesecake-cookies`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card. Cheesecake
filling: cream cheese (6 oz/170g, cold), sugar (3 tbsp/38g), vanilla (½ tsp). Blueberry jam:
fresh blueberries (12 oz/340g), sugar (¼ cup/50g). Cookies: all-purpose flour (2¾ cups/344g,
spooned and leveled), baking powder (½ tsp), baking soda (½ tsp), salt (½ tsp), granulated
sugar (1 cup/200g, plus ¼ cup for rolling), lemon zest (2 tbsp/20g), unsalted butter (1
cup/227g, very softened), egg (1, room temp), vanilla (2 tsp).

## Method

`` `lemon-blueberry-cheesecake-cookies`[¶1] `` Beat and freeze the cheesecake discs as in
this creator's other stuffed cookies. Cook the blueberries and sugar ~40 minutes to a thick
jam, reduced to about ½ cup; chill. Rub the lemon zest into the sugar before creaming with
the butter — "to extract the most lemon flavor from the zest" — then beat in the egg and
vanilla, then the dry ingredients. Layer and fold in the jam in quarters as with the
strawberry version, leaving distinct pockets rather than mixing fully. Scoop 20 portions,
enclose a frozen cheesecake disc in each, roll in sugar, and bake 6 at a time at 350°F for
11–12 minutes, reshaping warm.
