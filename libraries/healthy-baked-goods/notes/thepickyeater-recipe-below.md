# Sweet Potato Chocolate Donuts

## Central claim

**A blender-batter chocolate donut built on roasted sweet potato puree and banana rather
than butter or oil as the main wet base, baked (not fried) in a donut pan and dipped in
dark chocolate.** `[¶6]` "¾ cup (180g) roasted sweet potato purée, cooled / ½ medium very
ripe banana... / 1 large egg / 3 tbsp maple syrup / 2 tbsp milk of choice / 2 tbsp melted
coconut oil or avocado oil / 1 tsp vanilla extract / ¾ cup (75g) oat flour / ¼ cup (25g)
unsweetened cocoa powder / 1½ tsp baking powder / ¼ tsp baking soda / ¼ tsp salt."
Remix-credited to `[¶4]` "@chefbosquet (recipe modified from original chefbosquet version)."

**Note on the creator's own "7 ingredients" claim:** `[¶2]` "you only need 7 ingredients to
make them!" — but the itemized batter list runs to 12 items, before the 2-ingredient
chocolate coating is even counted. Preserved here as the creator's own stated framing
rather than corrected, but `ingredient_count` above reflects the actual itemized batter
list, not the marketing claim, since that field is meant to power an accurate filter.

## Ingredients

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Batter | Sweet potato purée (¾ cup/180g), banana (½ medium), egg, maple syrup (3 tbsp), milk (2 tbsp), coconut or avocado oil (2 tbsp), vanilla (1 tsp), oat flour (¾ cup/75g), cocoa powder (¼ cup/25g), baking powder (1½ tsp), baking soda (¼ tsp), salt (¼ tsp) |
| Chocolate coating | Dark chocolate (70g), coconut oil (1 tsp) |

## Method

`[¶10]` Preheat to 350°F (180°C) and grease a donut pan. Blend the sweet potato, banana,
egg, maple syrup, milk, oil, and vanilla until smooth; add the dry ingredients and pulse
just until combined — "Avoid continuing to blend once the flour is incorporated." Pipe or
spoon into 8 donut cavities, about ¾ full. Bake 12–15 minutes, until the tops spring back
and a toothpick has a few moist crumbs. Cool in the pan 5–10 minutes, then cool completely
before dipping the tops in the melted chocolate-and-coconut-oil coating.

## Nutrition, as stated

`[¶11]` "~175 calories | 4g protein | 24g carbs | 8g fat | 4g fiber | 9g sugar" per donut
(makes 8). `[¶12]` The creator's own caveat: this assumes "the full 100g dark chocolate
coating" is used and evenly split across all 8 donuts — actual sugar/fat per donut will
vary with how much coating ends up on each one.
