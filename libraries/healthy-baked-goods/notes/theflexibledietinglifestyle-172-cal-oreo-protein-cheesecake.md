# 172-Calorie Oreo Protein Cheesecake

## Central claim

**A blended, no-flour cheesecake built on Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, and fat-free cream
cheese instead of a full-fat cream-cheese base, baked in a water bath (bain marie) like a
conventional cheesecake, and finished with a whey-protein icing rather than sour cream or
ganache.** `[¶5]` "480g Plain Non Fat Greek Yogurt / 240g 2% Cottage Cheese / 1 Block
(226g) Fat Free Cream Cheese... / 2 Large Eggs / 100g Egg Whites / 5g Vanilla Extract /
100g Vanilla Whey/Casein Blend Protein Powder / 28g Sugar Free/Fat Free Cheesecake Pudding
Mix / 20g Zero Cal Sweetener / 8g OREO Thins."

## Ingredients

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Cheesecake | Nonfat Greek yogurt (480g), 2% cottage cheese (240g), fat-free cream cheese (226g), eggs (2 large), egg whites (100g), vanilla extract (5g), vanilla whey/casein protein powder (100g), sugar-free cheesecake pudding mix (28g), zero-cal sweetener (20g), crushed Oreo Thins (8g) |
| Oreo protein icing | Vanilla whey/casein protein powder (20g), powdered sugar (20g), black cocoa powder (10g) |

## Method

`[¶7]` Blend all cheesecake ingredients on low until smooth. Wrap a 9" springform pan's
outside in foil for the water bath, spray the inside, add the batter, and mix in the
crushed Oreo Thins. Set the springform in a deep sheet pan and pour in warm water 1–1.5"
high. Bake at 300°F for 55 minutes, then turn the oven off, crack the door, and continue
cooking 35 minutes more. Cool at room temperature an hour, then refrigerate overnight to
set fully. The next day, whisk the icing ingredients with a little cold water to icing
consistency — "Be careful not to add too much water!" — and top with icing, whipped cream,
and crushed Oreos to serve.

## Nutrition, as stated

`[¶3]` Per slice (makes 8), without toppings: "172 Cals, 15g Carbs, 3.5g Fat, 22g Protein."
`[¶4]` With toppings: "206 Cals, 20g Carbs, 4g Fat, 24g Protein."
