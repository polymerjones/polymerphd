# Strawberry Banana Bread with Strawberry Cream Cheese Frosting

## Central claim

**A standard banana quick bread studded with diced fresh strawberries — never frozen, by
the creator's explicit instruction — and topped with a cream cheese frosting whose
concentrated strawberry flavor comes from blitzed freeze-dried strawberries rather than
fresh purée.** `[¶1]` "strawberries and cream banana bread 🍓🍌 inspired by wimbledon 🎾🎾."
`` `a-fruity-twist-that-makes-banana-bread-even-better`[¶1] `` "I would only use fresh
berries here since frozen strawberries can add excess moisture." The Instagram caption
gates the recipe (`[¶1]` "comment COZY and i'll DM you the recipe").

## Ingredients

`` `a-fruity-twist-that-makes-banana-bread-even-better`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card:
unsalted butter (½ cup, melted), avocado or olive oil (3 tbsp), mashed banana (1⅓
cups/300g), granulated sugar (¾ cup/150g), vanilla (1 tsp), eggs (2 large), baking powder
(1 tsp), baking soda (½ tsp), kosher salt (½ tsp), all-purpose flour (1½ cups/195g), diced
fresh strawberries (1 cup/140g). Frosting: cream cheese (3 oz, room temp), unsalted butter
(4 tbsp, room temp), powdered sugar (1½ cups), freeze-dried strawberries (⅓ cup/8g), heavy
cream (1 tbsp), vanilla extract (½ tsp), kosher salt (pinch).

## Method

`` `a-fruity-twist-that-makes-banana-bread-even-better`[¶1] `` Whisk the melted butter, oil,
mashed banana, sugar, vanilla, and eggs, then the baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
Fold in the flour just until combined, then fold in the diced strawberries. Bake at 350°F
for 65–75 minutes. Cool at least an hour before frosting — "Even slightly warm banana bread
can cause the cream cheese frosting to melt and slide off." For the frosting: blitz the
freeze-dried strawberries to a powder, beat the cream cheese smooth, beat in the butter,
then the powdered sugar, strawberry powder, salt, vanilla, and heavy cream for a full 2
minutes until fluffy. Swirl over the cooled loaf.

## Source-stated confidence

The blog gives specific, tested reasoning for two ingredient choices that could easily be
substituted carelessly: fresh (not frozen) strawberries, and freeze-dried (not fresh)
strawberries specifically in the frosting "providing concentrated strawberry flavor without
watering it down" — both stated as deliberate fixes for a moisture problem, not arbitrary
preference.
