# Apple Oatmeal Bars with Vegan Caramel Sauce

## Central claim

**An oat-and-almond-flour crumble bar with a cooked cinnamon-apple filling, sandwiched
between two crust layers and topped with a dairy-free caramel made from refrigerated
coconut cream instead of butter and heavy cream.** `[¶3]` "These apple oat bars are so
great to make for breakfast and enjoy throughout the week." The Instagram caption gives an
explicit blog link (`[¶4]` `https://veggieworldrecipes.com/apple-bars/`), fetched here as a
second source; the blog itself states `` `easiest-apple-bars-with-vegan-caramel-sauce`[¶1] ``
"They are vegan and gluten free."

## Ingredients

`` `easiest-apple-bars-with-vegan-caramel-sauce`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card:

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Crust | Old-fashioned oats (2¼ cups/180g), blanched almond flour (1½ cups/180g), cinnamon (1 tsp), baking powder (1½ tsp), maple syrup (¾ cup), coconut oil (⅓ cup), vanilla (1 tsp), salt (dash) |
| Apple filling | Apples, any kind (3 medium/510g), lemon juice (½ lemon), coconut sugar (⅓ cup), vanilla (½ tsp), cinnamon (1 tsp), cornstarch (2 tbsp) |
| Vegan caramel sauce | Coconut cream, refrigerated overnight, solid part only (⅓ cup/100g), coconut sugar (½ cup/100g), cornstarch (1 tsp), vanilla (¼ tsp), salt (¼ tsp) |

## Method

`` `easiest-apple-bars-with-vegan-caramel-sauce`[¶1] `` Mix the crust ingredients, press
half into a lined pan as the base layer, reserving the rest for the top. Cook the sliced
apples with lemon juice, cinnamon, coconut sugar, and vanilla for 3–4 minutes, stir in the
cornstarch, then spread the filling over the base crust and top with the remaining crust.
Bake 30–35 minutes at 400°F/200°C. For the caramel: melt the scooped coconut cream solids
with coconut sugar until bubbling, then whisk in vanilla, salt, and cornstarch off the heat;
chill until it thickens before drizzling over the cooled, sliced bars. Makes 9 bars, per
the blog.

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

Positioned as a make-ahead breakfast/snack bar for the week rather than a special-occasion
dessert. `` `easiest-apple-bars-with-vegan-caramel-sauce`[¶1] `` Both vegan and gluten-free
by ingredient choice (oats, almond flour, coconut oil/sugar/cream — no dairy, eggs, or
wheat flour anywhere in the recipe).
