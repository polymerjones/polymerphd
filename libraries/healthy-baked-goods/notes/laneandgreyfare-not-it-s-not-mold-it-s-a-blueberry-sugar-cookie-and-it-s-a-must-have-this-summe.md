# Blueberry Cookies

## Central claim

**The plain (no-lemon) version of this creator's eggless, gluten-free blueberry cookie —
frozen blueberries are microwaved into a quick jam and mixed straight into the dough for a
naturally periwinkle color, with no fruit folded in whole and no glaze at all.**
`` `blueberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` "their beautiful periwinkle color is completely
natural and they're gluten free, nut free and easily vegan!!!" The Instagram caption's
own title jokes about the color (`[¶1]` "Not it's not mold, it's a Blueberry Sugar Cookie")
and gates the recipe (`[¶2]` "Comment 'blueberry' and I'll send the recipe to your or
google: lane and grey blueberry cookies").

## Ingredients

`` `blueberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card: frozen
blueberries (1 cup, microwaved into jam), Cup4Cup gluten-free multipurpose flour (2 cups),
cornstarch (1½ tbsp, into the flour, plus a separate 2 heaping tbsp + 3 tbsp water as an
egg substitute), baking powder (1½ tsp), kosher salt (½ tsp), unsalted butter or vegan
baking stick (½ cup), granulated sugar (1¼ cups + 2 tbsp for coating), milk (2 tbsp). No
glaze in this version.

## Method

`` `blueberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` Microwave the whole cup of blueberries into a
thick jam first and let it cool completely — "You do not want to add hot blueberries into
the dough." Cream butter and sugar, beat in the cornstarch slurry, milk, and 3 tbsp of the
cooled jam, fold in the flour mixture. **Do not add vanilla** — the creator states it "turns
the dough a grey color instead of lavender." **Refrigerate at least 2.5 hours** (chilling
overnight shifts the color from lavender to solid blue with flecks, by the creator's own
account). Scoop 12 cookies, roll in sugar, bake 325°F for 15 minutes, reshape immediately.

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

Same allergy-accommodation framing as the creator's other fruit cookies. Positioned as a
color-forward, conversation-starting treat as much as a flavor one — the Instagram title
itself leans into the cookies' unusual periwinkle appearance.

## Source-stated confidence

The color-chemistry claims here are stated as directly observed and repeatable by the
creator across her other blueberry-based recipes (fresh vs. frozen berries, dough-chill
duration, and vanilla all named as specific levers on the final hue) — presented as her own
kitchen experience, not sourced to any food-science reference.

## Conflicts with other sources

`` `lemon-blueberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` is the same creator's lemon-and-blueberry
variant of this exact base cookie — it adds lemon zest/juice and a blueberry glaze that this
plainer version omits entirely, and the two together demonstrate the creator's own claim
that "I have two ways to make them... this way makes them a light purple and the other
keeps them a lovely yellow." `` `lemon-raspberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` and
`` `strawberry-jam-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` share the same underlying base technique.
