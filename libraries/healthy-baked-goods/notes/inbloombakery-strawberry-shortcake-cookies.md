# Strawberry Shortcake Cookies

## Central claim

**A white-chocolate-and-strawberry-jam sugar cookie topped (not stuffed) with a piped
mascarpone whipped cream and a separately oven-baked shortcake crumble, rather than the
frozen-cheesecake-disc filling this creator uses elsewhere.** `[¶2]` "Soft & chewy sugar
cookies, with jammy strawberries and white chocolate throughout, frosted with fresh whipped
cream and sprinkled with shortcake crumble." The Instagram caption points to `[¶4]`
"inbloombakery.com" for the full recipe.

## Ingredients

`` `strawberry-shortcake-cookies`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card. Strawberry jam: fresh
strawberries, diced (14 oz/396g), sugar (¼ cup/50g). Shortcake crumble: flour (½ cup/62g),
sugar (1 tbsp/12g), salt (⅛ tsp), cold cubed butter (3 tbsp/42g). Cookies: all-purpose flour
(2¾ cups/344g), baking powder (½ tsp), baking soda (½ tsp), salt (½ tsp), sugar (1
cup/200g), unsalted butter (1 cup/227g, very softened), egg (1), vanilla (2 tsp), white
chocolate bars, chopped (6 oz/170g). Mascarpone whipped cream: mascarpone (4 oz/113g),
powdered sugar (3 tbsp/25g), heavy cream (1 cup/240ml).

## Method

`` `strawberry-shortcake-cookies`[¶1] `` Cook the strawberries and sugar ~45 minutes to a
thick jam; chill. Cut the cold butter into the crumble's dry ingredients to small crumbs,
spread on a tray, and bake 10–12 minutes until light golden; cool. Cream the cookie butter
and sugar, beat in the egg and vanilla, mix in the dry ingredients, then fold in the chopped
white chocolate. Layer and fold in the jam in quarters, as with this creator's other cookies
in this library, to leave distinct pockets. Scoop 22 portions, space at least 2" apart, and
press lightly flat — this version is not stuffed with a cheesecake disc. Bake 6 at a time at
350°F for 10–11 minutes, reshaping warm. Once fully cool, beat the mascarpone and powdered
sugar, fold in the cream in two additions to soft peaks, then pipe onto each cookie and
finish with crumble and a strawberry slice.

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

Built to evoke a classic strawberry shortcake in cookie form rather than tied to a
particular dietary need. The creator explicitly recommends frosting just before serving,
not before freezing for storage (§ Source-stated confidence).

## Source-stated confidence

The blog gives a specific, tested reason for its whipped-cream mixing order: "Cream has a
funny way of looking like it's not whipped enough, to being too whipped, so it's better to
mix less and have it be softer" since piping adds further air — presented as a corrective
technique note, not a guess. Storage guidance is split accordingly: bake and freeze the
plain cookies, but add the mascarpone cream only right before serving.

## Conflicts with other sources

`strawberry-cheesecake-cookies`, `pumpkin-cheesecake-cookies`, and
`lemon-blueberry-cheesecake-cookies` are this same creator's other jam-folded sugar
cookies, but all three stuff a frozen cream-cheese disc inside the dough — this recipe is
the one exception in the creator's own lineup, topping with whipped cream and crumble
instead of stuffing with cheesecake filling.
