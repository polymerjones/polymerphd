## Ingredients

`[¶2]`–`[¶3]` Cookie dough: unsalted butter (½ cup, softened), granulated sugar (⅔ cup),
vanilla extract (1 tsp), egg (1, cold), all-purpose flour (1½ cups), cornstarch (½ tsp),
baking soda (½ tsp), baking powder (½ tsp), salt (¼ tsp), freeze-dried strawberries (½
cup). Strawberry jam: fresh strawberries, chopped (12 oz), granulated sugar (¼ cup, more if
strawberries aren't sweet). Cream cheese filling: cream cheese (4 oz, softened), granulated
sugar (2 tbsp), vanilla extract (½ tsp).

## Method (as far as the caption goes)

`[¶4]` For the cream cheese filling: mix the softened cream cheese and sugar smooth, scoop
small portions onto a lined tray, and freeze at least 1 hour until firm. `[¶5]` For the
jam: cook the chopped strawberries and sugar over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until
they release their juices and break down, continuing until thickened into a jam (about
10–15 minutes); cool completely. The caption stops here — see the gap noted in § Central
claim.
