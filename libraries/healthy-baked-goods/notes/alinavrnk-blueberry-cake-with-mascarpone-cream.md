# Blueberry Cake with Mascarpone Cream

## Central claim

**A Greek-yogurt loaf cake with blueberries folded into the batter, topped with a
mascarpone-whipped-cream frosting that's itself flavored with the same cooked blueberry jam
used to sweeten it, rather than a separate glaze.** `[¶2]` "Super berry-packed, moist, and
topped with a silky mascarpone cream."

## Ingredients

`[¶5]` Cake: eggs (3, room temperature), neutral vegetable oil (140g/⅔ cup), granulated sugar
(130g/⅔ cup), Greek yogurt (230g/1 cup, room temperature), baking powder (2 tsp), all-purpose
flour (250g/2 cups), blueberries (150g/1¼ cups), salt (pinch). `[¶7]` Blueberry jam:
blueberries (50g/⅓ cup), granulated sugar (20g/1½ tbsp), cornstarch (1 tsp). `[¶9]`
Mascarpone cream: mascarpone (250g/1 cup), heavy cream (130g/½ cup + 1 tbsp, chilled),
powdered sugar (30g/2½ tbsp, optional), blueberry jam (1–2 tbsp, from the batch above).

## Method

`[¶11]` Preheat to 175°C (350°F); line a loaf pan. Whisk eggs, oil, sugar, and Greek yogurt in
one bowl; combine flour, baking powder, and salt in another. Add dry to wet and "gently mix
... until just combined." Toss the blueberries with 1 tsp flour before folding in — the
flour-coating step keeps them from sinking. Bake 50–55 minutes to a clean toothpick, then
cool completely on a rack before frosting. `[¶12]` For the jam: cook blueberries and sugar
until they "start bubbling and breaking down," blend smooth, then thicken with a cornstarch
slurry for 2–3 minutes and cool completely. `[¶13]` For the cream: whip chilled heavy cream to
soft peaks, then add mascarpone, powdered sugar, and 1–2 tbsp of the cooled jam and whip
smooth. `[¶14]` Spread the cream over the cooled cake, drizzle with the remaining jam, and
finish with fresh blueberries.
