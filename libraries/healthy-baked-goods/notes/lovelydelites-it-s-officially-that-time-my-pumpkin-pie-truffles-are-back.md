# Pumpkin Pie Truffles

## Central claim

**A gluten-free, no-bake truffle built from pumpkin purée thickened with coconut flour rather
than blotted dry, then dipped in white or dark chocolate.** `[¶2]` "I've been making these for
years and they continue to be one of the most-loved fall recipes on my website... genuinely taste
like pumpkin pie in one little bite." `[¶3]` "They're also gluten-free, made with just a handful
of simple ingredients and require zero baking."

## Ingredients

`[¶6]`:

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Almond butter (or cashew/oat butter for nut-free) | ½ cup |
| Pumpkin purée (not pie filling) | ⅓ cup |
| Maple syrup | 3 tbsp |
| Coconut flour | ⅓ cup |
| Pumpkin pie spice | 1 tsp |
| Cinnamon | 1 tsp |
| White or dark chocolate | 1 cup |
| Coconut oil | 1–2 tsp |

## Method

`[¶7]` Mix the almond butter, pumpkin purée, and maple syrup until smooth, then stir in the
coconut flour, pumpkin pie spice, and cinnamon. Scoop and roll into balls, dip each in melted
chocolate loosened with the coconut oil, and freeze about 15 minutes until set.

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

Positioned as a fall craving that doesn't require turning on the oven: `[¶3]` "perfect for those
September days when you're craving all the fall flavors but it's still way too warm outside."
Explicitly gluten-free, with a stated nut-free substitution for the almond butter (cashew or oat
butter, `[¶6]`).

## Source-stated confidence

Low-to-moderate: the creator cites her own track record ("I've been making these for years") as
the basis for calling it "one of the most-loved" recipes on her site, but gives no explanation of
why coconut flour (rather than, say, blotting the pumpkin dry) is her chosen way to manage the
purée's moisture.

## Conflicts with other sources

Shares its core format — no-bake, pumpkin-purée-based, rolled and chocolate-dipped — with
`kat-farinha-i-m-addicted-to-protein-balls`, but the two disagree on how to handle the purée's
moisture: this recipe thickens with coconut flour, while the other recipe blots the pumpkin
dry between paper towels and states that step is "non-negotiable... wet pumpkin will not roll."
Neither source addresses the other's technique, so this may simply be two workable approaches
rather than a real disagreement — this recipe never blots the pumpkin, and doesn't report the
rolling failure the other recipe warns about. Also distinct from that recipe in being a
plain dessert truffle rather than a protein-fortified snack (no protein powder, no stated macros).
