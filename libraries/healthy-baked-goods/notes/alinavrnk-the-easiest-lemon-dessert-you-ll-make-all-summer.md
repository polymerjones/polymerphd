# Lemon Brownies

## Central claim

**A simple, single-bowl lemon brownie — no chocolate, leavened lightly with baking powder, and
finished with a two-ingredient lemon glaze — pitched as the easiest lemon dessert for summer.**
`[¶1]` "🍋 The easiest lemon dessert you'll make all summer!"

## Ingredients

`[¶3]`, `[¶5]` (metric, with cup equivalents as given):

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Brownie | Granulated sugar (150g / ¾ cup), zest of 2 lemons, unsalted butter (110g / ½ cup, melted), 2 eggs, lemon juice (2 tbsp), all-purpose flour (125g / 1 cup), salt (½ tsp), baking powder (¼ tsp) |
| Glaze | Powdered sugar (60g / ½ cup), lemon juice (1 tbsp) |

## Method

`[¶7]` Heat the oven to 175°C / 350°F and line the baking pan with parchment. Melt the butter and
let it cool slightly. Rub the lemon zest into the sugar until fragrant, then whisk in the melted
butter for about a minute. Add the eggs and lemon juice, whisking until smooth and glossy. Fold in
the flour, salt, and baking powder just until combined, and spread evenly in the pan. Bake 20–25
minutes, until the edges are set and a toothpick comes out mostly clean. Mix the powdered sugar
with the lemon juice until smooth and pour over the cooled brownies; let the glaze set before
slicing.
