# Healthy Pumpkin Pie Cheesecake

## Central claim

**A crustless pumpkin cheesecake that halves the usual cream cheese with Greek yogurt and
sweetens entirely with maple syrup rather than sugar, baked in a springform with no water
bath.** `[¶1]` "Healthy Pumpkin Pie Cheesecake 🎃 Easy Dessert!"

## Ingredients

`[¶1]` Cream cheese (8 oz/225g, softened), pumpkin puree (½ cup), egg (1, large), maple
syrup (¼ cup), Greek yogurt (½ cup), pumpkin pie spice (1 tsp), vanilla extract (1 tsp).

## Method

`[¶2]` Preheat to 160°C (325°F); line a 7-inch springform pan with parchment. Beat the cream
cheese smooth, then add the pumpkin puree, Greek yogurt, maple syrup, egg, pumpkin pie spice,
and vanilla, mixing "just until smooth." Bake 40–45 minutes "until the edges are set and the
center has a slight jiggle." Cool completely, then refrigerate at least 4 hours before
slicing.
