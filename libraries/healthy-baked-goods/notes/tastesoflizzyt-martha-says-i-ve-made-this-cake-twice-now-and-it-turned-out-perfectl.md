# Peach Blueberry Cake with Sugar Crunch Top

## Central claim

**A single-layer sour cream coffee cake studded with fresh peaches and blueberries, distinguished
by a "sugar crunch" top made by sprinkling sugar over the batter before baking rather than glazing
after.** The Instagram caption gives only a testimonial and a link (`[¶1]` "Martha says 'I've made
this cake twice now, and it turned out perfectly each time.'" `[¶5]` links to the blog), so the
recipe itself is drawn from the creator's blog, fetched here as a second source. `` `peach-blueberry-cake-tastesoflizzyt`[¶1] ``
"The sugar crunch is what sets this cake apart from other peach blueberry cakes."

## Ingredients

`` `peach-blueberry-cake-tastesoflizzyt`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card (serves 8):

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Salted butter, softened | ½ cup |
| Granulated sugar, divided | 1¼ cups (1 cup in the batter, ¼ cup for the top) |
| Eggs | 2 large |
| Vanilla extract | 1 tsp |
| All-purpose flour | 1⅓ cups |
| Baking powder | 1¼ tsp |
| Salt | ¼ tsp |
| Full-fat sour cream | ½ cup |
| Blueberries | 1 cup |
| Fresh peaches, diced | 1 cup (1–2 peaches) |

## Method

`` `peach-blueberry-cake-tastesoflizzyt`[¶1] `` Preheat to 350°F and prepare a 9-inch round cake
pan. Whip the butter with 1 cup of the sugar (reserving ¼ cup for the top) until pale and fluffy,
about 5 minutes, then beat in the eggs one at a time followed by the vanilla. Whisk the flour,
baking powder, and salt together separately, then add to the butter mixture alternately with the
sour cream, beginning and ending with the flour, mixing just until combined each time. Fold in the
blueberries and peaches with a rubber spatula, spread the batter in the pan, and sprinkle the
reserved ¼ cup sugar evenly over the top — optionally misted with 1–2 tbsp hot water first "for a
smoother, glossy sugar crunch." Bake 35–40 minutes, until the top is crackly and lightly browned
and the center still jiggles slightly; a toothpick will show moist crumbs. Cool completely in the
pan before serving.

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

Framed as a mid-to-late summer cake, timed to when fresh peaches come into season
(`` `peach-blueberry-cake-tastesoflizzyt`[¶1] `` "as soon as the middle of July hits, I switch up
the fruits to include fresh peaches"). The blog notes frozen or canned fruit can substitute for
fresh if well-drained first, and full-fat sour cream can be swapped for whole-milk Greek yogurt
"which adds protein," but warns light or fat-free sour cream "doesn't work as well." No specific
dietary restriction (gluten-free, dairy-free, etc.) is addressed.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **"Sugar crunch top"** — the creator's term for a crackly baked-sugar crust formed by
  sprinkling raw sugar over batter before baking, distinct from a post-bake glaze or icing
