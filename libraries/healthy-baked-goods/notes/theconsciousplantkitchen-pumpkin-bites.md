# Pumpkin Bites (3 Ingredient, No Eggs, Gluten-Free)

## Central claim

**A three-ingredient almond-flour pumpkin dough, rolled into mini-muffin-sized balls, baked
plain, then optionally brushed with maple syrup and rolled in pumpkin-spiced sugar.** `[¶1]`
"No gluten, no dairy, no eggs. Comment' RECIPE' and I'll DM you the full recipe for these
Pumpkin Bites are moist, soft in the center, covered with cinnamon sugar, and crispy on the
edges." The full recipe is posted on the creator's own site rather than gated behind a DM: `[¶1]`
"recipe linked in bio and on my website." `` `theconsciousplantkitchen-pumpkin-bites-blog`[¶1] ``
"These Pumpkin Bites are moist, soft in the center, covered with cinnamon sugar, and crispy on
the edges."

## Ingredients

`` `theconsciousplantkitchen-pumpkin-bites-blog`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card. Dough:
almond flour (1 cup), pumpkin puree (3 tablespoons), maple syrup (3 tablespoons), pumpkin spice
(1 teaspoon, optional — "recommended if you don't coat them with pumpkin spice sugar").
Optional coating: unrefined cane sugar (½ cup), pumpkin spice (1–2 teaspoons), maple syrup (2
tablespoons, for brushing).

## Method

`` `theconsciousplantkitchen-pumpkin-bites-blog`[¶1] `` Preheat to 350°F (180°C); line a
mini-muffin pan or baking sheet. Stir almond flour, pumpkin puree, maple syrup, and pumpkin
spice (if using) "until it forms a sticky, firm dough." Roll into small balls, bake 12–14
minutes "until the edges are golden and dry." Cool on the tray 5 minutes, then transfer to a
rack — "they are still a bit soft and firm up as they cool down." Cool a further 30 minutes
before eating plain or coating. For the coating: brush each bite with maple syrup and roll it
in the pumpkin-spice sugar mixture.

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

A fall pumpkin-spice craving in a mini-donut-hole-sized bite, pitched at readers avoiding
gluten, dairy, and eggs. `[¶1]` "No gluten, no dairy, no eggs."

## Analogies worth reusing

`` `theconsciousplantkitchen-pumpkin-bites-blog`[¶1] `` The blog compares the finished texture
to a familiar shape rather than a familiar bake: "a bit like mini donuts, but they are slightly
moister with a borderline cakey texture inside."

## Source-stated confidence

The blog presents both the base dough ratios and the bake time as tested defaults rather than
starting points, and adds troubleshooting-style substitution notes (e.g. on almond flour vs.
almond meal: "will result in a darker color and grittier texture") that read as observed
outcomes rather than general caution.

## Conflicts with other sources

No other note in this library uses a plain almond-flour-pumpkin-maple dough baked without egg
or dairy — the closest relative, `inbloombakery-pumpkin-cheesecake-cookies`, drains pumpkin
purée the same way to control moisture but binds its dough with butter, egg yolks, and flour
rather than almond flour and maple syrup alone. No genuine conflict; these are two different
approaches to the same fall pumpkin-spice craving.
