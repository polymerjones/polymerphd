# Coconut Cheesecake

## Central claim

**A classic graham-cracker-crust cheesecake where coconut milk stands in for the usual
sour cream/heavy cream, giving the filling its coconut flavor without any shredded coconut
in the batter itself.** `[¶2]` "4 Tablespoons salted butter, melted / 1½ cups crushed
graham crackers / 3 (8-ounce) blocks cream cheese, room temperature / 3 large eggs / ½ cup
coconut milk / 1 cup granulated sugar / 1 Tablespoon cornstarch." Shredded coconut and Cool
Whip appear only as optional garnish, not in the bake itself.

## Ingredients

Butter (4 tbsp, melted), crushed graham crackers (1½ cups), cream cheese (3× 8 oz blocks,
room temp), eggs (3 large), coconut milk (½ cup), granulated sugar (1 cup), cornstarch
(1 tbsp). Optional topping: Cool Whip, shredded coconut.

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

`[¶5]` "It's perfect for holidays, spring gatherings, potlucks, or anytime you're craving a
creamy coconut dessert." No dietary restriction is claimed — standard cream cheese, sugar,
and a graham-cracker crust throughout.
