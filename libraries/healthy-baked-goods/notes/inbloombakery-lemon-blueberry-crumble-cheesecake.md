# Lemon Blueberry Crumble Cheesecake

## Central claim

**A full water-bath-baked cheesecake — not a cookie, despite sharing this library's
"cheesecake cookie" naming pattern — on a Nilla Wafer crust, with lemon cheesecake batter
swirled (not fully mixed) with homemade blueberry jam, and a pre-chilled lemon streusel
baked on top.** `[¶2]` "Bright & creamy lemon cheesecake with jammy blueberries throughout,
over a Nilla Wafer crust, topped with a thick layer of sweet, buttery streusel." The
Instagram caption points to `[¶3]` "my blog inbloombakery.com" for the full recipe.

## Ingredients

`` `lemon-blueberry-crumble-cheesecake`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card. Blueberry jam:
fresh blueberries (16 oz/340g), sugar (¾ cup/150g), lemon juice (2 tbsp), vanilla (1½ tsp),
cornstarch (2 tsp). Crumble: sugar (½ cup + 2 tbsp/125g), lemon zest (½ tbsp), unsalted
butter (½ cup/112g, softened), vanilla (½ tsp), flour (1¼ cups/156g), salt (¼ tsp). Crust:
Nilla Wafers (~68 cookies/4 cups, crushed), melted butter (½ cup/112g), sugar (2 tbsp/25g).
Cheesecake: cream cheese (32 oz/907g, room temp), sugar (1¼ cups/250g), cornstarch (2
tbsp/16g), sour cream (⅓ cup/82g), lemon zest (3 tbsp/30g), vanilla (1 tbsp), eggs (3) and
egg yolks (3), all room temp. Topping: heavy cream (¾ cup) and powdered sugar (¼ cup) for
whipped cream; fresh blueberries and lemon slices to garnish.

## Method

`` `lemon-blueberry-crumble-cheesecake`[¶1] `` Cook the jam ingredients 20–23 minutes until
thick; cool. Make the crumble (rub lemon zest into sugar, cream in butter and vanilla, mix
in flour and salt to coarse crumbs) and chill it. Press the crust into a foil-wrapped 9"
springform pan and pre-bake 10 minutes. Beat the cream cheese, sugar, and cornstarch smooth;
add sour cream, zest, and vanilla; add eggs and yolks one at a time on low speed, stopping
"as soon as the last one is fully mixed in" to avoid overmixing. Layer half the batter, half
the jam swirled in with a knife, then repeat; top with the chilled streusel. Bake in a water
bath at 350°F for 1 hour 15 minutes, then turn the oven off and crack the door for another
hour before removing. Cool completely, then refrigerate 8 hours or overnight before
unmolding and piping whipped cream around the edge.
