# Lemon Blueberry Cake with Cream Cheese Frosting

## Central claim

**A flour-tossed-blueberry lemon cake (to stop the berries sinking) with a
homemade-buttermilk shortcut, finished with cream cheese frosting and a separately cooked
blueberry sauce swirled on top rather than baked in.** `[¶4]` "200g unsalted butter,
softened / 180g caster sugar / 3 large eggs... 170g fresh blueberries / 1 tbsp flour (for
coating the blueberries)."

## Ingredients

`[¶4]` Cake: unsalted butter (200g), caster sugar (180g), eggs (3), vanilla (1 tsp), lemon
zest (1) and juice (1–2 tbsp), buttermilk (60g/¼ cup — homemade from `[¶6]` 60ml milk + 1
tsp lemon juice, rested 5–10 min), all-purpose flour (250g), baking powder (1 tsp), baking
soda (½ tsp), fresh blueberries (170g), flour for coating (1 tbsp). `[¶13]` Frosting:
unsalted butter (125g), cream cheese (125g), icing sugar (250g, sifted), vanilla (1 tsp),
lemon juice (1 tsp, optional). `[¶19]` Blueberry drizzle: blueberries (85g), sugar (25g),
lemon juice (½ tbsp), water (1 tbsp + ½ tbsp for slurry), cornstarch (½ tsp).

## Method

`[¶9]` Preheat to 170°C, grease and line an 8×8" pan. Toss the blueberries in flour. Cream
the butter and sugar, add eggs one at a time, then vanilla, zest, and juice. Whisk the dry
ingredients, add alternately with the buttermilk, then fold in the floured blueberries. Bake
40–50 minutes until a skewer comes out clean; cool completely. `[¶15]` For the frosting:
beat the butter, add cream cheese, then gradually beat in the icing sugar, vanilla, and
lemon juice. `[¶21]` For the drizzle: simmer the blueberries, sugar, lemon juice, and water
5–7 minutes until soft, stir in a cornstarch slurry, and cook 1–2 minutes more until
thickened; cool completely. `[¶23]`–`[¶24]` Spread the frosting over the cooled cake,
drizzle with the cooled blueberry sauce, and swirl gently.
