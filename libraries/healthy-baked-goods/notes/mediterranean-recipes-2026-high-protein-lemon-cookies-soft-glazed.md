# High-Protein Lemon Cookies (Soft & Glazed)

## Central claim

**A blended-cottage-cheese cookie dough built around vanilla protein powder and oat flour
instead of all-purpose flour and butter, scooped and baked rather than rolled, then
finished with an optional powdered-sweetener lemon glaze.** `[¶3]` "1 cup low-fat cottage
cheese (or thick Greek yogurt) / 1 large egg / ½ cup vanilla protein powder... / ¾ cup oat
flour... / ¼ cup granulated sweetener... / 2 tbsp lemon juice / 1 tbsp lemon zest / 1 tsp
baking powder / ½ tsp vanilla extract / Pinch of salt."

## Ingredients

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Cookie dough | Low-fat cottage cheese or thick Greek yogurt (1 cup), egg, vanilla protein powder (½ cup, ~20–25g protein/scoop), oat flour (¾ cup), granulated sweetener (¼ cup), lemon juice (2 tbsp), lemon zest (1 tbsp), baking powder (1 tsp), vanilla extract (½ tsp), salt (pinch) |
| Lemon glaze (optional) | Powdered sweetener (½ cup), lemon juice (1–2 tbsp), extra lemon zest for topping |

Makes about 18 cookies, `[¶1]` as stated.

## Method

`[¶5]` Preheat to 175°C/350°F, line a tray. Blend the cottage cheese smooth first —
"important for soft texture" — then mix in the egg, lemon juice, zest, and vanilla. Add
the protein powder, oat flour, sweetener, baking powder, and salt; mix to a "thick,
scoopable dough." Scoop about 2 tbsp per cookie, flatten slightly, and bake 10–13 minutes
until set and lightly golden underneath. Cool completely, then whisk and drizzle the glaze,
letting it set 5–10 minutes.

## Nutrition, as stated

`[¶5]` Per cookie, approximate: "Calories: ~95–110 / Protein: ~6–8 g / Carbs: ~10 g / Fat:
~2 g," with the caveat "(Depends on protein powder & sweetener used.)"

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

A high-protein cookie built to satisfy a soft, glazed sugar-cookie craving without relying
on butter or all-purpose flour as the base. No gluten-free or dairy-free claim is made —
oat flour and dairy cottage cheese/yogurt are both used as-is.
