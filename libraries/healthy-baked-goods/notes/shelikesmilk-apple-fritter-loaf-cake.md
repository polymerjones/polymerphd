# Apple Fritter Loaf Cake

## Central claim

**A baked reinterpretation of a fried apple fritter — cinnamon-sugar apple chunks layered
and swirled through a vanilla loaf batter, then finished with a powdered-sugar glaze — built
specifically to skip hot-oil frying.** `[¶3]` "I love apple fritters, but I don't love the
hassle & mess of hot oil frying them at home to enjoy." `[¶4]` "that is until I transformed
them into a baking recipe! a simple & in my opinion, more delicious way to enjoy my favorite
fall dessert."

## Ingredients

`[¶6]` Cinnamon-sugar apple filling: brown sugar (⅓ cup), cinnamon (3 tsp total, split
between the filling and tossed with the apples), apples (2½, peeled and rough-chopped),
granulated sugar (2 tbsp, for tossing the apples). Batter: softened butter (½ cup),
granulated sugar (⅔ cup + 1 tbsp), eggs (2), vanilla (1½ tsp), flour (1½ cups), baking
powder (1¾ tsp), salt (1 tsp), milk (½ cup + 3 tbsp, split between the batter and the
glaze). Glaze: powdered sugar (1 cup), milk (3 tbsp, to finish).

## Method

`[¶7]` Preheat to 350°F; prep a 9×5" loaf pan. Mix the brown sugar and 2 tsp cinnamon for the
topping; separately toss the chopped apples with 2 tbsp granulated sugar and the remaining 1
tsp cinnamon. Cream the butter and ⅔ cup granulated sugar until fluffy (about 2 minutes), then
beat in the eggs and vanilla. Whisk the flour, baking powder, and salt separately, add to the
creamed mixture, then mix in ½ cup milk until smooth. Layer: pour half the batter into the
pan, top with half the apple mixture, then half the brown-sugar-cinnamon mix; repeat with the
remaining batter, apples, and brown sugar mix, then "use a knife or toothpick to gently swirl
the apple mixture into the batter." Bake about 1 hour to a clean toothpick; cool on a rack.
`[¶8]` For the icing: combine the powdered sugar with the remaining 3 tbsp milk, adjusting
milk or powdered sugar "to reach desired consistency," then drizzle over the cooled loaf.

`[¶5]` "a highly requested fall treat in my house." No dietary restriction is claimed.

## Conflicts with other sources

`everydayextra-apple-cinnamon-crumble-loaf` uses the identical layering technique — half the
batter, a filling layer, repeat — in the same loaf-pan format, but the two diverge on the
apple treatment and the middle layer: that recipe grates the apple directly into the batter
and layers in a separate flour-brown-sugar-butter crumble, while this one keeps the apple as
a distinct rough-chopped, cinnamon-sugar-tossed filling swirled in with a knife, and skips a
butter crumble entirely. Both finish with a powdered-sugar glaze drizzle. No genuine conflict —
two independent creators building the same layered-loaf structure around different apple
treatments.
