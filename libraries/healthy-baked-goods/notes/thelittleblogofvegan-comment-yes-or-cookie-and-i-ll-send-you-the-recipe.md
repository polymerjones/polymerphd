# Vegan White Chocolate Raspberry Cookies

## Central claim

**A fully vegan cookie using a flaxseed "egg" as binder, stuffed with a raspberry jam
center, loaded with dairy-free white chocolate chunks and freeze-dried raspberries, then
dip-coated in a second layer of melted dairy-free white chocolate.** `[¶2]` "VEGAN + 10
MINUTES TO BAKE! Deliciously crisp on the outside, gooey on the inside." The Instagram
caption gives a direct blog URL (`[¶4]` a link to the exact recipe page), fetched here as
the full second source.

## Ingredients

`` `vegan-white-chocolate-raspberry-cookies`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card. Cookie:
water (20ml) with ground flaxseed (2 tsp, made into a "flax egg"), dairy-free butter
(120g), caster sugar (150g), vanilla extract (1 tsp), plain flour (240g), bicarbonate of
soda (1 tsp), cornstarch (1 tsp), dairy-free white chocolate chunks (100g), freeze-dried
raspberries (50g), raspberry jam (for the center). Chocolate shell: dairy-free white
chocolate (200g), freeze-dried raspberries (to decorate).

## Method

`` `vegan-white-chocolate-raspberry-cookies`[¶1] `` Mix the water and flaxseed and let sit 5
minutes to form the flax egg. Cream the dairy-free butter, sugar, and vanilla; mix in the
flax egg. Sift in the flour, bicarbonate of soda, and cornstarch — the creator notes
cornstarch's role here is specifically to stop the cookies spreading too much. Fold in the
white chocolate and freeze-dried raspberries. Scoop into 40g balls, press a dent, fill with
raspberry jam, and reshape the dough fully around it "to prevent it oozing out when baking."
Bake at 180°C (fan) for 10–12 minutes, then reshape warm with a round cutter. Once fully
cool, dip in melted white chocolate (base first, then drizzle over the top), and finish with
freeze-dried raspberries while the chocolate is still wet.
