# Air Fryer Lemon Blueberry Donut Holes

## Central claim

**A blended-cottage-cheese donut hole dough, rolled and air-fried rather than deep-fried,
studded with frozen blueberries and finished with a powdered-sugar glaze.** `[¶2]` "¾ cup
Good Culture 2% cottage cheese, blended / 1 cup all-purpose flour / 1 egg / 1 tbsp
sugar-free maple syrup / Zest of 1 lemon / 2 tsp baking powder / ¼ tsp baking soda / ¼ tsp
salt / ⅓ cup frozen wild blueberries." `[¶1]` "Soft on the inside, bursting with
blueberries, and dipped in a sweet vanilla glaze."

## Ingredients

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Dough | Cottage cheese (¾ cup, blended smooth), all-purpose flour (1 cup), egg, sugar-free maple syrup (1 tbsp), lemon zest, baking powder (2 tsp), baking soda (¼ tsp), salt (¼ tsp), frozen wild blueberries (⅓ cup) |
| Vanilla glaze | Powdered sugar (½ cup), vanilla extract (¼ tsp), milk (1–2 tbsp) |

## Method

`[¶5]` Whisk the blended cottage cheese, egg, syrup, and lemon zest. Add the flour, baking
powder, baking soda, and salt; mix just until combined. Fold in the frozen blueberries.
Scoop and roll into about 12 donut holes — "If the dough is too sticky, add flour 1 tbsp
at a time until workable." Air fry at 350°F for 6–8 minutes until lightly golden. Cool,
whisk the glaze, then dip. `[¶8]` No-air-fryer alternative given directly: "Bake at 350°F
for 12–15 minutes!"
