# Raspberry Cheesecake Crumble Bars

## Central claim

**A three-layer bar — buttery crumble base, unbaked sweetened cream cheese, homemade
raspberry jam — topped with more of the same crumble, using one batch of crumble split
between crust and topping.** `[¶1]` "buttery crumble is layered with sweet cream cheese,
homemade raspberry jam (made using frozen raspberries!), and even MORE crumble!" The
Instagram caption gates the written version (`[¶2]` "Comment 'send recipe' and I'll DM it to
you, or find the full recipe on my website!"), fetched here as the second source.

## Ingredients

`` `raspberry-cheesecake-crumble-bars-butternut-bakery`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card.
Raspberry filling: frozen raspberries (12 oz bag/3 cups), granulated sugar (¼ cup),
cornstarch (1 tbsp), water or lemon juice (2 tbsp). Cream cheese filling: full-fat cream
cheese (12 oz, room temp), granulated sugar (⅓ cup), vanilla extract (1 tsp), egg (1
large). Crumble (used for both crust and topping): light brown sugar (½ cup), granulated
sugar (½ cup), all-purpose flour (2¾ cups), kosher salt (½ tsp), baking powder (1 tsp),
unsalted butter (1 cup, melted and cooled).

## Method

`` `raspberry-cheesecake-crumble-bars-butternut-bakery`[¶1] `` Cook the raspberries, sugar,
and cornstarch with water or lemon juice until thick and jammy (5–8 minutes), then chill.
Mash the cream cheese with sugar, then the vanilla and egg, until smooth. Mix the dry
crumble ingredients, stir in the melted butter until evenly moist but still crumbly. Press
about ⅔ of the crumble into the pan as a thin base layer, spread the cream cheese layer
over it, spoon (not pour, to avoid disturbing the cream cheese) the raspberry jam on top,
then break up and scatter the remaining crumble over the surface. Bake at 350°F for 35–40
minutes until the edges are golden and the center barely jiggles. Cool at room temperature
20 minutes, then refrigerate at least 2 hours before slicing into 12 bars.

## Source-stated confidence

The blog is explicit about which substitutions it has and hasn't tested: a 1:1 gluten-free
flour swap is recommended by brand name as something the creator has used successfully, and
swapping in blueberries or blackberries is offered by reference to the creator's own other
posted jam recipes — but jarred jam and fresh raspberries are flagged as workable-but-untested
alternatives ("I think that could work," "You can, but..."), preserved here as stated
uncertainty rather than a confirmed substitution.
