# Lemon Raspberry Cookies

## Central claim

**An eggless, gluten-free sugar cookie where a cornstarch-water slurry replaces the egg,
frozen (not fresh) chopped raspberries are folded in for color and flavor without excess
moisture, and the dough must be frozen — not just chilled — for at least an hour before
baking.** `` `lemon-raspberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` "these cookies are gluten free,
nut free, eggless and can easily be vegan!" The Instagram caption gates the recipe (`[¶2]`
"Comment 'lemon raspberry' and I'll send the recipe link directly to you! Or google: lane
and grey lemon raspberry"); the blog itself is fetched here as the second, full-recipe
source.

## Ingredients

`` `lemon-raspberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card:
gluten-free 1:1 flour with xanthan gum (2 cups), baking powder (1.5 tsp), kosher salt
(½ tsp), unsalted butter or vegan baking stick (½ cup), granulated sugar (1¼ cups, plus
3 tbsp for coating), cornstarch mixed with water (2 heaping tbsp + 3 tbsp water, as an
egg substitute), milk (3 tbsp), fresh lemon juice (2 tbsp) and zest (1 tbsp + 1 tsp,
divided), frozen raspberries, chopped (½ cup). Optional glaze: confectioners' sugar (1
cup), 1–2 frozen raspberries, water.

## Method

`` `lemon-raspberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` Zest and juice the lemons; finely chop
frozen raspberries and keep them frozen until ready to use — "there will be too much
moisture" otherwise. Cream the butter and sugar, beat in the cornstarch slurry, milk, zest,
and juice, then fold in the flour mixture and finally the frozen raspberries. **Freeze the
dough at least 1 hour** — "You must not skip this step... it must be frozen, not in the
fridge." Scoop (11 large cookies), roll in lemon sugar, bake at 325°F for 15–16 minutes,
and reshape immediately with a spatula while warm. Cool before glazing.

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

`` `lemon-raspberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` Framed around allergy accommodation
first: gluten-free, nut-free, egg-free, and "can easily be dairy free (vegan)" by swapping
in a dairy-free flour blend, vegan sugar, vegan baking sticks, and non-dairy milk. The
creator states plainly this hasn't been formally tested with those substitutions: "This
recipe has not been tested with these substitutions and/or variations due to celiac disease
and food allergies."

## Source-stated confidence

The blog is explicit about which technique details are load-bearing versus flexible —
freezing (not refrigerating) the dough, using frozen and chopped (not fresh or whole)
raspberries, and not substituting a different fruit are all stated as hard requirements
tied to the eggless dough's moisture balance, not stylistic preferences. The
untested-substitutions disclaimer (§ above) is preserved as the creator's own stated
confidence boundary.

egg substitute, chill/freeze requirement, sugar coating, fruit glaze) — they converge
