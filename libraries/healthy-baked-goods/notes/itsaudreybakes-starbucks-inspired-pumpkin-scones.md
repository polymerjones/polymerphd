# Starbucks-Inspired Pumpkin Scones

## Central claim

**A copycat of the Starbucks pumpkin scone — cold-butter cut into a pumpkin-spiced flour
base, wedge-cut and chilled before baking, then double-dipped in a plain icing and a
separate pumpkin-spice drizzle.** `[¶1]` "Starbucks-Inspired Pumpkin Scones... This with a
pumpkin cream chai." `[¶2]` "(recipe makes 8 scones)."

## Ingredients

`[¶2]` Dough: all-purpose flour (2¼ cups/280g, spooned and leveled), baking powder (2 tsp),
salt (½ tsp), pumpkin spice (2 tsp), cinnamon (½ tsp), nutmeg (pinch), brown sugar (¼
cup/50g, packed), granulated sugar (3 tbsp/38g), cold unsalted butter (½ cup/113g, cubed),
pumpkin purée — not pie filling (½ cup/120g), cold heavy cream (¼ cup/60g, plus extra for
brushing), egg (1), vanilla bean paste or extract (1½ tsp). `[¶3]` Icing: powdered sugar (1
cup/120g), heavy cream (~2 tbsp), vanilla (½ tsp). `[¶4]` Pumpkin drizzle: powdered sugar
(½ cup/60g), pumpkin purée (1 tsp), pumpkin spice (¼–½ tsp), heavy cream (1–2 tsp/5–10g).

## Method

`[¶5]` Whisk the dry ingredients and sugars, then cut in the cold butter until it looks like
coarse crumbs. `[¶6]` Whisk the pumpkin, cream, egg, and vanilla separately; add to the dry
mix and combine gently — "don't overwork it." Press into a 6–7" circle, cut into 8 wedges,
and chill 15–20 minutes. `[¶7]` Bake at 400°F for 16–20 minutes, brushing tops with cream
before baking. Cool completely. `[¶8]` Dip tops in the thick icing, let set slightly, then
drizzle with the pumpkin spice glaze.

Explicitly framed as a copycat of a specific commercial product — `[¶1]` paired with "a
pumpkin cream chai" the same way the Starbucks original is typically ordered. No dietary
