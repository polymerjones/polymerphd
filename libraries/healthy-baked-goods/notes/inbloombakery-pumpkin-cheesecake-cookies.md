# Pumpkin Cheesecake Cookies

## Central claim

**A pumpkin cookie stuffed with a frozen cream cheese disc, where the pumpkin purée must be
pressed dry with paper towels — down from ½ cup to just under ¼ cup — or the cookies bake
cakey instead of chewy.** `[¶2]` "The chewiest pumpkin spice cookies filled with creamy
cheesecake and rolled in spiced sugar." `` `pumpkin-cheesecake-cookies`[¶1] `` "Pumpkin is
hard to get right in cookies because it adds so much moisture to baked goods, but by drying
it before adding it to the dough, the cookies keep their perfect chewy texture." The
Instagram caption points to `[¶3]` "inbloombakery.com" for the full recipe.

## Ingredients

`` `pumpkin-cheesecake-cookies`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card. Cheesecake filling:
cream cheese (6 oz/170g, cold), sugar (3 tbsp/38g), vanilla (½ tsp). Spiced sugar: sugar
(¼ cup/50g), pumpkin pie spice (½ tsp). Cookies: canned pumpkin purée, dried (½ cup/122g,
specifically Libby's brand per the creator), all-purpose flour (1¾ cups/219g), pumpkin pie
spice (1 tbsp), baking powder (½ tsp), baking soda (½ tsp), salt (½ tsp), unsalted butter
(¾ cup/168g, softened), light brown sugar (1 cup/220g), egg yolks (2, room temp), vanilla
(2 tsp).

## Method

`` `pumpkin-cheesecake-cookies`[¶1] `` Beat the cheesecake filling fluffy, portion into 16
discs, and freeze solid. Press the pumpkin purée between paper towels repeatedly — "at
least four more times until hardly any liquid transfers" — until reduced from ½ cup to just
under ¼ cup. Whisk the dry spice/flour mixture. Cream the butter and brown sugar, beat in
the egg yolks and vanilla, mix in the dried pumpkin, then the dry ingredients. Scoop 16
portions, flatten, enclose a frozen cheesecake ball, reshape into a ball, and roll in the
spiced sugar. Bake 6 at a time at 350°F for 12–13 minutes.

## Source-stated confidence

The blog states a specific, testable failure mode tied directly to skipping the drying
step ("Why did my cookies come out cakey? ... not enough moisture was removed from the
pumpkin") and names a preferred brand (Libby's) based on stated consistency experience
across "almost all cans" tried — both presented as tested findings, not general advice. It
also explicitly declines to vouch for fresh pumpkin purée as a substitute: "It may cause
the cookies to bake incorrectly."
