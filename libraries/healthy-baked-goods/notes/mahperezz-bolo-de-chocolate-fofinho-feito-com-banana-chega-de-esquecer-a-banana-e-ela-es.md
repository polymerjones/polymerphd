# Bolo de Chocolate Fofinho, Feito com Banana (Fluffy Chocolate Cake Made with Banana)

## Central claim

**A mashed-banana chocolate cake, in this creator's version made with oats standing in for
flour, framed as a way to use up ripe bananas before they spoil.** Given entirely in Brazilian
Portuguese by the creator, quoted verbatim here rather than translated, per this project's
rule to preserve a source's own words. `[¶1]` "Bolo de chocolate fofinho, feito com banana
(chega de esquecer a banana e ela estragar kkk)" ("Fluffy chocolate cake, made with banana —
no more forgetting the banana and letting it spoil, lol"). `[¶3]` "-1 xícara de farinha (usei
aveia)" ("1 cup of flour (I used oats)") — a substitution the creator flags as her own choice,
not the base recipe.

## Ingredients

`[¶3]` Ripe bananas (3, "Nanicas" — the common Brazilian Cavendish-type banana), eggs (3),
sugar (3 tablespoons, "usei refinado" — "I used refined"), melted butter (3 tablespoons), 70%
cocoa powder (3 tablespoons), flour (1 cup — the creator substituted oats here), baking powder
(1 tablespoon), chocolate chips or chopped chocolate (to taste). `[¶4]` Pan: rectangular,
23×13cm.

## Method

`[¶6]` "Amasse as bananas e misture os ovos." ("Mash the bananas and mix in the eggs.") Add
the remaining ingredients, stir in chopped chocolate (optional), pour into the greased pan,
and bake in a preheated 180°C oven for about 25 minutes, "ou até que asse" ("or until it's
baked through").

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

A use-up-the-ripe-bananas chocolate cake craving, pitched at avoiding food waste rather than
any dietary restriction — `[¶1]` "chega de esquecer a banana e ela estragar" ("no more
forgetting the banana and letting it spoil"). No dietary restriction is claimed; the oats
substitution is the creator's personal preference, not framed as gluten-free.
