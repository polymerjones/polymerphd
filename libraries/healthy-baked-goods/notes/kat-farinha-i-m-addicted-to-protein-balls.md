# Pumpkin Protein Balls

## Central claim

**A no-bake protein ball where blotting the canned pumpkin dry before mixing is presented
as the one non-negotiable step** — `[¶4]` "Blot the pumpkin between paper towels until it's
visibly thicker. Non-negotiable. Wet pumpkin will not roll." Built on beef isolate protein
powder and almond flour/butter, dipped in white chocolate.

## Ingredients

`[¶3]` Canned pumpkin purée, blotted dry (1 cup), almond butter (⅓ cup), beef isolate
protein powder (4 oz), blanched almond flour (½ cup), monk fruit sweetener (¼ cup), pumpkin
pie spice (2 tsp), vanilla (1 tsp), sea salt (½ tsp), white chocolate chips with coconut oil
(½ tbsp) for coating. Makes 16 balls (4 servings of 4).

## Method

`[¶4]` Blot the pumpkin between paper towels until visibly thicker. Stir it with the almond
butter, vanilla, and monk fruit until smooth. Add the protein powder, almond flour, spice,
and salt, and mix into a thick dough. Roll into 16 balls and rest 10 minutes in the fridge.
Melt the coconut oil with the white chocolate chips, coat the chilled balls, and return to
the fridge at least 10 minutes to set.
