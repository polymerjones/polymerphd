# Lemon Brownies with White Chocolate Glaze

## Central claim

**A one-bowl, brownie-textured (not cakey) lemon bar, where creaming the butter and sugar
— rather than melting the butter, as an actual brownie would — is what the creator
identifies as the fix for a too-gooey center in her own recipe testing.**
`` `why-every-lemon-lover-needs-this-brownie-recipe-in-their-life`[¶1] `` "These Lemon
Brownies are soft, fudgy citrus bars finished with a glossy white chocolate lemon glaze.
The best part? The whole thing comes together in one bowl." The Instagram caption gates the
recipe (`[¶1]` "comment COZY and ill DM you the recipe").

## Ingredients

`` `why-every-lemon-lover-needs-this-brownie-recipe-in-their-life`[¶1] `` — the blog's own
recipe card: granulated sugar (1¼ cups/272g), lemon zest (2 tbsp), unsalted butter
(¾ cup/170g, softened), vanilla extract (1 tsp), lemon extract (1 tsp), lemon juice
(2 tbsp), eggs (2 large), all-purpose flour (1¼ cups/162.5g), baking powder (¾ tsp), kosher
salt (½ tsp). Glaze: white chocolate (¾ cup/113g), lemon juice (2 tbsp), lemon extract
(¼ tsp), salt (pinch), lemon zest (1 tbsp, for garnish).

## Method

`` `why-every-lemon-lover-needs-this-brownie-recipe-in-their-life`[¶1] `` Rub the lemon
zest into the sugar by hand to release the oils, then cream in the softened butter, vanilla,
and lemon extract until fluffy. Beat in the lemon juice and eggs, then the flour, baking
powder, and salt — mixing only until combined, since "overmixing can encourage gluten to
form" in this recipe. Bake at 350°F for about 25 minutes. Let cool before glazing: melt the
white chocolate with lemon juice, stir in lemon extract and salt, pour over the cooled bars,
and let set 20 minutes before slicing.

## The creator's own testing notes

`` `why-every-lemon-lover-needs-this-brownie-recipe-in-their-life`[¶1] `` The blog documents
three explicit test iterations: Test #1 (melted butter and white chocolate mixed into the
batter) was "a solid start" but needed more moisture, lift, and lemon flavor. Test #2 (part
brown sugar, added baking powder, more zest) improved the lemon flavor but turned "too gooey
in the center," and both versions were judged too rich to glaze. Test #3 — creaming butter
and sugar instead of melting, and moving the white chocolate out of the batter and into a
glaze — is the version given above.
