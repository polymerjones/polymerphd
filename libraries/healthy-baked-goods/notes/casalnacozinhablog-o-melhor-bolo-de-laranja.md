# O Melhor Bolo de Laranja (The Best Orange Cake)

## Central claim

**A Brazilian-Portuguese-language orange cake recipe using both fresh orange zest and
juice plus plain yogurt in the batter, finished with an orange soak and a simple
orange-powdered-sugar glaze.** Given entirely in Portuguese by the creator, quoted verbatim
here rather than translated, per this project's rule to preserve a source's own words.
`[¶2]` "Ingredientes:" `[¶3]` "155g de açúcar / Raspas de 1 laranja / 55g de manteiga
derretida / 55ml de óleo / 2 ovos / 50g de iogurte natural / 30ml de suco de laranja / 140g
de farinha de trigo / ½ colher de sopa de fermento em pó / 1 pitada de sal."

## Ingredients

`[¶3]` Batter: sugar (155g), zest of 1 orange, melted butter (55g), oil (55ml), eggs (2),
plain/natural yogurt (50g), orange juice (30ml), wheat flour (140g), baking powder (½
tbsp), a pinch of salt. `[¶4]` Soak ("Calda"): orange juice (50ml), sugar (1 tbsp). `[¶5]`
Glaze ("Cobertura"): powdered sugar (1 cup), a little orange juice. `[¶6]` Pan size
("Forma"): 22×12cm.
