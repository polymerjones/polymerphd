# Lemon Blueberry Cookies

## Central claim

**The same eggless, gluten-free base as this creator's other fruit sugar cookies, here
using frozen wild blueberries (not regular-size) microwaved into a quick jam before mixing,
specifically to dye the dough a natural lavender.** `` `lemon-blueberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] ``
"these cookies also gluten free, nut free and can easily be vegan!" `[¶1]` "I have two ways
to make them on the blog. This way makes them a light purple and the other keeps them a
lovely yellow." The Instagram caption gates the recipe (`[¶2]` "Comment 'blueberry' and I'll
send the recipe to you or google: lane and grey lemon blueberry cookies").

## Ingredients

`` `lemon-blueberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` — the blog's own recipe card: Cup4Cup
gluten-free multipurpose flour (2 cups), baking powder (1.5 tsp), kosher salt (½ tsp),
unsalted butter (½ cup), granulated sugar (1¼ cups, plus 3 tbsp for coating), cornstarch
mixed with water (2 rounded tbsp + 3 tbsp water), milk (3 tbsp), fresh lemon juice (2 tbsp)
and zest (1 tbsp + 1 tsp, divided), frozen wild blueberries (1 cup, ½ cup microwaved into
jam first). Glaze: confectioners' sugar (1 cup), blueberry jam (1 tbsp), water.

## Method

`` `lemon-blueberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` Microwave half the blueberries in
20-second bursts until jammy, then cool. Cream butter and sugar, beat in the cornstarch
slurry, milk, zest, and juice, fold in the flour mixture, then fold in the remaining whole
frozen blueberries — "The dough will become a lovely purple color." **Refrigerate at least
1 hour** (unlike the raspberry version, this one is chilled, not frozen). Scoop 11 cookies,
roll in lemon sugar, bake 325°F for 15 minutes, reshape immediately. Glaze once fully cool
with the cooled blueberry jam mixed into confectioners' sugar.

## Cravings, occasions, and dietary needs addressed

Same allergy-accommodation framing as the creator's other fruit cookies: gluten-free,
nut-free, egg-free, vegan-adaptable. `` `lemon-blueberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` The
color itself is stated as a selling point, not just a side effect — "these beauties have a
gorgeous marbled lavender hue."

## Source-stated confidence

The blog is specific about which variable controls the visible color outcome — wild vs.
regular frozen blueberries, and how long the dough chills — and states this as tested,
repeatable cause-and-effect ("chill the dough overnight... it will turn blue instead of
lavender"), not a guess. The untested-substitutions disclaimer applies here too.

## Conflicts with other sources

`` `lemon-raspberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] ``, `` `strawberry-jam-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] ``,
and `` `blueberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare`[¶1] `` share this note's exact base technique
(gluten-free flour, cornstarch-water egg substitute, chill/freeze step, sugar coating, fruit
glaze); this one and `blueberry-cookies-lane-grey-fare` both use blueberries but diverge on
whether lemon zest/juice is included at all — the plain blueberry version omits lemon
entirely.
