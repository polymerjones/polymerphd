# Lemon Blueberry Oatmeal Cream Pies

## Central claim

**A lemon-zest oatmeal cookie sandwiching both a homemade blueberry compote and a lemon
cream cheese frosting — a summer variant of the classic oatmeal cream pie.** `[¶3]` "Meet

## Method

`` `lemon-blueberry-oatmeal-cream-pies-easy-summer-recipe-munchies-by-mallory`[¶1] `` Make
the compote first (blueberries, honey, cornstarch, heated until thickened) so it has time
to cool. Rub the lemon zest into the sugar "until the sugar turns pale yellow" — the
creator's own stated technique for pulling out the fragrant citrus oils — then cream in the
butter, followed by eggs and vanilla. Fold in the dry ingredients (flour, baking powder,
cornstarch, salt, oats), then gently fold in the whole blueberries "so you do not smash
[them] and turn the dough purple." Scoop 24 cookies (~3 tbsp each), bake at 350°F for
11–13 minutes, cool completely. Beat the frosting ingredients together, then assemble:
pipe a ring of frosting onto half the cookies, spoon compote in the center, and sandwich.

## Source-stated confidence

The blog states its own technique choices as deliberate, tested decisions rather than
guesses — e.g., using only lemon zest and no juice "because I want a light, bright lemon
flavor without making the cookies wet," and folding blueberries in gently specifically to
avoid discoloring the dough. Substitutions (frozen berries, raspberry/blackberry swap,
maple syrup for honey) are offered as the creator's own tested alternatives, not
speculation.
