# Apple Cinnamon Crumble Loaf

## Central claim

**A cinnamon-spiced apple loaf built in two layered pours with a brown-sugar crumble sandwiched
in the middle and again on top, finished with a vanilla icing drizzle.** `[¶1]` "🍎 Apple Cinnamon
Crumble Loaf." `[¶8]` "Pour half of dough into bread pan. Layer 1/2 of the crumble. Repeat with
second half of dough & crumble on top."

## Ingredients

`[¶4]`–`[¶6]`:

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Loaf | Sugar (1 cup), flour (2 cups), baking powder (1 tbsp), salt (½ tsp), cinnamon (1 tbsp), 1 egg, milk (1 cup), melted butter (⅓ cup), 2 apples (peeled and grated) |
| Crumble | Flour (½ cup), brown sugar (½ cup), melted butter (4 tbsp) |
| Icing drizzle | Powdered sugar (½ cup), vanilla extract (1 tsp, "good vanilla"), milk (1–2 tbsp) |

## Method

`[¶8]` Preheat the oven to 350°F. Mix the dry loaf ingredients in a large bowl and the wet
ingredients (excluding the apples) in a separate small bowl, then combine and fold in the grated
apples. Line a bread pan with parchment ("you can wet it and wring it out to make any form"), pour
in half the batter, layer with half the crumble, then repeat with the remaining batter and crumble
on top. Bake 55 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean, checking a few minutes early if the
oven runs hot. While the loaf cools, mix the icing ingredients, adjusting consistency with more
powdered sugar (if too thin) or a bit more milk (if too thick), and pour over the cooled loaf.
