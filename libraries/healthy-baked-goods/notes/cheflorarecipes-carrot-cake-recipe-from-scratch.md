# Carrot Cake from Scratch

## Central claim

**A classic three-layer carrot cake spiced with cinnamon, nutmeg, and cloves, with optional
crushed pineapple in the batter, finished in cream cheese frosting.** `[¶1]` "carrot cake recipe
from scratch."

## Ingredients

`[¶3]`:

| Component | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Cake | All-purpose flour (2 cups), baking soda (2 tsp), salt (½ tsp), cinnamon (2 tsp), nutmeg (¼ tsp), cloves (¼ tsp), granulated sugar (1½ cups), brown sugar (½ cup), vegetable oil (1 cup), 4 large eggs, vanilla extract (2 tsp), freshly grated carrots (3 cups), crushed pineapple (½ cup, drained, optional), chopped walnuts (½ cup) |
| Cream cheese frosting | Cream cheese (16 oz, softened), unsalted butter (½ cup, softened), powdered sugar (4 cups), vanilla extract (2 tsp), pinch of salt, chopped walnuts for topping |

## Method

`[¶5]` Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and prepare three 8- or 9-inch round pans with parchment.
Whisk the flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, nutmeg, and cloves in one bowl; in another, whisk
the sugars, oil, eggs, and vanilla until smooth. Combine the dry into the wet just until mixed,
then fold in the grated carrots, pineapple (if using), and walnuts. Divide evenly among the three
pans and bake 25–30 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean. Cool in the pans 10 minutes, then
turn out onto a rack to cool completely. For the frosting, beat the cream cheese and butter until
smooth, then gradually mix in the powdered sugar, vanilla, and salt until fluffy. Stack and frost
the three layers, cover the top and sides with the remaining frosting, sprinkle with chopped
walnuts, and chill 30 minutes before slicing "for cleaner layers."
