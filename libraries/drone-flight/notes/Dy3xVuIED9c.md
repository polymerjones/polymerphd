# DJI Air 3S - 44 Tips & Settings: The Complete Beginners Guide

## Central claim

**This video works through the Air 3S's menus tip-by-tip (44 in total) rather than around a
single flight, prioritizing settings the presenter judges worth changing from default over an
exhaustive menu tour.** `[00:00]` "I have 44 tips and settings for the DJI [Air 3S]." Several
tips are explicitly flagged as new to this model rather than carried over: `[00:00]` face
tracking/face focus "is brand new, I've never seen this before on a DJI drone."

## Safety menu (10:09–16:14)

The obstacle-avoidance action is presented as a graded choice, not a single on/off: `[11:09]`
"obstacle avoidance action is off... if I flew towards a tree it wouldn't notice the tree and it
would hit the tree. The other option I have is brake... [or] bypass, which is my favorite one...
it does a really good job of avoiding obstacles" — with a more aggressive "nifty" bypass variant
flagged as riskier: "it does give you a warning cuz it's a little bit more risky." Return-to-home
altitude is given a specific recommended range rather than a single number: `[12:11]` "I would
recommend anywhere from 80 m to 100 m." One safety toggle is called out with an explicit
consequence if disabled: `[15:13]` "Vision positioning and obstacle sensing... normally checks
out what's underneath the drone so that it stops at a certain point... if you disable that, the
drone can literally crash down to the ground and bounce off the ground. So I wouldn't recommend
it in most scenarios."

## Control and camera menus (16:14–24:23)

Focus-track radius/height parameters for active track 360 are adjustable per subject type:
`[16:14]` "we can change the inner radius, we can change the outer radius, so we can change that
all the way up to 20 m... I think the lowest is 4 m." Gimbal/Expo tuning is named as the setting
formerly confused for something else: `[17:15]` "this is actually where you go in to change a lot
of the gimbal settings... cine, normal, [and] sport" each with distinct max speed and brake
sensitivity, plus a smoothness parameter that trades responsiveness for a cinematic trail effect:
`[18:16]` "if I put that right up... it's going to slowly trail to make it look a little bit more
cinematic. Now if I pull that right down, you'll see it's more jarring, it's more jumpy." FPV mode
is described as a visual style choice, not a different flight envelope: `[19:17]` "it gives you
more of that FPV effect while still having the control of a drone that's just going to hover and
fly as safely as possible." On the camera side, the presenter recommends enabling the
overexposure warning and grid lines/crosshair as standing defaults: `[23:22]` "it will just let
you know if things are overexposed"; `[23:22]` grid lines plus a center crosshair are "the one[s]
that I prefer having... enabled."

## Cruise control and button customization (20:18–21:19)

Cruise control is defined precisely: `[20:18]` "any input you put into the drone straight when
you press cruise control... it's just going to continue doing those two stick inputs that I put
in." The presenter frames it as worth assigning to a programmable button given how useful it is:
`[20:18]` calling it "really cool, which is a whole feature on its own."

## Transmission, about, and housekeeping menus (25:24–29:29)

`[25:24]` frequency band: "by default it's on dual band... you can then swap into 5.8 GHz... [or]
2.4 GHz to see what the interference is like in the area," recommended only as a diagnostic step
when range is poor, not a default change. `[27:26]` firmware: "always do a test flight after
doing these updates." `[27:26]` full reset before selling: "make sure that you unlink your
account from the drone and then... reset all the settings and... clear all the data." `[29:29]`
app cache: aircraft flight records and local logs "can start to fill up" and should be cleared
periodically.

## Warnings and pilot signals addressed

- **Obstacle avoidance set to "off" will not stop the aircraft**: `[11:09]` explicitly, "it
  wouldn't notice the tree and it would hit the tree."
- **Disabling vision positioning removes the ground-proximity stop**: `[15:13]` "the drone can
  literally crash down to the ground and bounce off the ground," recommended only for
  experienced pilots in open areas at low altitude.
- **A max-altitude increase attempt is gated by a legal warning**: `[11:09]` "if you do try to
  increase it, it comes up with a warning letting you know that it is a breach of laws and
  regulations."
- **Battery percentage readout carries three named thresholds, not just a percentage**:
  `[09:07]` at 34% the display showed "6 minutes [to] return to home... 8 minutes [to] force a
  landing... 10 minutes 55 [to] completely depleted... that's the danger zone you don't want to
  get anywhere near."
- **A firmware update should be followed by a test flight before normal use**: `[27:26]` "always
  do a test flight after doing these updates."

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Bypass / bypass-nifty (obstacle avoidance)** — bypass routes around a detected obstacle
  rather than stopping; the "nifty" variant is a more aggressive bypass behavior flagged with its
  own in-app warning `[11:09]`.
- **Cruise control** — holds whatever stick inputs were active at the moment the assigned button
  is pressed, sustaining that motion hands-off `[20:18]`.
- **Gain and Expo tuning** — the presenter's clarification that this menu (previously named
  differently) is the gimbal-speed and control-response tuning page, not a separate feature
  `[17:15]`.

## Source-stated confidence

Every recommendation here is explicitly the presenter's own preference among available options,
not a manufacturer-mandated setting, and the video repeatedly marks that distinction:

- **Obstacle-avoidance mode is stated as personal preference among three named options**:
  `[11:09]` "bypass, which is my favorite one... this one is the one I use all the time purely
  because it gives a dynamic kind of viewing perspective."
- **RTH altitude is given as a recommended range, not a single required value**: `[12:11]`
  "anywhere from 80 m to 100 m."
- **The low-altitude/vision-positioning override is scoped explicitly to experienced pilots in
  controlled conditions**: `[15:13]` "if you need to fly really low and you are an experienced
  pilot and there's no one around."
