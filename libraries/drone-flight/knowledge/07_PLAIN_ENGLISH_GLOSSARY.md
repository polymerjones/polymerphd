# 07 — Plain English Glossary

Every technical term introduced across this knowledge package, defined the way the source
material defines it. Terms are alphabetical, and no citations appear here — see the individual
notes in `notes/` for the `[mm:ss]` anchor each term traces back to.

---

## A

**AE lock (auto-exposure lock)** — a controller-button assignment that freezes the camera's
automatic exposure reading at whatever it was when the button was pressed, so exposure doesn't
drift as the drone points at brighter or darker parts of a scene during a shot.

## B

**Bypass (obstacle avoidance mode)** — an obstacle-avoidance setting where the aircraft routes
around a detected obstacle and continues toward its original destination, rather than stopping.
A more aggressive variant, sometimes labeled "nifty," bypasses obstacles with faster, more dynamic
movement and carries its own in-app risk warning.

## C

**Cruise control** — a controller-button assignment that, once pressed, sustains whatever stick
inputs (direction, altitude change) were active at that moment, so the aircraft continues that
same motion hands-off until cancelled.

## D

**D-Log M** — a flat, low-contrast camera color profile intended to preserve detail for color
grading in video-editing software, in contrast to the default "normal" profile that looks correct
straight out of the camera.

## G

**Gain and Expo tuning** — the settings page (sometimes confused for a differently-named menu)
that controls gimbal movement speed, brake sensitivity, and stick-response curves, separately
configurable for each flight mode (cine, normal, sport).

## M

**Master Shots** — an automated shot mode that generates a short sequence of varied camera
movements around one selected subject, built from parameters the pilot sets (movement width,
distance from subject, start position).

## O

**Optimal (return-to-home routing)** — a return-to-home mode where the aircraft calculates and
flies the most efficient path back to the pilot, rather than retracing its outbound flight path
or using a fixed preset route.

## Q

**Quick Shots** — a set of six pre-programmed automated camera movements available from a single
subject selection: Dronie (pulls up and away), Rocket (climbs straight up), Circle (orbits at a
fixed radius), Helix (spirals outward while ascending), Boomerang (an elliptical out-and-back
path), and Asteroid (ascends while capturing a photo series that gets stitched into a tiny-planet
sphere).
