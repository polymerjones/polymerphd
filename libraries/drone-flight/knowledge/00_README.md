**Polymer Ph.D. — Drone Flight knowledge package**

This folder contains a synthesised knowledge base built from **3 YouTube transcripts** from the
channel **[DansTube.TV](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCa10m04VDy8yJghT3eJET9Q)**, all covering
setup, settings, and flight technique for the DJI Air 3S.

# What this is

**This package synthesises three videos from one YouTube channel about one drone.** That is its
scope and its whole content today. Every claim in it traces back to a specific video, at a
specific timestamp.

**Nothing in this package originates outside the transcripts.**

# What the package covers

- **Menu-by-menu settings walkthroughs for the DJI Air 3S** — flight checklist limits, obstacle
  avoidance modes, return-to-home behavior, camera exposure and color profile, gimbal tuning.
- **Physical setup** — fitting propellers and ND filters, binding the controller to the aircraft,
  choosing an ND filter by a shown stop calculation.
- **Automated flight/camera features** — active track, focus track, waypoints, Quick Shots,
  Master Shots, Hyperlapse, and Panorama modes, each demonstrated in the field.

# What the package does not cover

- **Other drone models.** Every source here is specific to the DJI Air 3S; nothing has been
  generalized to other aircraft.
- **Regulatory/legal flight rules** beyond what the sources themselves mention in passing (e.g. a
  400 ft altitude reference). This is not a substitute for reading your own jurisdiction's
  drone regulations.
- **A single channel's point of view, presented as if it were the only one.** All three sources
  are from the same creator; no other channel's technique has been cross-checked against it yet.
