# 00 — README

This package synthesizes {videos} sources on character, relationships, and everyday life advice —
drawn from two very different kinds of channel, which this synthesis treats as distinct rather than
blending into one voice.

## What this collection actually is

**Two clusters with very different evidentiary footing sit side by side in this library:**

1. **Named-researcher content** (a minority of notes) — relationship science from John and Julie
   Gottman, attachment and dating philosophy from Alain de Botton, developmental psychology from
   Lisa Damour and Becky Kennedy, moral/social psychology from Paul Bloom, trauma research from
   Rachel Yehuda, friendship research from Robert Waldinger, and communication frameworks from
   Charles Duhigg and Vanessa Van Edwards. These cite real research, name real studies, and are
   attributable to specific credentialed people.
2. **"Shi Heng Yi Wisdom" / Inspire Forward content** (the majority of notes by count) — third-
   person motivational narration nominally attributed to a Shaolin monk, covering solitude,
   boundaries, character, composure, and self-development. This cluster is, by its own individual
   notes' documented findings, thinly and inconsistently sourced: the name behind it is spelled at
   least three different ways across notes ("Shi Heng Yi," "Shiheni," "Shihangi"), at least two
   pairs of videos share duplicate on-screen titles with unrelated content, at least two videos
   share near-verbatim scripted templates with the topic swapped, and several videos borrow
   neuroscience or physics vocabulary ("quantum field," "heart-brain coherence," "the collective
   unconscious") to back claims that have no named study behind them.

This distinction matters for how confidently anything in this library should be relayed — see
`06_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md` for the full accounting, and `05_CROSS_SOURCE_COMPARISON.md` for where the
two clusters converge on similar themes despite the gap in evidentiary rigor.

## How this package is organized

- **01_CORE_PRINCIPLES.md** — patterns that recur across both clusters, independently arrived at.
- **02_RELATIONSHIPS_AND_ATTACHMENT.md** — partnership dynamics, dating, attachment, and how to
  handle someone returning after rejection or estrangement.
- **03_CHARACTER_TRUST_AND_EMOTIONAL_REGULATION.md** — how character and trustworthiness are
  revealed, boundary-setting, composure under provocation, and reading/being read by other people.
- **04_SELF_DEVELOPMENT_AND_GROWTH_CLAIMS.md** — productivity, self-talk, inner-work practices, and
  a set of more idiosyncratic claims (birth-month psychology, a vitamin-D muscle claim) that warrant
  more scrutiny than this file's other content.
- **05_CROSS_SOURCE_COMPARISON.md** — where the two clusters converge, and documented cases of
  template reuse or duplicate content within the Shi Heng Yi Wisdom cluster.
- **06_OPEN_QUESTIONS.md** — the specific sourcing weaknesses, unverifiable claims, and internal
  inconsistencies this library's notes have already flagged.
- **07_PLAIN_ENGLISH_GLOSSARY.md** — every named term, alphabetically, no citations.
- **08_QUESTIONS_AND_ANSWERS.md** — worked example answers (not yet populated).
- **09_SOURCE_CATALOG.md** — every source, auto-generated.

Per this project's provenance rule, files 01–08 carry no video-ID citations — only the individual
notes in `notes/` carry those.
