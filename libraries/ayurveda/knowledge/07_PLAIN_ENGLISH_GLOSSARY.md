# 07 — Plain English Glossary

Every technical term introduced across this knowledge package, defined the way the source
material defines it. Terms are alphabetical. Where a source hedges its own claim about a term,
that hedge is kept rather than smoothed away.

---

## A

**Ayurveda** — the traditional Indian system of medicine this library covers; treats a person as a
pattern across many data points (mind, body, digestion, skin, etc.) rather than dividing the body
into specialized, isolated systems the way Western medicine does.

## C

**Charaka Samhita** — a classical Ayurvedic text, cited directly in this library's first source for
a page comparing different kinds of milk and their dosha-specific uses.

**Cognitive fingerprint** — one source's own coinage for an individual's unique cognitive makeup,
used to argue that generic advice ("be more disciplined") only suits some constitutional types.

## D

**Dosha** — one of three constitutional categories (Vata, Pitta, Kapha) used to classify mind,
body type, and disease tendency. Most people carry a mix of all three rather than falling cleanly
into one.

## P

**Prakruti** — a person's genetic baseline mix of the three doshas — described by one source as
"the stats you are created with."
