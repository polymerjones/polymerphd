## 1. Dupuytren's Contracture

- **Source ID:** `assh-dupuytrens-contracture`
- **URL:** https://www.assh.org/handcare/condition/dupuytrens-contracture
- **Author:** American Society for Surgery of the Hand
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/assh-dupuytrens-contracture.txt`
- **Main subjects:** what Dupuytren's contracture is and how it forms, who is affected and the known risk factors, how the disease is noticed, evaluated, and staged, nonsurgical treatment options and their recurrence rates, surgical treatment options and recovery
- **Anatomy:** palmar fascia, cords, nodules, ring finger, little finger, knuckles, soles of the feet, genital tissue
- **Management options:** observation for mild cases, splinting or stretching (not preventive, but safe), needle fasciotomy (needle aponeurotomy), collagenase injection, surgical fasciotomy, surgical fasciectomy, post-treatment splinting and hand therapy
- **Important concepts:** fascia thickening into non-contractile cord tissue, Dupuytren's diathesis, the tabletop test, recurrence versus cure
