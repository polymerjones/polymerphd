## 1. Dupuytren's Contracture

- **Source ID:** `assh-dupuytrens-contracture`
- **URL:** https://www.assh.org/handcare/condition/dupuytrens-contracture
- **Author:** American Society for Surgery of the Hand
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/assh-dupuytrens-contracture.txt`
- **Main subjects:** what Dupuytren's contracture is and how it forms, who is affected and the known risk factors, how the disease is noticed, evaluated, and staged, nonsurgical treatment options and their recurrence rates, surgical treatment options and recovery
- **Anatomy:** palmar fascia, cords, nodules, ring finger, little finger, knuckles, soles of the feet, genital tissue
- **Management options:** observation for mild cases, splinting or stretching (not preventive, but safe), needle fasciotomy (needle aponeurotomy), collagenase injection, surgical fasciotomy, surgical fasciectomy, post-treatment splinting and hand therapy
- **Important concepts:** fascia thickening into non-contractile cord tissue, Dupuytren's diathesis, the tabletop test, recurrence versus cure

## 2. Dupuytren's disease: a localised and accessible human fibrotic disorder.

- **Source ID:** `layton-2023-fibrotic-disorder-review`
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36566101/
- **Author:** Layton TB(1), Williams L(1), Nanchahal J(2).
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/layton-2023-fibrotic-disorder-review.txt`
- **Main subjects:** what makes Dupuytren's disease unusual among fibrotic disorders, the cellular composition of an early-stage nodule, a specific drug target (TNF) with an early clinical signal
- **Anatomy:** none recorded
- **Management options:** local TNF inhibition (described as producing softening and reduced nodule size)
- **Important concepts:** genetic, environmental, and epigenetic interplay in disease development, the stromal-immune cell interaction inside a nodule, Dupuytren's as an accessible model for less-accessible fibrotic diseases elsewhere in the body

## 3. Dupuytren's Disease: Hand Surgery Pathway

- **Source ID:** `girft-bssh-dupuytrens-pathway`
- **URL:** https://gettingitrightfirsttime.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/2023-12-11_Hand-surgery_Pathway_Dupuytrens.drawio.pdf
- **Author:** GIRFT / British Society for Surgery of the Hand
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/girft-bssh-dupuytrens-pathway.txt`
- **Main subjects:** the UK NHS clinical pathway from primary-care presentation through surgical recovery, the specific contracture thresholds that trigger a surgical referral, what happens when a patient does not meet the surgical threshold, the post-operative recovery and follow-up timeline
- **Anatomy:** MCPJ (metacarpophalangeal joint), PIPJ (proximal interphalangeal joint), thumb and index finger webspace, dorsal PIPJs, soles of feet, penile tissue
- **Management options:** clinical history and examination in primary care, cortisone injection for painful nodules, referral to a community MSK service, percutaneous fasciotomy in outpatients, daycase surgery, radiotherapy (flagged for local audit or research only), post-operative splinting, dressing changes, and hand therapy
- **Important concepts:** the 30°/20° flexion-contracture threshold for surgery, rapidly progressive disease as its own referral trigger, Patient Initiated Follow-up (PIFU)

## 4. Effectiveness and Safety of Dupuytren Contracture Treatments: A Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis Using the GRADE Approach.

- **Source ID:** `shaheen-2025-treatment-comparison-review`
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39451139/
- **Author:** Shaheen MS(1)(2), Karjalainen VL(3), Reddy A(2)(4), Karjalainen T(5), Chung KC(2).
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/shaheen-2025-treatment-comparison-review.txt`
- **Main subjects:** a head-to-head comparison of the three main treatments' contracture reduction, which treatment recurs least, and which is safest
- **Anatomy:** none recorded
- **Management options:** percutaneous needle fasciotomy (PNF), collagenase clostridium histolyticum (CCH), limited fasciectomy (LF)
- **Important concepts:** the GRADE approach to certainty of evidence, residual contracture as an effectiveness measure, distinct from recurrence, the effectiveness/durability/safety tradeoff across all three treatments

## 5. Likelihood of Dupuytren Contracture Recurrence After Limited Fasciectomy, Needle Aponeurotomy or Collagenase Clostridium histolyticum: Systematic Review of Prospective Data With 2- to 7-Year Follow-up.

- **Source ID:** `nocek-2026-recurrence-systematic-review`
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41853763/
- **Author:** Nocek M(1), Wakefield D(2), Vitale MA(2)(3).
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/nocek-2026-recurrence-systematic-review.txt`
- **Main subjects:** hard recurrence percentages for each of the three main treatments, whether the joint treated (MCP vs PIP) changes the odds of recurrence, how often patients need a repeat procedure after each treatment
- **Anatomy:** metacarpophalangeal (MCP) joint, proximal interphalangeal (PIP) joint
- **Management options:** limited fasciectomy (LF), collagenase Clostridium histolyticum (CCH), needle aponeurotomy (NA)
- **Important concepts:** prospective, in-person follow-up as an inclusion requirement, recurrence defined explicitly, not left to each study's own wording, reintervention rate as a downstream consequence of recurrence

## 6. Needle Fasciotomy for Dupuytren's Contracture

- **Source ID:** `nice-htg21-needle-fasciotomy`
- **URL:** https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/htg21/chapter/1-Recommendations
- **Author:** NICE
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/nice-htg21-needle-fasciotomy.txt`
- **Main subjects:** NICE's overall verdict on needle fasciotomy's safety and efficacy, what needle fasciotomy actually involves as a procedure, the procedure's recurrence rate and who benefits most, complications reported by specialist advisers
- **Anatomy:** palm, fingers, metacarpophalangeal joint
- **Management options:** needle fasciotomy (needle aponeurotomy), non-surgical options with data described as lacking (vitamin E cream, ultrasonic therapy)
- **Important concepts:** dividing a fibrous cord with a blade or needle bevel rather than removing it, patient selection by age and surgical suitability, recurrence as a near-universal long-term outcome across treatments

## 7. Progression of Dupuytren Contracture: A Randomized Controlled Trial Comparing Surgery, Needle Fasciotomy, and Collagenase Injection.

- **Source ID:** `detect-2026-progression-rct`
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41334972/
- **Author:** Karjalainen VL(1), Soikkeli J(2), Räisänen MP(1)(3), Leppänen OV(4), Reito A(5), Stjernberg-Salmela S(6), Buchbinder R(7), Karjalainen T(4)(7).
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/detect-2026-progression-rct.txt`
- **Main subjects:** a randomized head-to-head trial of surgery, needle fasciotomy, and collagenase, what predicts progression or the need for retreatment, whether treating one finger changes the odds for other, untreated fingers
- **Anatomy:** proximal interphalangeal (PIP) joint, little finger
- **Management options:** surgery, needle fasciotomy, collagenase injection
- **Important concepts:** progression defined as increased contracture angle from 3 months to 2 years, the DETECT trial's finger-level analysis design, {'risk factors': 'age, smoking, joint, and digit involved'}

## 8. Radiation Therapy for Early Dupuytren's Disease

- **Source ID:** `nice-htg427-radiotherapy`
- **URL:** https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/htg427/chapter/1-Recommendations
- **Author:** NICE
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/nice-htg427-radiotherapy.txt`
- **Main subjects:** NICE's overall verdict on radiotherapy for early-stage disease, what "early disease" means clinically, efficacy data across four separate patient cohorts, the safety/toxicity profile, acute and chronic
- **Anatomy:** palmar fascia, fourth finger, fifth finger
- **Management options:** radiation therapy (21, 30, or 32 Gy) for early Dupuytren's disease
- **Important concepts:** special arrangements for governance, consent, and audit or research, regression versus stability versus progression as the three outcome buckets, acute versus chronic toxicity, re-irradiation of a site that progresses in-field

## 10. The Likelihood of Future Dupuytren Disease Intervention After Initial Treatment in the Same Digit, Another Digit, and Contralateral Hand.

- **Source ID:** `future-intervention-risk-cohort-2026`
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42274446/
- **Author:** El Bachaoui R(1), Cossu E(2), Zhang D(2), Earp BE(2), Blazar PE(3).
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/future-intervention-risk-cohort-2026.txt`
- **Main subjects:** the odds of needing more treatment somewhere else after an initial procedure, how those odds split between the same digit, another digit, and the other hand, which patient factors predict spreading to a new location versus recurring in place
- **Anatomy:** same digit as initially treated, other digits of the same hand, contralateral hand
- **Management options:** collagenase treatment, surgical fasciectomy
- **Important concepts:** future intervention as a single umbrella outcome spanning three different locations, retrospective cohort design at a single institution

## 11. The Molecular Pathogenesis of Dupuytren Disease: Review of the Literature and Suggested New Approaches to Treatment.

- **Source ID:** `sayadi-2019-molecular-pathogenesis-review`
- **URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31232804/
- **Author:** Sayadi LR(1), Alhunayan D(1), Sarantopoulos N(1), Kong C(1), Condamoor S(1), Sayadi J(2), Banyard DA(1), Shaterian A(1), Leis A(1), Evans GRD(1), Widgerow AD(1).
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/sayadi-2019-molecular-pathogenesis-review.txt`
- **Main subjects:** a systematic review of the basic-science literature on Dupuytren's, organized into three categories, how many studies were screened versus actually included
- **Anatomy:** none recorded
- **Management options:** future biologic treatments aimed at the disease process itself, not yet named specifically
- **Important concepts:** the proliferative, involutional, and residual disease stages, three organizing categories -- molecular alterations, structural alterations, genetic predisposition, PRISMA-guided systematic review methodology applied to basic science literature
