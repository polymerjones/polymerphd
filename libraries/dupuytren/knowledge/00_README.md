# Dupuytren — Knowledge Base Overview

This package synthesizes 145 sources on Dupuytren's contracture: recorded conference talks (the
2010 Miami Dupuytren Symposium and the 2015 Dupuytren Symposium make up the bulk of the corpus),
standalone lectures and campaign videos from the Dupuytren Foundation, peer-reviewed systematic
reviews and journal articles, and a small number of patient-generated sources (Reddit and YouTube
comment threads).

**This is a dense, clinical, conference-proceedings corpus, not a patient-education pamphlet.**
Most sources are surgeons, hand therapists, and basic scientists talking to each other — the
material assumes familiarity with hand anatomy and clinical trial methodology, and the notes
preserve that register rather than simplifying it. Where a patient-voice source appears (the
Reddit thread, the YouTube comments), it is flagged as such and read differently: as anecdote and
lived experience, not as a controlled finding.

## What's in each file

- **`01_CORE_PRINCIPLES.md`** — the handful of ideas that recur across nearly every talk in this
  collection, regardless of which specific treatment or mechanism the speaker is presenting.
- **`02_CELL_AND_MOLECULAR_BIOLOGY.md`** — the myofibroblast-centered biology of the disease: what
  drives contraction, what drives spread, and the signaling pathways (TGF-beta, Wnt/beta-catenin,
  TNF, MMP/TIMP) researchers are targeting.
- **`03_CLINICAL_COURSE_AND_DIAGNOSIS.md`** — how the disease actually behaves over time and across
  populations: natural history, staging, epidemiology, associated conditions, and the long-standing
  absence of a diagnostic or predictive biomarker.
- **`04_TREATMENT_MODALITIES.md`** — the major interventions (needle fasciotomy, collagenase, open
  fasciectomy and its variants, radiotherapy, splinting) and the evidence behind each.
- **`05_RECURRENCE_AND_OUTCOME_MEASUREMENT.md`** — why "did it come back" is a far harder question
  than it sounds, and how that measurement problem distorts comparisons between treatments.
- **`06_PATIENT_EXPERIENCE_AND_PROMS.md`** — what patients actually report wanting, how that differs
  from what a surgeon can measure, and the tools (and their limits) built to capture it.
- **`07_PLAIN_ENGLISH_GLOSSARY.md`** — every technical term introduced across the collection,
  defined in plain English, alphabetical, no citations.
- **`08_QUESTIONS_AND_ANSWERS.md`** — worked example answers in this package's house style. Not yet
  populated for this library (see that file).
- **`09_SOURCE_CATALOG.md`** — auto-generated index of every source; do not hand-edit.

## A note on scope

This collection is overwhelmingly hand-surgery and hand-surgery-adjacent in its source material.
It has essentially nothing on primary care recognition, physical therapy as a sole non-surgical
management path, or long-term disability accommodation — a reader looking for those angles would
not be well served by this collection alone.
