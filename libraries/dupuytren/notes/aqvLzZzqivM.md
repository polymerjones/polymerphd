# Dupuytren Research Group: "Dupuytren Research 2022" Fundraising Bumper

## Central claim

**This 22-second fundraising bumper restates the Dupuytren Research Group's core message in its most
condensed form: no cure exists yet, but the blood test the group is developing is described as critical to
finding one, and donor support is directly requested to fund that work.** It states this plainly: **"there's
no cure, but there is hope. The Dupuytren Research Group is working to develop the blood test critical to
finding a cure. We need your financial support — please give generously for a future without Dupuytren's
disease"** `[00:00]`.
