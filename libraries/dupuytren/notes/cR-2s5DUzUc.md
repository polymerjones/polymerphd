# Dupuytren Foundation: "Using a Finger Goniometer" Demonstration

## Central claim

**This brief demonstration shows the correct technique for measuring a finger joint's angle with a
goniometer: locate the bone on the back of the finger on each side of the joint, place one arm of the
goniometer directly on the bone on each side, with the hinge centered exactly on the joint line.** The
presenter narrates the technique directly while performing it: **"you just find the areas where you can feel
the bone on the back of the finger ray, and you put one side of the goniometer right on the bone on one side
of the joint... and there's your angle measure"** `[00:00]`.

## Technique

`[00:00]` — For each joint measured, the same method is repeated: locate the bone by feel on the back of the
finger on both sides of the target joint, place a goniometer arm flush against the bone on each side, and
center the instrument's hinge precisely on the joint line itself — the resulting reading is the joint angle.
The presenter demonstrates this moving further along the finger, repeating the identical placement technique
at each subsequent joint.
