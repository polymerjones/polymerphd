# The Race to 240 | Shoulder Day

## Central claim

**Seth demonstrates a specific mid-set technique on side laterals — letting the arms hang and
doing a few small partial pulls partway through a set before finishing the remaining reps — and
gives the most granular description in this library yet of exactly where a bent-over rear delt
raise should stop before the traps and upper back take over.** `[08:53]` "Halfway through my set...
I let them hang, pull, pulls — just pulls — and I can feel it fill up with blood, so then the next
rep I can feel [a] better contraction... I think partials are important because I think they help
create that roundness in the insertions."

## Mid-set partial-rep refill technique (08:53–09:55)

The sequence: work partway through a set of side laterals, let the dumbbells hang, perform several
small partial pulls (not full reps), then resume the working set. Seth states the purpose directly:
`[08:53]` refilling blood into the muscle mid-set to improve the contraction quality of subsequent
reps, rather than simply resting.

## Rear delt raise: a precise stopping point (15:20–19:26)

Seth restates and sharpens the short-range-of-motion technique from his dedicated rear-delt video,
this time describing the exact cutoff: `[17:23]` "the shorter range of motion that I have, the more
I'm able to just focus on the rear delt... you want to go to the point whenever you are right before
you engage your traps going back, and that usually is right here." He explicitly names a heavier
weight as necessary for this specific version of the exercise: `[18:24]` "I like using a heavier
weight, simply because I'm able to get a better pull on it... with a lighter weight I can straight-
arm it and go all the way out... which is going to hit your rear delts, but not the same, in my
opinion." He categorizes the exercise directly: `[18:24]` "this is like the muscle builder of my
rear delt exercise... I'm trying to put a little bit of size and fullness on my rear delts," distinct
from a lighter, longer-range "concentration" version.

## The same principle applied to the pec deck reverse fly (19:26)

Tai extends the identical range-of-motion cutoff to a different exercise: `[19:26]` "speaking of the
pec deck, you see people go way too far back... I don't want to come past here, because as soon as
you come past here, that's when your traps and [rhomboids] take over." Seth affirms this is the
same underlying principle applied to different equipment.

## Form cues and body signals addressed

- **A mid-set partial-rep "refill" is used to restore contraction quality before finishing a set**,
  not just as a rest pause: `[08:53]`.
- **The rear delt raise has a named, precise stopping point** — just before the traps/upper back
  engage — rather than a vague "keep it short" instruction: `[17:23]`.
- **Heavier weight on this specific rear delt variant is stated as necessary for the intended
  "muscle builder" effect**, not incidental: `[18:24]`.
- **The same range-of-motion cutoff principle is explicitly generalized to another exercise** (pec
  deck reverse fly), reinforcing it as a transferable rule rather than one exercise's quirk:
  `[19:26]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Muscle builder (rear delt)** — Seth's category for a heavier, short-range bent-over rear delt
  raise aimed at adding size/fullness, distinct from a lighter, more strictly isolated
  "concentration" version of the same movement `[18:24]`.

## Source-stated confidence

Both the mid-set partial technique and the rear-delt stopping point are stated as personally
verified through repeated self-observation rather than external instruction:

- **The rear delt technique is explicitly checked against self-honesty**: `[19:26]`–`[20:28]` "you
  can't lie to yourself... is this actually working, do I feel it, am I noticing growth from doing
  it this way, and for me my answer has been yes."
- **The exercise categorization (muscle builder vs. concentration) is presented as Seth's own
  mental framework**, "thinking out loud" rather than an established taxonomy: `[18:24]`.
