# RUSHFIT Intermediate Training Calendar, Weeks 1-8

*Note on this note: like the Beginner calendar, this PDF's schedule-grid pages extracted as
unreadable scrambled text (a table-layout extraction failure, not a font/ligature issue). This
note's day-by-day description comes from directly viewing the rendered calendar pages during this
session. Only pages 2 and 4 carry any extracted text (pages 1 and 3 — the cover and the
heart-rate-zone chart — extracted as empty and have no page anchor in the clean file).*

## Central claim

**Intermediate uses the same seven DVDs, the same equipment, and the same cardio-dosage increase
at the midpoint as `rushfit-beginner-training-calendar`, but removes both extra rest days and
introduces Explosive Power Training earlier and more often — the calendar's only real
differences from Beginner are frequency and rest, not new content.** `[p.2]` Explosive Power
Training first appears at day 25, four weeks earlier than in the Beginner calendar (day 46), and
`[p.4]` recurs on days 30, 36, 44, and 51 — five appearances in this calendar versus two in
Beginner's.

## Structure

- **Rest days**: exactly days 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, and 56 — eight rest days total, a
  strictly weekly cadence with none of the extra mid-week rest days (11, 31) that
  `rushfit-beginner-training-calendar` includes `[p.2]`, `[p.4]`.
- **Full Body Strength and Conditioning** appears earlier too, first at day 6 `[p.2]` (versus
  day 20 in Beginner).
- **Cardio dosage increases at the midpoint on the same schedule as Beginner**: 30 minutes at
  Zone 3 for weeks 1-4 `[p.2]`, rising to 40+ minutes for weeks 5-8 `[p.4]`, with the identical
  stated ceiling: "You should feel refreshed, not exhausted like a workout."
- **The same standing reminder** to review Foundation Moves before each workout precedes the
  grid `[p.2]`.

## Form cues and body signals addressed

- **The cardio intensity ceiling is stated identically to the Beginner calendar**: `[p.4]` "You
  should feel refreshed, not exhausted like a workout" — the harder tier does not raise this
  ceiling, only the DVD frequency around it.

## Glossary terms introduced

No new terms — this document reuses workout names and the Zone 1-5 heart rate system already
established in `rushfit-workout-guide`.

## Analogies worth reusing

None — this is a schedule grid with operational instructions, not narrative or explanatory prose.

## Source-stated confidence

- **The calendar routes readers to the RUSHFIT Assessment before starting**, identically to
  `rushfit-beginner-training-calendar`: `[p.4]` "Take the RUSHFIT assessment... see page 13 of
  the workout guide to fill in your assessment."

## Conflicts with other sources

**Sits between `rushfit-beginner-training-calendar` and `rushfit-advanced-training-calendar`** in
this program's own three-tier progression. Confirmed by direct comparison: Intermediate keeps
Beginner's single-workout-per-day structure (no `[AM]`/`[PM]` splits) while compressing the same
DVD rotation into fewer rest days and an earlier introduction of the harder DVDs — it does not
introduce same-day double sessions, which is what actually distinguishes
`rushfit-advanced-training-calendar` from both lower tiers.
