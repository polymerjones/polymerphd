# Bodybuilding Arm Workout - BACK AT IT!! | Seth Feroce

## Central claim

**Seth demonstrates that hand and elbow position on dip-machine pressing changes tricep-head
emphasis in the same way grip changes it on cable press-downs, and names his personal top three
"mass builder" tricep exercises while explicitly warning against stacking all three in one
session because of joint strain.** `[17:51]` "Whenever I'm over here, I like to lean into it...
I feel the stretch in my lateral head and in my long head." `[18:51]`–`[19:51]` "if I put my elbow
out, you can feel it more in the long head... hand placement is going to play a role, shoulder
placement, and then how you move your elbow with it will play a role in... what part of your
tricep [you hit]."

## Dip-machine hand/elbow position (17:51–19:51)

Demonstrated across single-arm variants: `[18:51]`–`[19:51]` "you can feel it in the lateral head...
and then if I put my elbow out, you can feel it more in the long head and back... you could do it
this way, or change your hand position, [and] push from your palm differently." He explicitly
frames this as generative rather than fixed: `[19:51]` "so don't limit yourself, you can come up
with new ideas."

## Three tricep "mass builders" and a joint-strain caution (11:42–12:43)

Seth names a specific ranked list rather than treating all tricep exercises as interchangeable:
`[11:42]` "close-grip bench, [the] overhead dumbbell extension, and... the dip machine — there are
three... staple exercises for building thick triceps, overall tricep development. I think those are
the top three, in my opinion." He then states a direct caution about combining them: `[11:42]`
"putting them all in the same workout might be a bit much on your joints — so again, train intense
and train hard, but also train smart."

## Pairing exercises by gym proximity (10:40–11:42)

A practical, non-physiological reason for exercise pairing is stated directly: `[10:40]` "I will
pair exercises that are close to each other, in proximity — so... rather than have to run halfway
across the gym to go from one set to another, I will choose exercises close in proximity." He notes
this matters more in a crowded public gym: `[10:40]` "if you are training at like a big gym that's
not private... pair your bicep exercise and try to go in the gym with a plan, but understand you
might have to manipulate it a little bit mid-workout."

## Matching high-calorie meals to demanding training days (14:44–15:45)

Seth states a specific rule for when to eat his highest-calorie meals: `[15:45]` "if you're eating
those type of meals, you got to eat them on the days that are necessary to do so — big days, back
days, leg days, chest days... you get a nasty pump — keep that in mind when you're choosing your
food."

## Form cues and body signals addressed

- **Elbow position on dip-machine pressing is a stated lever between lateral-head and long-head
  tricep emphasis**, consistent with the same logic applied to cable press-downs elsewhere in this
  library: `[18:51]`–`[19:51]`.
- **Combining all three of his named top tricep exercises in one session is explicitly flagged as
  excessive joint strain**, not recommended by default: `[11:42]`.
- **High-calorie meals are matched to specific high-output training days**, not eaten
  indiscriminately: `[15:45]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **French curl** — Seth's term for the overhead single-dumbbell tricep extension, held with two
  hands `[11:42]`.
- **Thousand-calorie meal** — his term for a deliberately calorie-dense meal (e.g., meatloaf and
  potatoes, or a loaded breakfast sandwich) reserved for demanding training days `[12:43]`–`[14:44]`.

## Source-stated confidence

The "top three" tricep exercise ranking and the joint-strain caution are both stated as Seth's own
opinion and experience rather than a cited biomechanical claim:

- **The tricep exercise ranking is explicitly labeled as opinion**: `[11:42]` "I think those are the
  top three, in my opinion."
- **The joint-strain warning is stated as a general caution, scaled to the lifter's own
  pharmacological support**, an unusual explicit qualifier: `[11:42]` "if you're not taking [some
  support], because... your body can take way more abuse [if you are] than someone who's not, or
  [is] taking a lighter dose."

## Conflicts with other sources

Against `eY2npBUVU4Y`, `buTwER0sEjE`, and `QSkmRguNamg` (elbow flare, grip rotation, and overhead-
extension hand position as tricep-head levers respectively), this note adds dip-machine elbow/hand

shifting tricep-head emphasis across many different exercises.
