# Story Time - My WORST CYCLES EVER

## Central claim

**Derek attributes his worst physical outcomes to a specific, nameable dosing error from his early
years — stacking additional compounds on top of a fixed 500 mg/week testosterone "floor" that
forum culture treated as non-negotiable, rather than adjusting the base dose down as other
compounds were added.** `[01:02]` "you would be on your 500 [milligram] tests and then anything
you stack on top goes on top of 500 tests... there's no lowering your test base if you're adding
in estrogenic compounds on top." He states the outcome of one such stack (test + Dianabol) plainly:
`[04:06]` "the outcome of that was the highest blood pressure I've ever had in my life... I would
literally be sitting in lecture and then just spontaneously have a nosebleed."

## Story one: test + Dianabol stack, symptoms and stated cause (03:05–07:09)

Derek names the specific compounds and estimated doses (500 mg/week testosterone, 30–40 mg/day
Dianabol) and the reasoning error behind the dose: `[04:06]` "instead of thinking minimum effective
dose I was thinking like maximum tolerable dose on my first cycle." He lists concrete stated
symptoms: elevated blood pressure ("maybe 160 over 120"), two to three nosebleeds per day,
exercise-triggered "back pumps" severe enough to force him to drop specific lifts from his program
(`[05:08]`), water retention he describes as feeling "waterlogged," and situational erectile
dysfunction requiring high-dose PDE5 inhibitors (`[07:09]`). He attributes the cluster of symptoms
to a specific stated mechanism: `[06:08]` "you're using two testosterone bases essentially, and
dosages exceeding what I needed even to fulfill that physiologic function to begin with — you're
just leading to a overabundance of [estrogen]... you're way too estrogenically burdened." He
separately names diet as a contributing factor: `[06:08]` "my diet was trash back then, I used to
do like [a] dirty bulk."

## Story two: a bad injection site was a mechanical accident, not a drug reaction (08:09–10:10)

Derek is explicit that this second story is categorically different from the others — a physical
injury, not a pharmacological adverse effect: `[08:09]` "it wasn't necessarily a compound
reaction, it was more so just like a freak accident... internal bleeding in my leg." He states the
resolution required a minor procedure: `[09:10]` "I actually had to cut a little slit in my leg to
drain all the blood out because it was just way too painful."

## Story three: high-dose test + trenbolone and psychological effects (10:10–14:13)

Derek describes escalating a cycle to roughly 750 mg/week testosterone plus 600 mg/week
trenbolone enanthate "for no reason," culminating in an unplanned large final dose: `[11:10]` "I
[pulled] out like two to three milliliters of test plus like two to three milliliters of tren...
just bombed it in like one giant shot." He states the psychological effect directly and
attributes it to dose, not to trenbolone categorically: `[12:11]` "my hair got decimated that
cycle and I was so hyper paranoid the relationship I was in at the time got [wrecked]... you
cannot possibly perceive what is going on in any sort of accurate way and manage emotional stress
properly whatsoever when you're on that high of a dose of trend." He explicitly scopes this as
individual-dependent rather than universal, citing a contrasting personal experience at a
different (leaner, more controlled) time: `[12:11]`–`[13:12]` "maybe like one in ten individuals
might tolerate trend like super well... I definitely wasn't one of those individuals," yet later
in the same cycle-type "the trend elevated my confidence rather than [made] me paranoid... because
my ducks were like all in a row at the time."

## A named training-and-drug interaction: "fluff" volume with no progressive overload (10:10–11:10)

Distinct from the dosing stories, Derek separately describes deliberately training with no
progression (fixed weight, volume-for-pump only) while on a high dose, modeled on what he
perceived some professional bodybuilders doing on camera: `[10:10]` "I'm just gonna do a giant
blast phase and just try and get a good pump and... not try and add any sort of poundages
whatsoever." He states this combination wasted the cycle's muscle-building potential: `[14:13]`
"I was just wasting the cycle."

## Form cues and body signals addressed

- **Repeated spontaneous nosebleeds and blood pressure in the 160/120 range are named as the
  overt warning signs of the test+Dianabol stack**, not something to push through: `[04:06]`.
- **"Dbol back pumps" are described as severe enough to require dropping specific exercises**
  (bent-over row, deadlift, squat) from a program entirely: `[05:08]`.
- **Streaking/redness around an injection site is distinguished from ordinary post-injection
  soreness** as the marker Derek uses (in the separate injury story) to judge infection risk,
  though in his case the swelling ultimately was not infection-related: `[08:09]`–`[09:10]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **"Deca dick"** — Derek's term for erectile dysfunction he attributes to running deca (nandrolone)
  stacked with test and Dianabol, mentioned but not clinically explained `[08:09]`.
- **"Fluff" workout / "fluff and puff"** — high-volume, no-progressive-overload training done
  purely for a pump rather than strength or size gain, Derek's own term `[10:10]`, `[14:13]`.

## Source-stated confidence

- **Derek explicitly frames trenbolone's psychological effect as individual-dependent rather
  than a fixed drug property**, contrasting his own paranoid response in one cycle against
  confidence-elevating effects he reports from a different, better-controlled period on a similar
  compound `[12:11]`–`[13:12]`.
- **The injection-site injury story is explicitly separated from the drug-reaction stories** —
  Derek states directly it "wasn't necessarily a compound reaction" `[08:09]`, preserving the
  distinction between a mechanical accident and a pharmacological adverse effect.
- **The entire video is framed as retrospective personal anecdote for a "learning opportunity,"**
  not a dosing guideline: `[17:18]` "our knowledge bank all starts at zero and we learn from
  scratch."
