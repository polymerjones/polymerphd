# RUSHFIT Beginner Training Calendar, Weeks 1-8

*Note on this note: this PDF's page-2 and page-4 schedule grids are laid out as dense tables with
background art, and pypdf's text extraction scrambled them into unreadable fragments — a
structural extraction failure, not a font/ligature issue like the other guides. This note's
day-by-day description comes from directly viewing the rendered calendar pages during this
session, not from the extracted text layer. Only pages 2 and 4 carry any extracted text at all
(pages 1 and 3 — the cover and the heart-rate-zone chart — extracted as empty and have no page
anchor in the clean file), so citations below are limited to those two.*

## Central claim

**The calendar sequences the program's own seven DVDs across 8 weeks with more built-in rest than
either harder tier, and deliberately holds back two DVDs — Full Body Strength and Conditioning,
and Explosive Power Training — until after the program's midpoint.** `[p.2]` Weeks 1-4 rotate
Strength and Endurance, Abdominal Strength and Core Conditioning, The Fight Conditioning Workout,
and cardio days paired with either the Balance and Agility or Stretch for Flexibility "Bonus
Workout," with Full Body Strength and Conditioning introduced only at day 20 and day 25.
`[p.4]` Explosive Power Training is introduced later still, first appearing at day 46, with a
second appearance at day 55, one day before the program ends.

## Structure

- **Rest days**: days 7, 11, 14, 21, 28, 31, 35, 42, 49, and 56 (the closing day, explicitly
  marked "REST and congratulate yourself! You are RUSHFIT!") `[p.2]`, `[p.4]` — ten rest days
  across the 8 weeks, two more than either `rushfit-intermediate-training-calendar` or
  `rushfit-advanced-training-calendar` schedules, both of which rest on a strictly weekly cadence
  (day 7, 14, 21... 56) with no extra rest days at 11 or 31.
- **Cardio-paired days** always combine a plain cardio session with either Balance and Agility or
  Stretch for Flexibility in the later weeks, though several early-calendar cardio days
  (days 2, 9, 16, 23, 37) run cardio alone with no DVD pairing `[p.2]`, `[p.4]`.
- **Cardio dosage increases at the midpoint**: `[p.2]` weeks 1-4 call for 30 minutes of cardio at
  medium intensity (Zone 3); `[p.4]` weeks 5-8 increase that to 40 minutes or more at the same
  zone, with an explicit ceiling: "You should feel refreshed, not exhausted like a workout."
- **A standing reminder precedes the grid**: `[p.2]` "Review the Foundation moves prior to
  beginning each workout to ensure familiarity with the exercises."

## Form cues and body signals addressed

- **The cardio intensity target is explicitly bounded on both ends** — not "as hard as possible,"
  but a specific felt state: `[p.4]` "You should feel refreshed, not exhausted like a workout."

## Glossary terms introduced

No new terms — this document reuses workout names and the Zone 1-5 heart rate system already

None — this is a schedule grid with operational instructions, not narrative or explanatory prose.

## Source-stated confidence

- **The calendar routes readers to the RUSHFIT Assessment before starting**, framing the 8-week
  plan as something to enter with a known baseline rather than assumed fitness: `[p.4]` "Take the
  RUSHFIT assessment... see page 13 of the workout guide to fill in your assessment."

## Conflicts with other sources

**Extends `rushfit-workout-guide` directly** — both the equipment line (2-25 lb dumbbells) and the
heart-rate-zone reference point back to that guide's own pages verbatim. **Sits alongside**
`rushfit-intermediate-training-calendar` and `rushfit-advanced-training-calendar` as the mildest
of the three progressions — see those notes for how the DVD rotation intensifies (more frequent
Explosive Power Training in Intermediate; same-day double sessions in Advanced) while starting
from this identical structure and rest-day cadence.
