# RUSHFIT Advanced Training Calendar, Weeks 1-8

*Note on this note: like the Beginner and Intermediate calendars, this PDF's schedule-grid pages
extracted as unreadable scrambled text (a table-layout extraction failure, not a font/ligature
issue). This note's day-by-day description comes from directly viewing the rendered calendar
pages during this session. Only pages 2 and 4 carry any extracted text (pages 1 and 3 — the cover
and the heart-rate-zone chart — extracted as empty and have no page anchor in the clean file).*

## Central claim

**Advanced is the only one of the three calendars to schedule two full workouts on the same day —
one AM, one PM — which is the actual structural feature distinguishing it from Intermediate, not
just added frequency.** `[p.2]` A standing instruction precedes the grid: "On days where there
are two workouts do one in the AM and one in the PM. On Cardio days you can choose to do them
back to back or split them AM/PM." Double-session days begin immediately in week 1 — day 3
pairs Abdominal Strength and Core Conditioning [AM] with Balance and Agility [PM], and day 5
pairs Full Body Strength and Conditioning [AM] with Balance and Agility [PM] `[p.2]` — and recur
regularly through week 8, including days combining two full workout DVDs rather than a workout
plus a cardio/bonus pairing (e.g. day 32: Explosive Power Training [AM] + Full Body Strength and
Conditioning [PM] `[p.4]`).

## Structure

- **Rest days**: exactly days 7, 14, 21, 28, 35, 42, 49, and 56 — the same strictly weekly
  cadence as `rushfit-intermediate-training-calendar`, with none of
  `rushfit-beginner-training-calendar`'s extra mid-week rest days `[p.2]`, `[p.4]`.
- **Equipment is unchanged from the other two tiers** despite the added session volume: `[p.2]`
  "Dumbbells 2lbs. to 25lbs." — the calendar scales frequency and session-doubling, not load.
- **Explosive Power Training** appears five times (days 20, 25, 32, 36, 44, 51, 55 — several as
  one half of a double session), a similar frequency to the Intermediate calendar but compressed
  into more same-day pairings `[p.2]`, `[p.4]`.
- **Cardio dosage increases at the midpoint on the same schedule as both other tiers**: 30
  minutes at Zone 3 for weeks 1-4, rising to 40+ minutes for weeks 5-8, with the identical stated
  ceiling: "You should feel refreshed, not exhausted like a workout" `[p.4]`.

## Form cues and body signals addressed

- **The AM/PM split itself is the guide's own accommodation for a harder volume**, not an
  arbitrary scheduling choice — it exists specifically to avoid stacking two full sessions
  back-to-back without separation `[p.2]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

No new terms — this document reuses workout names and the Zone 1-5 heart rate system already
established in `rushfit-workout-guide`.

## Analogies worth reusing

None — this is a schedule grid with operational instructions, not narrative or explanatory prose.

## Source-stated confidence

- **The calendar routes readers to the RUSHFIT Assessment before starting**, identically to both
  other tiers: `[p.4]` "Take the RUSHFIT assessment... see page 13 of the workout guide to fill in
  your assessment."

## Conflicts with other sources

**Extends both `rushfit-beginner-training-calendar` and `rushfit-intermediate-training-calendar`**
by adding same-day double sessions on top of the same seven-DVD rotation, same equipment, and same
midpoint cardio increase — confirmed by direct comparison across all three calendars as the one
real structural axis this program varies by difficulty tier (rest-day count for Beginner vs. the
other two; same-day session-doubling for Advanced vs. both lower tiers).
