# Plain-English Glossary

Terms introduced across this library's notes, defined in plain English. Alphabetical. No citations here — see the individual note for the source of each term.

**Anticipation of reward** — what dopamine actually signals: not pleasure itself, but the expectation of a reward, which is what drives seeking and checking behavior.

**Dorsal anterior cingulate cortex** — a brain region tied to social-exclusion pain and grief.

**Flow state** — full, pleasurable immersion in a task that's challenging enough to hold attention without being so hard it becomes aversive.

**Monkey mind** — a restless, constantly task-switching mental state, unable to settle on one thing — the opposite of flow.
