# Prednisone for life

## Central claim

**After 13 years on prednisone and a year spent carefully tapering down to 2mg, the conclusion of
this post — the most recent in the library — is that going fully off the drug isn't achievable for
her: it's a permanent, low-dose baseline rather than a temporary bridge.** Stated directly: **"It
turns out that not taking prednisone is not in the cards for me"** `[¶1]`, after **"having been
completely off for a month."**

## The taper, and the psychological reframe along the way

`[¶1]`

The taper had gone well by subjective measure: **"I don't experience side effects of which I am
aware, even at 5 mg... I began 2024 at 5 mg of prednisone and was down to 2 mg with stable
appointments."** Partway through, she reframes the drug's role in her own mind: **"I began to think
of prednisone as my sort of anti-placebo. Instead of taking a drug that was actually nothing, I
thought I needed a drug, but I didn't. It seemed to be my crutch in tiny pill form."** But she
continued tapering anyway, citing prednisone's known long-term risks named in `prednisone-part-i`
and `prednisone-part-ii`: **"corticosteroids are not known to be something one wants to continue
for long periods of time... especially as a female in her 40's (i.e. bone density risk)."**

## Caught by imaging, not by feeling

`[¶1]`

The recurrence that ended the taper was entirely subclinical from her own perspective: **"My eyes
felt super good this morning; not dry, and my vision seemed clear (for me)... if not for the fundus
photography... and the fact that my doctor knows my eyes so well, it likely would have gone
undetected."** This directly exemplifies the pattern she first named in `diagnosis-story` years
earlier — no reliable subjective warning sign — resolved here specifically by the imaging routine
described in `into-the-eyes-of-the-beholder`.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- Explicitly *no* felt symptoms at the point of recurrence — clear vision, no dryness — with
  detection made entirely by fundus photography and her doctor's comparative judgment `[¶1]`.

## Source-stated confidence

The conclusion ("not taking prednisone is not in the cards for me") is stated as her own
firsthand-confirmed finding from a real, careful taper attempt — not assumed in advance. She frames
the resumed dose as evidence-based self-knowledge rather than resignation: **"Now I know that I need
a low dose to keep my eyes stable... I will make my own luck"** by continuing sleep, supplements,
diet, and exercise alongside the medication.
