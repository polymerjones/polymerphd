# Living with uveitis: all variables and no control

## Central claim

**What makes posterior uveitis distinctly maddening for this author, a self-described "scientist
by training," is not the vision loss itself but the impossibility of running any kind of
controlled experiment on her own body — there's no usable feedback loop between what she does and
what her eye does.** Stated directly: **"I'm a scientist by training and an experimenter at heart.
I want to conduct research, form a hypothesis and test my hypothesis"** `[¶1]`, but **"there is no
immediate reaction to anything I've done, or at least no way for me to realize it. By the time
something is amiss, the inflammation has occurred, along with the permanent scaring and possible
or probable vision loss"** `[¶1]`.

## Why no pattern ever holds

`[¶1]`

She contrasts uveitis explicitly with autoimmune conditions that do give feedback: **"It's not
like many autoimmune disease where someone eats 'X', digestive issues ensue or when this do 'Y',
they can't get out of bed the next day."** Flares defy her own attempts to find a trigger: **"I've
tried various diets, and restrictions... and different exercise regimes... Flare ups have happened
when I'm stressed (new baby, no sleep) and when I would think least likely (normal routine or a
vacation in Greece)."** A tapering attempt made this concrete: she went off prednisone after 10
years, and **"wasn't off more than a month or two when I had more activity show up on my optos
photos. So, back on again I went."**

## The site's name, explained

`[¶1]`

The post closes by explaining where "Living With Uveitis" comes from — a family member sent her an
article on chronic illness, and: **"I then realized the coincidence of what I had called this
website (living with uveitis). But that is my wish for myself and others: not to just be alive with
uveitis but to be living, in the fullest sense of the word."**

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- No early-warning symptoms before inflammation and scarring have already occurred, particularly
  for autoimmune conditions confined to the eye `[¶1]`.
- General absence of felt symptoms even during active disease: **"often, I'm oblivious to the
  whole thing"** `[¶1]`.

## Analogies worth reusing

- **Uveitis as an experiment with "all variables and no control"** — the post's own framing (and
  its title) for why a scientifically-minded patient can't find a stable cause-and-effect pattern
  `[¶1]`.

## Source-stated confidence

Entirely first-person and explicitly uncertain about causation: **"I can't discern a pattern
because I never actually know how long something's been going on in my eye before I see or feel
symptoms."** This is presented as an open, unresolved frustration, not a claim about mechanism.
