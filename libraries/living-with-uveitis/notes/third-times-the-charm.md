# Third Time's the Charm

## Central claim

**Being immunosuppressed for uveitis didn't map cleanly onto any public vaccine-eligibility
category, which meant she had to actively hunt for an appointment rather than simply qualify — and
the same mismatch resurfaced getting a third dose, since uveitis is never named on standard
eligibility checklists.** Framed directly: **"people on immunosuppressants due to medical
conditions or disease treatment sat like sitting ducks... I became a 'vaccine chaser' in my town,
calling and asking every clinic and pharmacy to get on extras lists"** `[¶1]`.

## Why a third dose specifically

`[¶1]`

The rationale is explicitly immunological, not precautionary: **"Studies have shown that those on
immunosuppressants aren't necessarily making the antibodies when they receive the vaccine. The CDC
says that a large number of hospitalized 'break through' cases are actually immunocompromised
people who didn't make antibodies or enough antibodies."** At the point of service, the mismatch
between her condition and standard eligibility categories surfaced again directly: **"The guy at
the counter asked me if I qualified in a tone that didn't surprise me... Have you had a liver
transplant? Do you have cancer? No, but I take or have taken many of the same medications."**

## What actually worried her

`[¶1]`

She's specific that the concern wasn't primarily getting sick herself: **"It's not even necessarily
about me getting sick now though... What worries me is being contagious and not being able to
attend my appointment every 6 weeks at which my doctor gives me an Avastin injection to keep my CNV
at bay"** — i.e., COVID exposure risked disrupting an unrelated, ongoing eye treatment schedule,
not just direct illness.

## Source-stated confidence

The reduced-antibody-response claim is attributed generally to "studies" and CDC hospitalization
data rather than a single named source, but stated with confidence as the direct rationale for
seeking a third dose. Her own zero-side-effect outcome across three doses is explicitly flagged as
personal experience: **"So far, I have had zero side effects."**
