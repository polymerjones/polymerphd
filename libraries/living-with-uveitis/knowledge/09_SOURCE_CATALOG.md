## 2. Avoid the splash: swimming with uveitis

- **Source ID:** `swimming-with-uveitis`
- **URL:** https://livingwithuveitis.com/2022/10/26/avoid-the-splash-swimming-with-uveitis/
- **Author:** Wendy
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/swimming-with-uveitis.txt`
- **Main subjects:** a stable, low-incident year for once, briefly contrasted against a COVID-19 infection, the specific mechanism by which chlorinated pool water raises infection risk for eye patients
- **Anatomy:** tear film, cornea
- **Management options:** swim goggles (with a specific caution about intraocular pressure), rinsing eyes after swimming, and preservative-free drops, a prescription antibacterial drop (Ofloxacin) as an optional precaution for frequent swimmers
- **Important concepts:** a mostly quiet year used as the occasion to research a previously-avoided question, chlorine as an imperfect sanitizer that also strips the eye's own protective tear film

## 3. COMPLICATIONS

- **Source ID:** `treatment-complications`
- **URL:** https://livingwithuveitis.com/home/treatments/complications/
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/treatment-complications.txt`
- **Main subjects:** complications the author has personally encountered, listed by name, how treatment side effects can snowball into their own separate complications
- **Anatomy:** choroid, retina, optic nerve, trabecular meshwork
- **Management options:** cataract surgery, anti-VEGF therapy (e.g. Avastin) for choroidal neovascularization, vitrectomy for epiretinal membrane and complex retinal detachment, glaucoma procedures (SLT, trabeculectomy, aqueous shunt implantation)
- **Important concepts:** complications as often driven by the treatment (steroids) rather than the disease itself, the 10 mmHg SUN Working Group threshold for steroid-induced pressure rise

## 4. Coping when a treatment fails

- **Source ID:** `coping-when-treatment-fails`
- **URL:** https://livingwithuveitis.com/2020/03/30/coping-when-a-treatment-fails/
- **Author:** Wendy
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/coping-when-treatment-fails.txt`
- **Main subjects:** a specific flare that followed a tapering attempt, told in real time, the emotional structure of treatment failure as a kind of loss, concrete, generalizable reasons a medication can appear not to work
- **Anatomy:** none recorded
- **Management options:** Yutiq implant tapering, followed by a new CellCept/prednisone pulse, then an Ozurdex bridge injection
- **Important concepts:** a steroid-sparing dose as a treatment goal distinct from being off steroids entirely, drug efficacy stated numerically rather than as pass/fail

## 5. Diffusing the bomb

- **Source ID:** `diffusing-the-bomb`
- **URL:** https://livingwithuveitis.com/2022/02/22/diffusing-the-bomb/
- **Author:** Wendy
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/diffusing-the-bomb.txt`
- **Main subjects:** a month-long bleb leak crisis following a trabeculectomy performed six years earlier, the two specific dangers a bleb leak poses (infection and hypotony), a bandage contact lens as an interim fix, and the ordeal of managing it at home
- **Anatomy:** trabecular meshwork, sclera, anterior chamber
- **Management options:** a bandage contact lens plus pressure-reducing and antibiotic drops, autologous blood injection named as the next-step option if drops/lens failed
- **Important concepts:** a bleb leak as a delayed complication of glaucoma surgery performed years earlier, not a new event in isolation, hypotony defined by a specific pressure threshold, distinct from and opposite to the high-pressure complications discussed elsewhere

## 6. Evil Eyes

- **Source ID:** `evil-eyes`
- **URL:** https://livingwithuveitis.com/2020/10/26/evil-eyes/
- **Author:** Wendy
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/evil-eyes.txt`

## 7. HEALTHY VISION

## 14. Prednisone for life

- **Source ID:** `prednisone-for-life`
- **URL:** https://livingwithuveitis.com/2024/12/15/prednisone-for-life/
- **Author:** Wendy
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/prednisone-for-life.txt`
- **Main subjects:** a year-long taper attempt, after 13 years on prednisone, ending in the conclusion that a low dose is permanently necessary, fundus photography catching a subtle recurrence her own senses missed entirely
- **Anatomy:** none recorded

## 15. Prednisone: The Good, the Bad, & the Ugly (Part I)

- **Source ID:** `prednisone-part-i`
- **URL:** https://livingwithuveitis.com/2020/08/17/prednisone-the-good-the-bad-and-the-ugly-part-i/
- **Author:** Wendy
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/prednisone-part-i.txt`
- **Main subjects:** what prednisone is and how it mimics the body's own cortisol, a systematic, body-system-by-system side effect survey grounded in personal experience
- **Anatomy:** pituitary gland, adrenal gland
- **Management options:** prednisone dosing bands (high dose >40mg, low/steroid-sparing dose <7.5mg), calcium, vitamin D, and weight-bearing exercise as mitigations for bone loss
- **Important concepts:** prednisone as roughly four to five times as potent as the body's own cortisol, adrenocortical insufficiency, where the body reduces its own cortisol production in response to supplementation

## 20. Stick a needle in my eye: intravitreal steroid implant injection tutorial

- **Source ID:** `intravitreal-injection-tutorial`

## 25. TREATMENTS

- **Source ID:** `treatments-overview`
- **URL:** https://livingwithuveitis.com/home/treatments/
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/treatments-overview.txt`
- **Main subjects:** the drug-class ladder from eye drops to systemic immunosuppression to biologics, localized versus systemic treatment, and what decides between them
- **Anatomy:** vitreous humor
- **Management options:** steroid eye drops (Pred Forte, Maxidex, Lotemax, Vexol), immunosuppressants and corticosteroids (prednisone, hydrocortisone), antimetabolites (methotrexate, azathioprine, mycophenolate mofetil), T-cell/calcineurin inhibitors (cyclosporine, tacrolimus), biologic TNF inhibitors (adalimumab/Humira, infliximab/Remicade), periocular and intravitreal injections, intravitreal implants (Ozurdex, Yutiq, Retisert)
- **Important concepts:** localized treatment sparing the rest of the body from systemic side effects, drug classes escalating roughly by how broadly they suppress the immune system

- **Author:** Wendy
- **Source file:** `sources/clean/pregnancy-journey.txt`
