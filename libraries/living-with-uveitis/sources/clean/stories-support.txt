[¶1] Stories Uveitis isn’t common and it’s not well known. However, stories of people living with uveitis are out there! Support Many larger clinics have in-person support groups. Online support groups can be helpful but can be a bit depressing in my experience, depending on the group. Advice from individuals in groups should never be taken in place of, or without consulting, your doctor. Facebook Groups in addition to OUIF’s FB Groups: - Uveitis the Eye inflammation group - Uveitis worldwide awareness support for adults & parents with kids Low Vision Resources Living Well with Low Vision by Prevent Blindness.org Resources for the visually impaired by Allaboutvision.com
