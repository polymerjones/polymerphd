# Are you still touching receipts?

## Central claim

**The host argues thermal-paper receipts contain BPA and BPS, both endocrine-disrupting chemicals
absorbable through skin contact, and while occasional exposure isn't itself a crisis, he minimizes
cumulative exposure by declining receipts or handling them with a barrier (his shirt) rather than
bare skin.** `[00:00]` "Don't touch the thermal paper receipts and you will decrease your exposure
to BPA and BPS, endocrine disrupting chemicals that can be messing up your hormones."

## The exposure and the response

`[00:00]` — Thermal receipt paper is coated with BPA and BPS, described as absorbed through skin on
contact. The host is explicit that occasional exposure ("touching one at a grocery store every once
in a while") isn't itself alarming, but frames his own practice as minimizing cumulative endocrine
disruptor exposure generally — declining the receipt outright, or using his sleeve/shirt as a
barrier to put a receipt in the trash without direct skin contact.
