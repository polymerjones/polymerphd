# I never use toothpaste…

## Central claim

**The host describes a minimalist oral hygiene routine — a bamboo toothbrush with boar's hair
bristles (avoiding plastic-bristle microplastic exposure), PFAS-free silk floss (citing a study
linking PFAS-containing glide floss to higher body levels of these chemicals in women), and no
toothpaste at all — arguing plaque can be removed with just water and a toothbrush, and that bad
breath reflects oral/gut microbiome dysbiosis rather than insufficient toothpaste use.** `[00:00]`
"Bad breath is not the result of not using toothpaste."

## The routine and the reasoning

`[00:00]` — **Toothbrush**: bamboo with boar's hair bristles, chosen to avoid microplastic exposure
from plastic bristles. **Floss**: silk, explicitly avoiding glide flosses, which the host says a
study found contain PFAS/fluorine derivatives, correlating with higher PFAS body burden in women who
used them. **Toothpaste**: none — plaque is removed with water and brushing alone. **Bad breath
reframing**: attributed to dysbiosis in the mouth and gut microbiome, addressed by improving food
quality and gut health rather than by using toothpaste or mouthwash.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Bad breath** — reframed as a symptom of oral/gut microbiome dysbiosis, treatable through diet
  and gut health rather than toothpaste `[00:00]`.
