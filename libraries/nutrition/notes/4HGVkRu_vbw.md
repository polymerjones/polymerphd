# How much protein should you eat?

## Central claim

**The host gives a simple protein rule of thumb — one gram of protein per pound of bodyweight per
day — and illustrates it with concrete food combinations at three example body weights (120 lb,
160 lb, 200 lb).** `[00:00]` "The simple rule of thumb is one gram of protein per pound of body
weight."

## The examples

`[00:00]` — **120-lb woman**: ~120g protein/day, achievable with two eggs, 100g Parmesan cheese, a
glass of raw milk, and one and a half burger patties. **160-lb man**: ~160g/day, adding one more
glass of raw milk, one more egg, plus more red meat, chicken, or fish. **200-lb NFL linebacker
(example)**: ~200g/day, adding another egg or two, more steak, and optionally a scoop of
animal-based protein powder to hit the target.
