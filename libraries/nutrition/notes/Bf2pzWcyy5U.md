# Chick-fil-A’s FAKE chicken sandwich?

## Central claim

**Breaking down Chick-fil-A's grilled chicken sandwich component by component, the host counts 46
ingredients in the chicken patty (including soybean oil, canola oil, modified corn starch, processed
sugar, xanthan gum, and dimethyl polysiloxane) and 61 in the bun, plus a Chick-fil-A sauce whose
first ingredient is soybean oil — directly contradicting staff claims that the sandwich is "100%
chicken."** `[00:00]` "Marketing something like this as healthy and having 61 ingredients in this
bun and 46 ingredients in this chicken patty, that's not healthy. That's just us getting played."

## The ingredient breakdown

`[00:00]` — **Chicken patty (46 ingredients)**: soybean oil, canola oil, modified corn starch,
processed sugar, xanthan gum, and **dimethyl polysiloxane** ("an ingredient in silly putty"). **Bun
(61 ingredients)**: also containing processed sugars, seed oils, and gums. **Sauce**: first
ingredient listed as soybean oil. The host explicitly states he's "all for healthier fast food
options," framing the critique as being about the gap between marketing and actual ingredient content
rather than opposition to fast food itself.
