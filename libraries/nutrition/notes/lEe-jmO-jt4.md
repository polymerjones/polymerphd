# Why I don't drink Coffee!

## Central claim

**Coffee (and other methylxanthine sources — tea, chocolate) is argued to deplete minerals like
calcium, magnesium, and manganese, citing a study of post-menopausal women showing bone loss with
moderate coffee intake specifically when daily calcium fell below 800 mg — with the host
recommending sleep and diet, not caffeine, as the source of energy.** `[00:00]` "We know that
methylxanthines like caffeine in coffee and tea or theobromine in chocolate deplete minerals in
humans. Important nutrients like calcium, magnesium, or manganese... Caffeine, coffee, this is
borrowing tomorrow's happiness today."

## The cited study, precisely stated

`[00:00]` — Post-menopausal women, moderate coffee intake, bone loss specifically **conditional on
daily calcium intake below 800 mg** — the host notes the study "didn't even look at" magnesium or
manganese, an explicit limitation he states himself rather than glossing over.
