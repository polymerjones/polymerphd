# Do this to increase creatine absorption

## Central claim

**The host argues creatine-related bloating is generally caused by incomplete dissolution rather
than the creatine itself, and that fully dissolving a standard 5g dose in at least 300 mL of water
(more if heated) prevents the osmotic gut effects that cause bloating, gas, or diarrhea.** `[00:00]`
"Bloating from creatine is generally caused by not mixing it in enough water."

## The mechanism and the fix

`[00:00]` — **Mechanism**: undissolved creatine particles sitting in the gut draw water into the
gut osmotically, causing **osmotic diarrhea, gas, or bloating** — not an intolerance to creatine
itself. **Fix**: mix 5g of creatine monohydrate (from a "trusted third-party tested source") with
**at least 300 mL of water** at room temperature, or less water if heated (creatine is more soluble
warm). The host is explicit: **no dry scooping, no gritty undissolved creatine left in the glass.**

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Bloating, gas, and diarrhea from creatine use** — attributed specifically to incomplete
  dissolution rather than creatine itself, with a direct fix offered `[00:00]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Osmotic diarrhea/bloating** — fluid drawn into the gut by undissolved solute (here,
  undissolved creatine particles), named as the mechanism behind creatine-related GI symptoms
  `[00:00]`.
