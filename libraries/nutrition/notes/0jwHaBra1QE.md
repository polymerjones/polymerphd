# Never mix coffee with creatine

## Central claim

**Citing a 1996 study and a 2022 study, both on knee extension, the host argues combining caffeine
with creatine blunts or eliminates creatine's usual performance and muscle-thickness benefits, and
recommends separating the two by three to four hours to get creatine's full effect.** `[00:00]`
"Never mix your creatine with caffeine. It might abolish the positive effects of creatine."

## The two cited studies

`[00:00]` — **1996 study**: measured maximal torque for knee extension; creatine alone increased
maximal torque, but **creatine combined with caffeine showed no performance benefit**. **2022
study**: measured muscle thickness with the same knee-extension model; creatine alone increased
muscle thickness, but **combined with caffeine, there was no change**. Neither study is named by
author or journal, though both are specific enough (year, exact measure, exact muscle group) to be
identifiable claims.

## The proposed mechanism

`[00:00]` — Creatine is said to **shorten muscle relaxation time**, allowing faster maximal force
generation. Caffeine is said to do the opposite — causing **calcium release from the sarcoplasmic
reticulum**, which **lengthens** muscle relaxation time. The host frames these as directly opposed
effects that could cancel each other out, explicitly hedged: **"it's possible that creatine and
caffeine might cancel each other out a little bit."** The recommended fix: separate creatine and
caffeine intake by **three to four hours**.
