# Egg whites are BS

## Central claim

**The host describes personally switching from egg whites (in college, out of fear of yolk
cholesterol) to whole eggs, arguing the cholesterol concern has been "widely debunked," that yolks
carry nutrients (vitamin A, choline, riboflavin, folate) not found in the white, and that dietary
cholesterol has minimal effect on serum cholesterol, especially for someone metabolically
healthy.** `[00:00]` "Don't get egg whites. Just get the whole egg."

## The nutrient case, and the cholesterol reversal

`[00:00]` — **Egg white**: "a lot of good bioavailable protein and some nutrients." **Egg yolk**:
credited with vitamin A, choline, riboflavin, and folate — described as "unique nutrients that
aren't found in the white in a lot of cases." On the cholesterol concern that drove his earlier
egg-white-only habit: **"dietary cholesterol has a very minimal effect on serum cholesterol,"** with
an added hedge that for someone "metabolically healthy," he doesn't consider it "a big deal
anyway."
