# McDonald's salt is not just salt?

## Central claim

**The host points out that McDonald's french fry salt isn't just sodium chloride — it also
contains dextrose (sugar) and a silicoaluminate anti-caking agent containing aluminum, which he
links to neurological issues including neurodegenerative disease and Alzheimer's.** `[00:00]`
"Three ingredients in salt? What?"

## The three ingredients

`[00:00]` — **Sodium chloride** (standard salt), **dextrose** (a sugar), and **silicoaluminate**,
described as "an aluminum salt which contains aluminum," which the host links to "all sorts of
neurological issues in humans, including neurodegenerative issues, dementia, like Alzheimer's
disease." No specific study is cited connecting this ingredient's aluminum quantity to a measurable
health outcome.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Neurodegenerative disease, dementia, and Alzheimer's** — associated with aluminum exposure
  generally, in the context of a silicoaluminate additive in fast food salt `[00:00]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Silicoaluminate** — an aluminum-containing anti-caking agent named as an ingredient in
  McDonald's fry salt `[00:00]`.
