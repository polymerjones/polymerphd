# Beware of dishwashing detergents

## Central claim

**Citing a recent study, the host argues alcohol ethoxylates — an ingredient class in many
dishwasher pods and rinse aids — damage and disrupt the gut's epithelial barrier lining, and
recommends switching to natural detergents or hand-washing dishes with baking soda instead.**
`[00:00]` "A recent study found that some of the ingredients found in these dishwashing detergents
and rinse aids, specifically alcohol ethoxylates, were damaging and disruptive to the epithelial
barrier in your gut."

## The mechanism and the alternative

`[00:00]` — **Named compound**: alcohol ethoxylates, found in dishwasher pods and rinse aids, cited
in a recent study as damaging to the **epithelial barrier** lining the gut. **Exposure pathway**:
residue from dishwasher detergents remaining on dishes and being ingested with food. **Alternative**:
natural dishwasher detergents, or hand-washing dishes with baking soda (sodium bicarbonate), which
the host notes contains none of these concerning compounds — he also frames hand-washing as a
personally enjoyable, "zen meditative practice."
