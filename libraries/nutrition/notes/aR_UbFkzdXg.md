# Hospitals = profits?? 💰

## Central claim

trillion business — is structurally designed to keep patients returning for pills and treatments
rather than to resolve the root cause of illness, and that diet and lifestyle interventions, while
more powerful, are undervalued because they're free and don't generate the same profit.** `[00:00]`
"Our healthcare system is designed to keep you coming back for pills, for treatments. That's how
profits are maximized."
