# The amount of heavy metals in CHOCOLATE will shock you...

## Central claim

**Citing a Consumer Reports test of 28 chocolate bars, the host reports every single bar contained
significant lead and cadmium, with Hu Simple Dark Chocolate specifically exceeding the maximum
daily allowed amount of both — 210% for lead, 204% for cadmium, in just one ounce — concluding
"you cannot eat chocolate without getting significant amounts of heavy metals in your diet."**
`[00:00]` "Every single chocolate bar had significant levels of both lead and cadmium."

## The cited test and thresholds

`[00:00]` — **Consumer Reports** tested 28 chocolate bars; all contained detectable lead and
cadmium. **Hu Simple Dark Chocolate**, one ounce: **210% of the maximum daily allowed lead** and
**204% of the maximum daily allowed cadmium**. Stated maximum daily thresholds: **lead, 0.5
micrograms; cadmium, 4.1 micrograms**. Health effects named for lead/cadmium exposure generally:
cardiovascular disease, stroke, dementia, and increased cancer rates, in both adults and children.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed
