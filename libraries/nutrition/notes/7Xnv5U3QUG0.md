# Coconuts are tricky…

## Central claim

**The host gives a nuanced, product-by-product ranking of coconut foods: coconut water is
endorsed for its electrolytes and hydration, coconut oil is acceptable but ranked below animal
fats, and coconut meat, milk, and flour are flagged for phytic acid, digestive enzyme inhibitors,
and — in many coconut milks — carrageenan, which he says clearly damages the gut.** `[00:00]`
"This one is nuanced. Let's go through all of it."

## The product-by-product ranking

`[00:00]` — **Coconut water** (young coconuts): "not bullshit" — simple carbohydrates, electrolytes,
hydrating, and enjoyable. **Coconut oil**: "okay," better than seed oils but ranked below animal
fats like butter and tallow, which contain longer-chain saturated fatty acids like **stearic acid**
that "activate fat burning and the mitochondria" — Firestarter (from Heart and Soil Supplements) is
named as an alternative when tallow isn't available. **Coconut meat, milk, and flour**: "bullshit" —
the actual nut portion carries phytic acid and digestive enzyme inhibitors that "mess up your
digestion," and many coconut milk products additionally contain **carrageenan**, a sulfated
polysaccharide the host says is "very clearly" damaging to the human gut.
