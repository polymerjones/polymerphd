# Creatine = hair loss???

## Central claim

**The host argues creatine does not cause hair loss, tracing the myth to a small 2009 study that
found a modest DHT increase without directly measuring hair follicle growth, and citing a 2025
randomized controlled trial of 45 men (ages 18-40) taking 5g of creatine that found no change in
DHT, no change in the DHT-to-testosterone ratio, and no measurable effect on hair follicles or hair
growth.** `[00:00]` "There is essentially no evidence that creatine causes hair loss, guys."

## The two cited studies

`[00:00]` — **2009 study**: small sample size, found a modest increase in **DHT
(dihydrotestosterone)** in participants, but did not directly measure hair follicle growth or hair
loss itself — the actual outcome the "creatine causes hair loss" claim depends on. **2025 study**: a
**randomized controlled trial**, 45 males aged 18-40, taking 5g of creatine, found **no change in
DHT, no change in the DHT-to-testosterone ratio, and no change in hair follicle measures or hair
growth**. The host frames this as correcting a long-standing myth built on an inferential leap the
2009 study never actually tested.
