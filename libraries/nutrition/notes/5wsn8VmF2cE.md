# What are the best pans for cooking?

## Central claim

**The host argues stainless steel pans are safer than Teflon non-stick pans, since Teflon
aerosolizes above 488°F and is associated with headaches and irritability when inhaled, and
scratched non-stick coating can transfer directly into food, whereas stainless steel has no coating
to shed at all.** `[00:00]` "This is why I always use a stainless steel pan to cook, rather than a
nonstick pan."

## The specific claims

`[00:00]` — **Temperature threshold**: Teflon pans release aerosolized polymers into the air above
**488°F**. **Symptoms**: this exposure is associated with **headaches and irritability**, citing
"many studies" in animals and "many examples" in humans (none named specifically). **Physical
damage**: scratched non-stick coating is described as able to transfer into food during cooking.
**The alternative**: stainless steel, described as simply "a mix of iron and other elements" with
nothing to leach into food.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Headaches and irritability** — attributed to inhaling aerosolized Teflon polymers from
  overheated non-stick pans `[00:00]`.

## Source-stated confidence

Low to moderate. The 488°F threshold is a specific, checkable figure (roughly consistent with
publicly documented PTFE off-gassing temperatures), but "many studies" and "many examples in
humans" aren't individually named.
