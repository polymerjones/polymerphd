# I ate a vegetable…

## Central claim

**In a self-deprecating personal account, the host eats a green (unripe) banana before surfing for
quick energy, describes it as effectively eating a vegetable rather than fruit since unripe fruit
retains higher defense-chemical levels, reports feeling "horrible" afterward, and separately
rejects the claim that resistant starch from unripe fruit is necessary for gut health.** `[00:00]`
"Fruit doesn't want to get eaten when it's unripe... As fruit ripens, the amount of defense
chemicals in the fruit decrease... I ate that green banana before I surfed because I wanted energy
and I burped it up the whole time. I felt horrible."

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Burping and general feeling of illness ("horrible")** — self-reported after eating an unripe
  banana `[00:00]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Resistant starch** — used without full definition; a starch form found in unripe fruit,
  claimed by others (and rejected here) to be necessary for gut health `[00:00]`.
