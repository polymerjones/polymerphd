## Source-stated confidence

Low — "completely preventable" is a strong categorical claim; while diet quality is a genuine,
widely recognized contributor to childhood obesity in mainstream nutrition science, framing it as
fully preventable through diet alone doesn't account for other contributing factors (genetics,
socioeconomic access, activity levels) that mainstream research also identifies. No study is cited
in this clip.
