# Seed oils = obesity

## Central claim

**The host presents a graphic tracking a sharp rise in U.S. seed oil consumption since 1969
alongside U.S. obesity rates climbing from ~11.9% to over 43%, and — while explicitly noting
correlation isn't causation — points to separate interventional studies showing seed oil-derived
linoleic acid increases oxidative stress and decreases nitric oxide precursors as the mechanistic
case for seed oils, not declining meat/animal fat consumption, driving obesity.** `[00:00]` "Meat
and animal fat are not the cause of your obesity and chronic illness. Seed oils are."

## The correlation and the mechanism, kept distinct

`[00:00]` — Two separate lines of evidence, explicitly not conflated: **the trend graphic** — seed
oil consumption rising sharply over 110 years, especially since 1969, alongside obesity rising from
11.9% to 43%+ (with overweight+obesity combined now over 70% of the U.S. population); and
**interventional studies** (unnamed) showing increased dietary linoleic acid causes **increased
oxidative stress and decreased nitric oxide precursors**. The host adds that meat and animal fat
consumption has *declined* over the same period, used to argue against the alternative
explanation that animal fat drives obesity.
