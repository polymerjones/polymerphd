# Do. Not. Eat. Plants. Period. #health #shorts #carnivorediet

## Central claim

**The host argues domesticated kale is not meaningfully different from toxic, inedible wild garden
leaves — both are defended plant tissue containing toxins the plant doesn't want eaten — and
concludes leafy greens are "survival food at best," with no place in an animal-based diet.**
`[00:00]` "What is the difference between this domesticated kale that we call a superfood and these
leaves right here? Not a whole lot."

## The comparison

`[00:00]` — Demonstrated by contrast: eating wild leaves from his own backyard garden would cause
vomiting, diarrhea, or stomach pain ("I'm gonna be pooping or vomiting... a real stomachache real
fast"). Kale is framed as "basically the same thing, just a distant cousin with a few less toxins,"
still carrying "many plant defense chemicals." No specific toxin or defense chemical is named for
kale in this clip.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed
