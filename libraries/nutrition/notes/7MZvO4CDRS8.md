# Toothpaste is overrated…

## Central claim

**The host argues he doesn't use toothpaste at all — brushing with a natural boar-hair toothbrush
and water alone — because the actual purpose of brushing is to disrupt dental biofilm, which
doesn't require toothpaste, and states that bad breath comes from gut dysbiosis rather than the
absence of a minty toothpaste.** `[00:00]` "You can just brush your teeth with water. It works just
fine."

## The reasoning

`[00:00]` — Brushing's purpose is reframed as **disrupting biofilm** on the teeth, a mechanical
action achievable with a toothbrush and water alone, without toothpaste. For those who still want
to use toothpaste, the host recommends a natural product with **few ingredients and no fluoride**.
On bad breath specifically: he attributes it to **gut dysbiosis** rather than a lack of toothpaste,
reporting his own breath doesn't smell despite using no toothpaste.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Bad breath** — attributed to gut dysbiosis rather than toothpaste use, reframing a commonly
  assumed oral-hygiene issue as a gut-health issue `[00:00]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Biofilm** — the layer on teeth that brushing is meant to disrupt, named as the actual mechanical
  purpose of brushing, independent of toothpaste `[00:00]`.
- **Dysbiosis** — an imbalance in gut microbiota, named here as the host's proposed cause of bad
  breath `[00:00]`.
