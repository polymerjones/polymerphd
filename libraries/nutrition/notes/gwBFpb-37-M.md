# I Made an Energy Drink!

## Central claim

**Rejecting commercial energy drinks (caffeine, processed/artificial sugars) and vegetables alike
as sources of "fake energy," the host blends his own "energy drink" from animal-based
ingredients — pineapple, papaya, raw ground beef, raw heart, raw liver, raw testicle, goat's milk
kefir, raw egg yolk, salt, honey, and butter.** `[00:00]` "Why are you trying to get your fake
energy from things like caffeine, stimulants, sugar, or processed sugars, or artificial sugars...
All the nutrients that are magical in here are found in meat and organs... I'm going to show you how
to really make an energy drink out of nutritious food."
