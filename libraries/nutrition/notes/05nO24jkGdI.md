# Dryer sheets are disrupting your hormones

## Central claim

**The host argues dryer sheets release carcinogenic and irritating gases during drying, leave
phthalate-based fragrance residue on clothing that's absorbed through skin, and coat fabric in
"quats" (quaternary ammonium compounds) linked to skin and respiratory irritation — recommending
wool dryer balls as a simple substitute.** `[00:00]` "There's absolutely no reason to use dryer
sheets. These are totally toxic."

## The three concerns

`[00:00]` — **Off-gassing**: benzene (described as carcinogenic) and acetaldehyde (irritating)
released when dryer sheets are heated in the dryer. **Fragrance/phthalates**: described as
endocrine disrupting, absorbed through skin from treated clothing. **Quats (quaternary ammonium
compounds)**: waxy substances designed to stick to fabric, linked to skin disorders, asthma
worsening, irritation, and eczema. The suggested replacement: **wool dryer balls**, used in place
of a sheet.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Skin irritation and eczema** — attributed to quaternary ammonium compound residue on
  dryer-sheet-treated clothing `[00:00]`.
- **Worsening asthma** — separately attributed to the same quats `[00:00]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Quats (quaternary ammonium compounds)** — waxy fabric-softening compounds in dryer sheets,
  linked here to skin and respiratory irritation `[00:00]`.
