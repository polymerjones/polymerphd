# Grocery stores make me sick

## Central claim

**Walking through a regular grocery store, the host argues roughly 75% of the store is processed
sugar or seed oils — including foods not commonly flagged, like fried banana chips, tortillas, and
roasted nuts — with the meat counter as essentially the only section free of both.** `[00:00]` "It
is nearly impossible to find non-processed healthy food in a regular grocery store. Like 75% of the
store is processed sugar and seed oils... This is about the only spot in the whole store with the
meat counter where you're going to easily avoid processed sugars and seed oils."

## The store walkthrough

`[00:00]` — Named as containing seed oils: fried banana chips, the entire oil aisle (vegetable,
canola, corn, soybean, sunflower), tortillas, vegetable shortening, fried corn treats, and even
roasted nuts. Named as containing processed sugar: flavored yogurts and milks, energy drinks, and
the checkout-lane display. **Stated absence:** "Where are the animal fats? There's nothing here."
