# Are eggs the best shampoo? #animalbased #health

## Central claim

**The host argues raw egg — yolk and white — nourishes hair follicles when rubbed into the scalp
via nutrients like zinc, taurine, carnitine, B12, and branched-chain amino acids, and frames it as
a natural alternative to conventional shampoo, which he says carries fragrance-related endocrine
disruptors (phthalates, PFAS, other xenoestrogens) that can also penetrate the scalp.** `[00:00]`
"Eggs contain many nutrients in the white and the yolk that are proven in research studies to
penetrate the scalp and nourish the hair follicle."

## The two-sided argument

`[00:00]` — **For egg**: zinc, taurine, carnitine, vitamin B12, and branched-chain amino acids are
named as follicle-nourishing nutrients present in both the yolk and the white, said to be "proven
in research studies to penetrate the scalp" (no specific study named). **Against conventional
shampoo**: perfumes and fragrances are flagged as a source of phthalates, PFAS (perfluoroalkyl
compounds), and other xenoestrogens/hormone-disrupting compounds, which the host says "can also
penetrate your scalp and get into your body."
