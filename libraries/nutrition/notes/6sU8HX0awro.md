# This is my supplement stack…

## Central claim

**The host reframes a typical supplement stack — multivitamin, creatine, collagen, electrolytes —
as unnecessary given the right whole foods: raw liver in place of a multivitamin, grass-fed meat
for creatine, homemade bone broth for collagen, salt and fresh coconut water for electrolytes,
arguing this is how the same nutrients are obtained in bioavailable form.** `[00:00]` "I can get all
of those things in highly bioavailable forms in food."

## The food-for-supplement substitutions

`[00:00]` — **Multivitamin → raw liver**: credited with choline, carnitine, carnosine
(transcribed "anserine"), taurine, vitamin K2, copper, biotin, and riboflavin. **Creatine → grass-
fed meat**. **Electrolytes → salt and fresh coconut water** ("nature's can," consumed morning and
evening). **Collagen → homemade bone broth**. The host frames this as core to why he favors an
animal-based diet (organs, meat, fruit, honey, raw dairy) over a food-plus-pills approach.
