# CarnivoreMDonald’s

## Central claim

**In a bit filmed at a McDonald's drive-through, the host hands the staff his own pre-made
animal-based meal (grass-fed beef burger, avocado, organic banana, raw butter, spring water) instead
of ordering off the menu, using the contrast to argue McDonald's food — full of seed oils and
processed sugars — is a major contributor to poor health in America.** `[00:00]` "That is why most
of America is fat, sick, and unhealthy. I hope people will take the time to seek out good quality
food like this."

## The bit and the argument

`[00:00]` — The host, "on the run" without time to cook, drives to McDonald's but hands the staff his
own pre-made food (grass-fed beef burger, avocado, organic banana, raw butter, spring water) rather
than ordering. He expresses a genuine wish that fast food restaurants served food this healthy, then
names McDonald's actual menu content — seed oils, processed sugars, "other garbage" — as
incompatible with health, and frames this as connected to widespread poor health in America.
