## The recipe

`[00:00]` — Blend **2 eggs per 1 cup of cottage cheese**, plus shredded **Parmigiano-Reggiano
cheese** and a little salt. Pour onto parchment paper (lightly greased with tallow), bake at 350°F
for about 40 minutes. Described as usable for sandwiches, wraps, or burritos.
