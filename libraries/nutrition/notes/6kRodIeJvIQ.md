# Are you using non-stick pans?

## Central claim

**The host argues Teflon non-stick pans remain a problem even after PFOA was removed in 2013 for
its negative hormonal effects, because its replacement, GenX, "looks to be basically just as bad,"
and describes traveling with his own stainless steel pan specifically because cheap Teflon pans are
so common in Airbnb rentals.** `[00:00]` "If you have one of these in your home, throw it out and
replace it with one of these, a stainless steel pan, and you will be much healthier in the long
run."

## The PFOA-to-GenX history

`[00:00]` — **PFOA**: the original Teflon chemical, associated with headaches, irritability, and
"clear negative hormonal effects in humans," removed from pans in **2013**. **GenX**: PFOA's
replacement chemical, which the host states "looks to be basically just as bad... and it's in your
pan" — implying a regrettable substitution rather than a genuine fix. He notes he encounters cheap
Teflon pans in "almost every Airbnb" he's stayed in, which is why he now travels with his own
stainless steel pan.
