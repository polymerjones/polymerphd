# The amount of cups of brown rice to make 3tbsp of rice bran oil might shock you...

## Central claim

**The host argues a single Chipotle meal contains three tablespoons of rice bran oil — an amount
of linoleic acid that would require eating 24 cups of brown rice to obtain naturally — using this
to illustrate that seed oil consumption concentrates a dose of linoleic acid humans never
encountered evolutionarily.** `[00:00]` "This is why seed oils are completely evolutionarily
inconsistent for humans."

## The calculation and the restaurant claim

`[00:00]` — Directly asking a Chipotle worker on camera what oil the meat is cooked in, the host
confirms **rice bran oil**. He calculates that extracting **three tablespoons of rice bran oil**
naturally would require **24 cups of brown rice** — a quantity no one would eat in a day — framing
this concentration as the actual problem with seed oils, rather than rice itself. He extends the
warning to canola, sunflower, safflower, soybean, grapeseed, and rice bran oil generally as
restaurant cooking oils to watch for.
