# What does the “A” stand for?

## Central claim

**Asking Chick-fil-A customers what the "A" stands for (they guess "grade A"), the host contrasts
that branding with the grilled chicken sandwich's actual 47-ingredient chicken ingredient list —
including dimethyl polysiloxane, three seed oils, and multiple gums — and notes Chick-fil-A dropped
its antibiotic-free stipulation in March 2024.** `[00:00]` "100% real chicken has 47 ingredients?
There's nothing grade A about this junk food at all."
