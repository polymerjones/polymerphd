# Sprinting or jogging?

## Central claim

**Having previously run ultramarathons (including a 52-mile race), the host now prefers sprinting
over long-distance running, citing improvements to testosterone, VO2 max, and longevity from short,
intense exercise bouts, and incorporates three to five roughly 100-meter beach sprints a couple of
times per week.** `[00:00]` "It's been shown to improve testosterone, improves VO2 max, connected
with longevity. Doing intense, short bouts of exercise is incredibly valuable for humans."

## The shift from distance to sprinting

`[00:00]` — Background: the host used to run ultramarathons, including a 52-mile race in the
Colorado mountains (extended from a planned 50 miles after getting lost), and disliked sprinting at
the time. Current practice: three to five ~100-meter sprints, a couple of times a week, done on the
beach outdoors — combining the sprint work with grounding and sunlight exposure. Named benefits:
testosterone, VO2 max, and longevity. The host explicitly cautions against overusing sprinting due to
its intensity.
