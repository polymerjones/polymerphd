# The best food against the flu?

## Central claim

**Citing a 2007 study, the host claims daily colostrum consumption over two months was found at
least three times more effective than a flu vaccine at preventing flu, with significantly lower
incidence and shorter illness episodes in the colostrum group, attributed to colostrum's bioactive
peptides, immunoglobulins, and growth factors contributing to passive immunity.** `[00:00]`
"Colostrum is at least three times more effective than a flu vaccine."

## The cited study

`[00:00]` — **2007 study**, two-month duration, comparing a flu vaccine group to a group consuming
colostrum daily. Findings as stated: the colostrum group had **significantly lower incidence of
the flu**, and when they did get sick, illness duration was **"way, way shorter."** The host quotes
the study's authors directly as concluding colostrum is "at least three times more effective than a
flu vaccine" — though the study isn't named by author or journal in this transcript. Mechanism
offered: colostrum's bioactive peptides, immunoglobulins, and growth factors contribute to
**passive immunity**.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Flu incidence and illness duration** — both reported as reduced with daily colostrum

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Passive immunity** — immunity conferred by external antibodies/immune factors (here, from
  colostrum) rather than the body's own immune response, named as colostrum's proposed mechanism
  `[00:00]`.
