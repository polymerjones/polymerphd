# My favorite sleep hack

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates a triple-click iPhone shortcut that turns the screen almost entirely
orange at night by maximizing both the intensity and hue of iOS's Color Filters setting, paired
with red lighting placed around his house, to protect circadian rhythm as evening blue light
exposure decreases naturally.** `[00:00]` "I don't want to be looking at a phone that is projecting
a bunch of blue light into my eyes."

## How to set it up

`[00:00]` — Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Color Filters, turned on with both
**intensity and hue set almost all the way to the right** — a stronger, more fully orange setting
than a partial blue-light reduction. Then Accessibility → Accessibility Shortcuts → Color Filters,
enabling a triple-click toggle between the normal (blue-light-containing) screen and the orange
filtered mode. Separately, the host mentions placing red lights throughout his house for the same
circadian-protective reason.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Circadian rhythm disruption from evening blue light** — the stated reason for both the phone
  filter and the household red lighting `[00:00]`.
