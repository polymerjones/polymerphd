# Cheese is good for you

## Central claim

**High-quality raw cheeses (Parmigiano Reggiano, Grana Padano specifically named) are endorsed as
nutritious, containing stearic acid (linked to fat burning/leanness), odd-chain fatty acids (linked
to cognitive benefits), and vitamin K2 (for arterial health, scarce in plant foods) — with the host
noting raw cheese is often available even in places where raw milk itself is restricted or
unavailable.** `[00:00]` "It has many good fats in the dairy, things like stearic acid, an
18-carbon saturated fatty acid associated with fat burning and leanness, odd-chain fatty acids
which are associated with cognitive improvements in humans... vitamin K2, essential for arterial
health. K2 is not found in very many plant foods at all."

## The named nutrients

`[00:00]` — **Stearic acid:** an 18-carbon saturated fatty acid, linked to fat burning and
leanness. **Odd-chain fatty acids:** linked to cognitive improvement, described as "probably
essential for humans." **Vitamin K2:** linked to arterial health, described as scarce in plant
foods.

None named as symptoms directly — the claims address nutrient content and its associated benefits

## Source-stated confidence

Low-to-moderate — stearic acid and vitamin K2's roles are reasonably well-established in the
broader nutrition literature, though the specific "fat burning and leanness" and "cognitive
improvements" claims are stated without a named study in this clip; odd-chain fatty acids being
"probably essential for humans" is a notably hedged claim (the host uses "probably" himself) rather
than presented as settled.
