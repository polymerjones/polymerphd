# The death of bacon

## Central claim

**The host states he avoids bacon because nearly all commercially raised pigs ("99.99%") are fed
corn, soy, and seed oils, causing omega-6/linoleic acid to accumulate in their fat — fat that,
like human fat, cannot simply be eliminated once accumulated — while acknowledging pigs raised wild
or on different feed would theoretically be fine.** `[00:00]` "Pigs are fat animals. But like
humans, when pigs are fed corn and soy or seed oils, they accumulate the omega-6 fatty acids in
their fat. Yes, you accumulate omega-6 fatty acids in your body. You can't get rid of them. Pigs
can't get rid of them either... Theoretically, pigs would be fine if they were wild, but almost all
bacon I've ever seen is garbage."
