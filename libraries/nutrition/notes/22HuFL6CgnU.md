# What's in your baby's diapers?

## Central claim

**The host argues diaper ingredients deserve the same scrutiny as food, since fragrance
(phthalates and other xenoestrogens), chlorine, bleaches, parabens, and latex can be absorbed
through a child's skin during a period of active development, and recommends seeking diapers free
of all of these.** `[00:00]` "What we put on our child's skin, what we put on our skin, the closer
we wear, this gets into our bodies. Know what's going in your child's skin."
