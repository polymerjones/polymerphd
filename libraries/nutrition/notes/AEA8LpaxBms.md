# Healthiest protein bar in the world?

## Central claim

**The host frames Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese as his preferred "protein bar" — a 75g portion (about
0.17 pounds) providing roughly 26g of protein plus healthy fats, choline, calcium, and vitamin K2 —
noting it comes from grass-fed, grass-finished, raw milk from Italian cows and contains no artificial
flavors, ingredients, or binders, unlike commercial protein bars.** `[00:00]` "Show me your protein
bar with artificial flavors, artificial ingredients, binders, and compare it to this. Why are you not
eating this?"

## The nutrient breakdown

`[00:00]` — 100g of Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese contains 35-38g of protein; a 75g portion (0.17 lbs)
provides about 26g of protein. Additional named nutrients: healthy fats, choline, calcium, and
**vitamin K2**, the last specifically credited as "good for your heart." Production detail: all
Parmigiano-Reggiano is grass-fed, grass-finished, and raw, from cows in Italy eating spring grass.

## Source-stated confidence

Moderate. The protein content figures (35-38g per 100g) are consistent with published nutritional
data for Parmigiano-Reggiano; the "all Parmigiano-Reggiano is grass-fed, grass-finished, and raw"
claim reflects the cheese's Protected Designation of Origin production standards, though not cited
as such in this clip.
