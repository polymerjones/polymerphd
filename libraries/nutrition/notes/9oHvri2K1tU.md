# Top 3 travel hacks ✈️

## Central claim

**The host shares three airport travel hacks for maintaining an animal-based diet while flying:
bringing whole coconuts through security (since coconut water inside an unopened coconut isn't
treated as a liquid), using a food bag that doesn't count against the two-carry-on limit, and
preparing animal-based food (grass-fed beef, organs, coconut, honey, yogurt) in advance to bring on
the plane.** `[00:00]` "You can't bring water through security but you can bring coconuts."

## The three hacks

`[00:00]` — **Hack 1**: whole coconuts pass airport security screening (unlike liquid water),
providing coconut water in flight, cited as "a great source of magnesium, manganese all kinds of
good stuff." **Hack 2**: a bag containing only food does not count as one of the standard two
carry-on bags, allowing a third bag if it's all food. **Hack 3**: prepare animal-based food in
advance — the host names grass-fed beef, coconut, organs, honey, and yogurt as his own travel food.
