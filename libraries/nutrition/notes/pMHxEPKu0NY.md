# What causes cardiovascular disease??

## Central claim

**Rejecting the claim that meat, organs, and saturated fat cause cardiovascular disease, the host
argues the real driver is metabolic dysfunction (insulin resistance), which he links to
endothelial dysfunction and atherosclerosis — with seed oils and processed sugars named as the two
causes of that dysfunction, not animal foods.** `[00:00]` "I believe this is at the root of the
endothelial dysfunction in your blood vessels that leads to atherosclerosis, that leads to
cardiovascular disease... You reverse, you stop the two things that are causing it, seed oils and
processed sugars."
