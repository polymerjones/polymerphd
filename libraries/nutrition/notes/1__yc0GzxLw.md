# STOP eating SEED OILS!!

## Central claim

**The host ranks cooking fats from worst to best — seed oils (originally industrial lubricants)
at the bottom, olive and avocado oil next (often adulterated or oxidized), coconut oil in the
middle, and animal fats (tallow, butter) as the best choice — built around linoleic acid's
accumulation in cell membranes and adipose tissue driving oxidative stress and insulin
resistance.** `[00:00]` "These seed oils were originally created as machine lubricants, and they
should have stayed that way."

## The ranking, fat by fat

`[00:00]` — **Worst**: corn, canola, soybean, safflower, sunflower, and grapeseed oil — "garbage,"
high in linoleic acid. **Poor**: olive oil and avocado oil, flagged for being "often cut with
vegetable oils" and "often oxidized and old" — "these are a no for me." **Middle**: coconut oil,
described as "not as good as animal fats because it lacks the essential nutrients." **Best**:
tallow and butter, credited with **stearic acid, long-chain saturated fat, and odd-chain fatty
acids** — "essential for human health," per the host.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

None named individually — the concerns (oxidative stress, insulin resistance) are framed as
biomarker/metabolic outcomes rather than reported symptoms.
