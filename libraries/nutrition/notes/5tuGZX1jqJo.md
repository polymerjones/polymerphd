# Eat this food to reverse aging

## Central claim

**The host names meat as "the best anti-aging food on the planet," listing taurine, creatine,
vitamin B12, vitamin K2, ergothioneine, and collagen as compounds found in meat and associated with
longevity and long-term health.** `[00:00]` "If you wanna live strong, you wanna live well, eat more
meat. It's as simple as that, guys."

## The compound list

`[00:00]` — **Taurine**: "studied across animal species, associated with longevity, only found in
meat." **Creatine**: said to improve muscle recovery and mental clarity, and specifically to "make
vegetarians smarter" (implying creatine supplementation studies in vegetarians, who lack dietary
creatine, show cognitive improvement). **Vitamin B12, vitamin K2, ergothioneine**: named without
individual elaboration. **Collagen**: credited with skin health benefits.
