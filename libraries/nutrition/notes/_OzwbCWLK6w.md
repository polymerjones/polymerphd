# What are "Free-Range" eggs?

## Central claim

**The host ranks egg labels by actual chicken roaming space: cage-free chickens don't get outside
at all, free-range chickens get about two square feet each (better than a cage, but still high-
density), and pasture-raised chickens get 108 square feet each — enough space to actually forage
for bugs and worms instead of relying mainly on grain feed.** `[00:00]` "Free-range means that
chickens get two square feet of roaming space... but two square feet is not a whole lot for a
chicken."

## The space figures, ranked

`[00:00]` — Free-range: **~2 square feet per chicken**, with outdoor access but high density. Cage-
free: chickens "don't actually get outside at all," despite the label sounding like an improvement.
Pasture-raised: **108 square feet per chicken**, low enough density that the host says the chickens
are more likely to forage bugs and worms rather than eating "exclusively grains."
