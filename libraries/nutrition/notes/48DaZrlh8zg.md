# My outdoor workout routine

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates a bodyweight outdoor workout he does while traveling when he can find an
appropriate space — leg raises, pistol squats, box jumps, rope climbing, pull-ups, and monkey
bars — framing outdoor bodyweight training as preferable to any indoor gym.** `[00:00]` "That's
better than any indoor gym I've ever found."

## The routine

`[00:00]` — Single-leg pistol squats and single-leg box jumps ("more challenging than regular box
jumps"), a rope climb, standard and wide-grip pull-ups, L-sit leg extensions, deep-range push-ups
(explicitly deep "for full shoulder mobility... we are primates after all"), monkey bars, and a
"skin the cat" movement for shoulder mobility credited to a source named Adam Frater. The session
ends with time in the sun.
