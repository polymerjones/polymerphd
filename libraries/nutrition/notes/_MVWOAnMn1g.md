# The medical education system needs to change!

## Central claim

**The host argues Western medical education omits nutrition entirely, using his own training
history as the evidence: zero required nutrition courses across a pre-med chemistry degree, a
Physician Assistant degree, four years practicing cardiology, medical school, and residency — which
he says is why he studied nutrition independently and eventually concluded an animal-based diet is
"the single greatest lever in human health and disease."** `[00:00]` "Western medicine doesn't
teach doctors any nutrition and that is hurting so many people."

## The training history cited

`[00:00]` — Specific institutions and durations given: **four years at William & Mary** as a
chemistry/pre-med major (zero nutrition courses required for medical school admission); **two years
at George Washington University** for a PA degree, followed by **four years practicing as a PA in
cardiology** (zero nutrition courses, "lots of time thinking about pharmaceutical paradigms" instead
of root causes); **four years at the University of Arizona** for an MD; **four years at the
University of Washington** for residency (zero training on "the root cause of neuroinflammation or
autoimmune illness"). He states he pursued nutrition knowledge independently throughout, later
earning a **board certification as a physician nutrition specialist**.
