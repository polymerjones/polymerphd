# Healthy deodorant recipe

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates a homemade animal-based deodorant (grass-fed beef tallow, coconut butter,
baking soda, cornstarch, and orange essential oil), arguing regular deodorant contains toxic
chemicals that shouldn't be applied near the armpit's lymph nodes and hormonally active tissue, and
that he has used no conventional deodorant for over 10 years without odor issues.** `[00:00]`
"Regular deodorant is absolute garbage. It contains so many toxic, harmful chemicals. You do not
want to be putting regular deodorant into your armpits, which are close to your lymph nodes and
other hormonally active tissues."

## The recipe

`[00:00]` — Five tablespoons grass-fed (Lineage) beef tallow, melted; five tablespoons organic
coconut butter, melted; three tablespoons baking soda (optional, for odor-fighting, can be omitted
for sensitive skin); half cup cornstarch; about 20 drops orange essential oil. Mixed together,
refrigerated about an hour, then applied. `[01:01]` — Described as non-greasy, absorbing well, and
effective at controlling odor without "toxic, hormonal-disrupting ingredients."
