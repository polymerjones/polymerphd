# Sourdough bread is better, right? WRONG!!

## Central claim

**Sourdough bread is argued to be barely better than regular bread: fermentation reduces phytic
acid somewhat, but gluten and its lectin fragments remain, still causing gut inflammation and leaky
gut — with a specific claim that gliadin, a gluten fragment, crosses the gut epithelial lining and
ends up in nursing mothers' breast milk.** `[00:00]` "Sourdough bread is still full of tons of
gluten. It's still full of this lectin that is going to create inflammation, irritation, and leaky
gut in your gut. We know that a fragment of gluten called gliadin passes through the gut epithelial
lining and ends up in the breast milk of nursing mothers."

## The fermentation claim, precisely stated

`[00:00]` — Sourdough fermentation is credited with reducing **phytic acid** (described as a
compound that "chelates minerals and prevents their absorption") "a little bit," but explicitly
**not** with reducing gluten or lectin content meaningfully.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Leaky gut, gut inflammation, and irritation** — attributed to gluten/lectin content regardless
  of fermentation `[00:00]`.
- **Gliadin exposure in breastfeeding infants** — via breast milk, from nursing mothers who eat
  gluten `[00:00]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Gliadin** — a gluten fragment described as crossing the gut lining and appearing in breast milk
  `[00:00]`.
- **Phytic acid** — a compound that chelates (binds) minerals and reduces their absorption
  `[00:00]`.
