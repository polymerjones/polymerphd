# Avoid these cinnamon brands!

## Central claim

**Citing a Consumer Reports test, the host argues many cinnamon brands are contaminated with lead
at unsafe levels, linked to cardiovascular and kidney effects in adults and neurodevelopmental harm
in children, and names Whole Foods 365 Organic Cinnamon as one of the tested brands that came up
low for lead.** `[00:00]` "A recent study published by Consumer Reports found that many brands of
cinnamon were not even safe to eat at all."

## The findings and the fix

`[00:00]` — Health effects attributed to lead exposure: in adults, **heart disease, high blood
pressure, and kidney issues**; in children, **behavioral issues, neurodevelopmental problems,
decreased IQ, and learning difficulties**. The host names **Whole Foods Organic Cinnamon 365** as
one of the lowest-lead brands tested, with additional low-lead brands referenced as listed in the
video's caption (not included in this transcript).
