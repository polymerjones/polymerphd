# The Medical System is Broken

## Central claim

**Reflecting on 17 years of medical education and practice, the host states he learned hundreds or
thousands of pharmaceutical drugs in detail (indications, contraindications, interactions) but was
never taught about diet and food — which he believes is "the single most powerful intervention for
the majority of chronic illnesses" — and argues medical education should prioritize nutrition
ahead of pharmaceutical memorization.** `[00:00]` "I never learned about something that I believe
is the single most powerful intervention for the majority of chronic illnesses we see in Western
medicine today... Why are medical students not taught about what I believe... should be the first
intervention?"
