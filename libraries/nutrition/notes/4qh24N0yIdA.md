# This is harming your thyroid health

## Central claim

**The host argues spraying perfume on the neck delivers phthalates and other endocrine disruptors
directly through thin neck skin into the thyroid gland, and into the bloodstream generally
affecting testicles, ovaries, and liver, connecting this and similar fragrance/personal-care
chemical exposures to declining fertility rates and rising IVF use.** `[00:00]` "Is it any wonder
that fertility rates continue to decline, that more and more couples are being forced to use
fertility treatments like IVF? We are literally swimming in a toxic soup."

## The mechanism and the broader claim

`[00:00]` — **Neck application**: perfume sprayed on the neck is said to be absorbed through
particularly thin skin directly over the thyroid gland, described as "one of your hormone control
centers." **Wrist application**: also flagged, because surface veins sit close to the skin there.
**Systemic effect**: phthalates and endocrine disruptors from perfume are said to enter the
bloodstream generally, affecting testicles, ovaries, and liver — not just the thyroid. **Broader
context**: fragrances are named alongside personal care lotions (also flagged for phthalates and
PFAS) as part of a cumulative chemical exposure the host connects to falling fertility rates.
Recommendation: avoid synthetic fragrance entirely, or use a natural scent instead.
