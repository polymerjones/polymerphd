# This is why I'm drinking pet food at Sprouts #animalbased

## Central claim

**The host drinks raw kefir from Raw Farm (California), sold at Sprouts labeled as pet food
because direct consumer sales of raw dairy are illegal in 37 states including Texas, arguing this
regulatory gap is a "travesty" given raw dairy's association with reduced eczema, allergies, and
asthma — while cigarettes, processed food, seed oils, and fast food remain fully legal.** `[00:00]`
"When things like cigarettes, processed food, seed oils, French fries, garbage foods, they're
legal, but raw food isn't."

## The claim and the workaround

`[00:00]` — **Product**: raw kefir (fermented raw milk) from **Raw Farm** in California, a facility
the host states he's personally visited and seen the production process. **Legal status**: direct
sales of raw dairy to consumers are illegal in **37 states**, including Texas, forcing this product
to be sold labeled as pet food to reach consumers there. **Health claim**: raw dairy is associated
with improved eczema, allergies, and asthma — asthma specifically cited as killing **"13 kids a
day"** (scope/geography of this figure not specified in the clip).

## Symptoms and body signals addressed
