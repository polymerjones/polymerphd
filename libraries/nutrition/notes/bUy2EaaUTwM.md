# Can women do an animal based diet??

## Central claim

**Responding to a recurring question, the host argues women thrive on an animal-based diet,
citing better skin/hair/nails from more animal protein, better energy and libido from organs, fat
loss, and improved GI symptoms when vegetables are replaced with fruit as the carbohydrate source
— illustrated by his girlfriend's reported results.** `[00:00]` "Women thrive on this type of
diet... When they include organs, they have better energy, better libido... My girlfriend eats an
animal-based diet of meat, organs, fruit, and honey, and she literally kicks ass on this."

## The named benefits

`[00:00]` — More animal protein: better skin, hair, nails. Organs: better energy, better libido.
Overall animal-food increase: often weight/fat loss. Eliminating vegetables for fruit as the
carbohydrate source: frequently reported GI gas/bloating improvement. Girlfriend's specific
reported gains: strength, endurance, athletic performance.
