# Avoid the ROOTS of plants!

## Central claim

**Surveying a range of plant roots and related vegetables, the host argues none belong in an
optimal human diet, citing named toxic compounds for several — cassava's cyanide-related
compounds linked to a specific disease (konzo) and thyroid effects, and potatoes' solanine/chaconine
linked to autoimmune disease triggering — while naming several others (sweet potato, carrots,
beets, turmeric, ginger, garlic, onions) more briefly.** `[00:00]` "I am not a fan of eating
vegetables. These are the leaves, stems, seeds, and roots of plants. Because they contain defense
chemicals... I don't believe that any of these are part of an optimal human diet."
