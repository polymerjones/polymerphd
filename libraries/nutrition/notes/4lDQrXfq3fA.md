# Homemade healthy air freshener

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates a homemade air freshener — cotton balls soaked in a mix of coarse salt,
baking soda, vinegar, and eucalyptus essential oil in a repurposed plastic container — as a
substitute for fabric softener or perfume-based air fresheners, which he describes as full of
endocrine disrupting chemicals.** `[00:00]` "Instead of using garbage fabric softener or perfume
full of endocrine disrupting chemicals, I'm just going to use a couple of drops of eucalyptus
essential oil."

## The recipe

`[00:00]` — Cotton balls in a repurposed plastic container (poked with holes), combined with
roughly two tablespoons of coarse salt, one tablespoon of baking soda, one tablespoon of vinegar,
and a few drops of eucalyptus essential oil. The host adds that eucalyptus is also reported to
repel bugs and spiders.
