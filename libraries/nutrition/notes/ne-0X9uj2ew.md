# Animal-based day of eating!

## Central claim

**A quantified full day of eating — organs, red meat, bone marrow, several fruits, honey, and goat
kefir, with no supplements — presented as the diet that allows humans to "fully thrive," with the
host's lack of drug/hormone/supplement use cited as evidence of the diet's sufficiency.** `[00:00]`
"I'm 44 years old, I take no drugs, no hormones, and I'm a double board certified medical doctor. I
firmly believe that an animal-based diet like this is how humans fully thrive."

## The full day, quantified

`[00:00]` — Organs: 0.5-1 oz raw liver, 2-3 oz raw testicle, 2-4 oz raw heart. Muscle meat: 2 lb
grass-fed ribeye or ground beef with sea salt, plus bone marrow "most days." Fruit: ~0.5 pineapple,
~0.5 papaya, 2-3 mangoes, 5-6 bananas. Sweetener: 2-3 tbsp raw honey. Dairy: 3 glasses goat's milk
kefir. **Stated fallback:** desiccated organ supplements from "hardened soil supplements" when
fresh organs aren't available.
