# Less than $25/day for the top tier animal-based diet

## Central claim

**Responding to the objection that an animal-based diet is too expensive, the host itemizes a
same-day grocery list totaling $23.25 — two pounds of grass-fed ground beef, an ounce of raw liver,
two ounces of raw heart (or desiccated organ supplements), and a mix of fruit and fermented dairy —
framing this as "the most nutrient-rich diet on the plan[et]."** `[00:00]` "For you math nerds,
that's $23.25 for the most nutrient-rich diet on the plant. It's worth within seven minutes."

## The itemized list

`[00:00]` — Two pounds grass-fed ground beef ($6/lb, $12), one ounce raw liver ($8/lb, $0.50), two
ounces raw heart ($8/lb, $1) — or desiccated organs from Heart and Soil supplements as an
alternative (~$1.50/day). Fruit and other additions: papaya ($4), pineapple ($3), coconut ($0.50),
four bananas ($0.75), a plantain ($0.75), goat's milk kefir ($0.50), and two tablespoons raw honey
($0.25). Total: $23.25.
