# Fruit Loops = behavior issues in kids?

## Central claim

**The host highlights that Fruit Loops contains high fructose corn syrup, seed oils, and multiple
artificial dyes (Yellow 6, Red 40, Red 3, Blue 1) — some of which have been linked to hyperactivity
and behavioral problems in children — questioning why this is fed to kids and considered healthy for
being low in fat.** `[00:00]` "This is what we're feeding our children? This is what we consider
healthy because it's low in fat?"
