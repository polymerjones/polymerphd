# Doctors don't have time…

## Central claim

**The host argues the structural constraint of a 7-10 minute doctor visit — not just individual
doctors' choices — drives a symptom-treatment-only healthcare pattern, since there's no time to ask
about diet or lifestyle, so patients continue eating the foods likely causing their issues while
taking medication that only masks symptoms.** `[00:00]` "Doctors only have seven to 10 minutes to
spend with you. This isn't even close to enough time for your doctor to understand the nuances of
your symptoms and to uncover the true root cause of your issues."

## The structural argument

`[00:00]` — The core claim is structural rather than about individual doctor competence: **7-10
minute visits** don't allow time to ask about diet or lifestyle, so doctors "immediately reach for a
prescription pad" based on symptoms alone. The downstream effect: patients keep eating whatever is
driving their issues while medicating only the symptom, so "things don't get better because the
root cause of your issues was not addressed."
