# Beware of “Coconut Oil” SPRAY

## Central claim

**At Trader Joe's, the host contrasts plain extra virgin coconut oil (acceptable, though he prefers
animal fats like ghee, butter, or tallow) with coconut oil spray, which contains natural flavor, soy
lecithin, and propellant — arguing convenience formats hide additional processing.** `[00:00]` "As
we see so often, convenience is the enemy of optimal health."

## The comparison

`[00:00]` — **Extra virgin coconut oil**: described as "okay," though the host states a preference
for animal fats (ghee, butter, tallow). **Coconut oil spray**: marketed as a simpler cooking option,
but contains natural flavor, soy lecithin, and propellant — none of which are present in the plain
oil — prompting the rhetorical question "who wants to eat propellant in their coconut oil spray?"
