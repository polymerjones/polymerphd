# Do plants give gorillas muscles??

## Central claim

**Responding to the common "gorillas are muscular and only eat plants" argument, the host counters
that gorillas eat 45-65 lb of leaves per day (far more than any human could consume), ferment it in
a large "pot belly," lack visible abs, and have digestive anatomy fundamentally different from
humans — whose digestive system he compares instead to a dog's, with a larger small intestine and
smaller large intestine than a gorilla's — concluding humans are optimized for meat and organs, not
leaves.** `[00:00]` "Have you ever seen a gorilla with six-pack abs? No, you haven't... the human
digestive system is nothing like a gorilla's. Our digestive system is much more like a dog's...
That means we can get six-packs and we are optimally adapted to eating nutrient-rich meat and
organs."

## The comparison

`[00:00]` — Gorilla: 20-30 kg (45-65 lb) of leaves per day, fermented in an enlarged gut, producing
a "huge pot belly" rather than visible muscle definition. Human: compared anatomically to a dog
(larger small intestine relative to a much smaller large intestine than a gorilla's), framed as
built for nutrient-dense meat/organ digestion rather than bulk leaf fermentation.

Low-to-moderate — the stated gorilla leaf-intake volume (45-65 lb/day) is broadly consistent with
published primatology figures on gorilla foraging, and the human-gut-resembles-a-dog's-more-than-
a-gorilla's comparison reflects real relative-gut-proportion differences between hindgut fermenters
(gorillas) and simpler-gut omnivores, though no specific comparative anatomy source is named in this
