# Ground beef is amazing

## Central claim

**The host argues ground beef is underappreciated because it's typically made from cuts with more
connective tissue and collagen — providing glycine, which is used to make glutathione, a major
antioxidant — and that a butcher relationship allows requesting organs like heart and liver ground
in, plus a specific fat content, calling it "the most affordable" way to get these benefits.**
`[00:00]` "Ground beef is where it is at, guys."

## The case for ground beef

`[00:00]` — Butchers typically use cuts with more **connective tissue** for ground beef —
tissue that's often left out of the diet otherwise — providing **collagen** and, from that
collagen, **glycine**, which the host says is used to make **glutathione**, described as "a huge
antioxidant currency in your body." A cooperative butcher can grind in **heart and liver** on
request, and adjust fat content — the host states he typically requests **30% fat**, arguing lean
ground beef (what "most people end up eating") misses out on valuable animal fat.
