# Is Elon Musk wrong about THIS?? 😳

## Central claim

**Responding to a quoted remark dismissing cell phone radiation risk to fertility (attributed to
Elon Musk), the host argues this is wrong, citing an association between cell phone radiation and
lower testosterone in men, plus a specific sperm study finding DNA damage and impaired motility
after prolonged phone exposure.** `[00:00]` "Cell phone radiation is clearly associated with lower
testosterone in men."

## The cited sperm study

`[00:00]` — Study design as described: **two groups of sperm from the same men**, both kept in a
warm incubator; one group had a **cell phone placed nearby, powered on, for five hours**. The
exposed group showed **more DNA damage** and **clearly impaired motility** compared to the control
group. The host frames the phone carried in a pocket, and phone calls held to the head, as **the
largest RF-EMF exposure routes for most people** — day-long, close-proximity exposure rather than a
brief one.
