# 5 foods you wouldn't expect to have added processed sugars.

## Central claim

**Reading labels at a regular grocery store, the host finds processed sugar in five products not
typically flagged as sweet — Whole Foods granola (second ingredient), ketchup (corn syrup), a
"bougie" nut snack, hot sauce (second ingredient), and even regular (non-Frosted) corn flakes
(second ingredient) — arguing sugar "sneaks into" foods marketed as healthy.** `[00:00]`
"Processed sugars and seed oils are the single greatest drivers of chronic illness in the U.S. and
the world today... Sugar's in everything. It's sneaking into all your foods."

## The five products

`[00:00]` — Whole Foods granola (sugar as 2nd ingredient), ketchup (corn syrup), an unnamed
"bougie" nut snack (brown/organic cane sugar, "still processed sugar"), hot sauce (sugar as 2nd
ingredient), and regular corn flakes (sugar as 2nd ingredient, distinguished from the more
obviously sweet Frosted Flakes).
