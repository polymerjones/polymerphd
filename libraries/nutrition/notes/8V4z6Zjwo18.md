# Seed Oil Awareness..

## Central claim

**Walking a regular supermarket, the host finds seed oils in five common pantry items people
might not expect — salad dressing, mayonnaise, roasted cashews, a "health" drink, and Alaskan
salmon burgers — framing seed oils as the number one cause of obesity, diabetes, chronic disease,
and cardiovascular disease in the US.** `[00:00]` "Even the nuts are hiding [seed oils]."

## The five products checked

`[00:00]` — **Zesty Italian dressing**: vegetable oil, second ingredient. **Mayonnaise** (labeled
"real mayonnaise"): soybean oil, first ingredient. **Cashews**: canola, peanut, and/or cottonseed
oil. **A "Root Health O-Trink" (transcribed) health drink**: sunflower oil. **Alaskan salmon
burgers**: vegetable oil (soybean and/or canola), second ingredient — flagged specifically as a
product marketed as healthy.
