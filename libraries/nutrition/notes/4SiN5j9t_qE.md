# Do not eat plant-based meat

## Central claim

**Comparing ingredient labels side by side, the host argues grass-fed ground beef "wins in every
single way" over a wheat-gluten-based plant-based ground beef, citing beef's naturally occurring
nutrients against the plant product's lack of them, plus specific anti-nutrient concerns (lectins,
seed oils) in the plant version.** `[00:00]` "This is nothing like this. This is the real
superfood."

## The comparison

`[00:00]` — **Grass-fed beef ingredients**: beef, single ingredient. **Nutrients**: vitamin B12,
vitamin K2, riboflavin, vitamin B6, heme iron, creatine, carnitine, carnosine (transcribed
"ansarine"), taurine, and bioavailable animal-based protein. **Plant-based product ingredients**:
gluten, nutritional yeast, sunflower oil, natural smoke flavor. **Nutrients**: the host states there
isn't much "that isn't added synthetically when wheat gluten is your first ingredient," and flags
**lectins** (harmful for the gut) and **seed oils** (causing issues in cell membranes and
mitochondria) as anti-nutrient concerns specific to this product.
