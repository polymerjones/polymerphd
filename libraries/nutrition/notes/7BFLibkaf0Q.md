# Fasting is overrated

## Central claim

**In a rapid "overrated or underrated" format, the host rates AG1 (overrated, tested for heavy
metals), hyperbaric oxygen therapy (overrated, disputing its telomere-lengthening mechanism),
fasting (overrated, citing cortisol/glucagon/catecholamine stress), olive oil (properly rated, with
sourcing caveats), and mouth taping (properly rated) — recommending whole animal foods over AG1 and
a simple dinner-to-breakfast overnight fast rather than extended fasting.** `[00:00]` "Eat animal
foods, eggs, milk, butter, red meat. That's the real superfoods."

## The five ratings

`[00:00]` — **AG1**: overrated, "full of heavy metals," per the host's own testing. **Hyperbaric
oxygen**: overrated; the host disputes the telomere-lengthening claim, arguing the studies instead
show the therapy "killing off the cells that have the shortest telomeres" — cells he says "serve a
valuable role" — rather than genuinely lengthening telomeres. **Fasting**: overrated, described as
creating meaningful physiological stress (cortisol, glucagon, catecholamines); his own practice is
limited to the natural overnight gap between dinner (finished about two hours before bed) and
breakfast (eaten first thing on waking) rather than extended fasting. **Olive oil**: "properly
rated," conditional on being organic, cold-pressed, single-source, in an opaque glass bottle, and
never heated. **Mouth taping**: "properly rated," recommended to prevent mouth breathing during
sleep.
