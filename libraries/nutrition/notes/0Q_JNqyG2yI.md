# What's your dental floss made of?

## Central claim

**Citing an independent brand analysis and a specific study, the host argues several major dental
floss brands — Oral-B Glide worst among them — contain significant PFAS ("forever chemicals")
associated with endocrine disruption, and recommends switching to a silk floss to reduce both PFAS
and microplastic exposure.** `[00:00]` "Using this dental floss is basically like eating Teflon
twice a day."

## The cited evidence

`[00:00]` — An **independent analysis** is cited testing several brands: **Oral-B Glide** had "a
huge amount" of PFAS; **Colgate**, **Up & Up** (store brand), and **Salimo** (an Amazon brand) also
showed "significant amounts." Separately, **a study from a few years ago** found women who used
Oral-B Glide floss had **significantly higher blood PFAS levels**. The host recommends a silk floss
instead (referred to in the transcript as "soak floss," likely a mishearing of "silk floss"),
disclosing **no financial association with the brand** he uses.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

None named as an individually reported symptom — the concern (endocrine/hormonal disruption,
learning delay in children) is framed at the population/mechanism level.

## Glossary terms introduced

None distinct — PFAS is named without a full standalone definition in this clip.

## Source-stated confidence

Moderate. The Oral-B Glide/blood-PFAS study is specific enough (a named brand, a measured outcome
in a named population) to be a real, checkable citation, and is independently corroborated
elsewhere in this library. The independent brand-testing analysis is described but not named by
organization in this short.

## Conflicts with other sources

`Ry4G6ljw5EA` ("The dangers of PFAS in fish") and `jzszqg-xQGM` ("Why I don't eat fish, chicken or
pork!") both independently cite the same underlying study — women using Oral-B Glide floss showing
higher PFAS blood levels — with `jzszqg-xQGM` specifying the measured compound as **PFHxS
(perfluorohexane sulfonic acid)**, a detail this short doesn't include. This short adds three
additional flagged brands (Colgate, Up & Up, Salimo) that neither of those notes names, and
recommends silk floss specifically as the alternative, which neither of the other two notes
mentions.
