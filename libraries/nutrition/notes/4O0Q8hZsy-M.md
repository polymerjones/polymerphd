# How to reverse low testosterone

## Central claim

**If he woke up with low testosterone, the host says he'd correct diet first (animal-based, no
seed oils/dyes/processed food), then sleep consistency, avoiding alcohol and drugs (marijuana named
specifically as lowering testosterone), managing blue light at night, daytime sun exposure,
consistent exercise, and grounding — stating his own testosterone, at 47, is around 800 with no
exogenous hormones, through intentional effort.** `[00:00]` "If you have low testosterone, it is
correctable. Just change the way you eat and the way you live."

## The protocol

`[00:00]` — In the order given: **diet** (animal-based, eliminating seed oils, food dyes, processed
food); **sleep** (consistent bedtime, sufficient duration); **substance avoidance** (alcohol, drugs,

night to protect sleep; **daytime sunlight** exposure; **consistent exercise**; and **grounding**
(bare feet on the ground). He states his own testosterone at **age 47 is around 800 ng/dL**, with
no exogenous hormones, attributing it to sustained, intentional effort across all of these levers.
