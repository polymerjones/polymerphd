# Find and fix the root cause!

## Central claim

**Using a car-mechanic analogy — a mechanic who fixes engine damage without noticing the wrong oil
was used will see the customer return with the same problem — the host argues Western medicine
similarly treats symptoms with pills without addressing food and lifestyle as root causes of
diabetes, obesity, and cardiovascular disease, and calls for mainstream medical training to
change.** `[00:00]` "Mainstream training of physicians does not teach us as doctors to look for the
root cause of illness. Mainstream medical training needs to change."

## The analogy

`[00:00]` — Wrong oil in a car → engine problems → a mechanic who fixes the engine damage without
identifying the oil as the cause → the customer returns with the same problem. Mapped directly onto
medicine: wrong food → illness → a doctor who prescribes a pill for the symptom without addressing
diet → the patient returns with "worsening issues later." The host states plainly: **"doctors need
to be taught that food and lifestyle are the major drivers of illness like diabetes, obesity,
cardiovascular disease in humans."**
