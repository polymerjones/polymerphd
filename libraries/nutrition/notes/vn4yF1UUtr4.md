# Honey is definitely not BS

## Central claim

**Raw, unpasteurized, local honey is defended as a genuinely good food for humans, on the grounds
that fructose consumed within honey's "food matrix" behaves differently in the body than isolated
fructose in table sugar, and that honey has historically been sought and treasured by
hunter-gatherer groups such as the Hadza.** `[00:00]` "There's clear evidence from the medical
literature that fructose in the food matrix performs very differently than fructose in table sugar
in isolation."

None named in this clip.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Food matrix** — used to describe the surrounding structure/composition of a whole food (here,
  honey) that is argued to change how a component like fructose is metabolized, compared to that
  same component isolated `[00:00]`.
