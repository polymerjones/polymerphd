# Lab-grown meat is a crime in Florida

## Central claim

**Praising Florida Governor DeSantis's 2024 law banning the manufacture and sale of cultivated
(lab-grown) meat, the host cites a 2023 UC Davis study finding lab-grown meat has a significantly
higher carbon footprint than regular grain-fed beef (not even compared against carbon-negative/
neutral grass-fed beef), and argues it's dishonest to claim lab-grown meat matches the full
nutritional profile of conventionally raised, pasture-fed animals.** `[00:00]` "Grass-fed,
grass-finished is clearly the best for the environment from a carbon perspective, and it is full of
so many nutrients that help humans thrive. Lab-grown meat is a complete joke."

## The cited study and the nutrition claim

`[00:00]` — **Cited study**: a 2023 University of California, Davis study finding lab-grown meat has
a "much higher carbon footprint" than regular grain-fed beef — with the host noting the comparison
was to grain-fed beef, not the even more favorable grass-fed comparison. **Nutrition claim**: the
host argues it is "an insult to our intelligence and a bold-faced lie" to claim meat grown in a
culture medium contains the same vitamins, minerals, nutrients, and cofactors as meat from
pasture-raised animals (grass-fed cows, bug-and-worm-fed outdoor chickens).
