# How I recover so quickly 🤒

## Central claim

**Asked how he recovers from illness so quickly, the host describes prioritizing sleep, sunlight,
light exercise (when energy allows), and nutrient-dense food, with only two supplements he
specifically reaches for when sick: extra colostrum, for its immunoglobulin content, and
desiccated thymus when available.** `[00:00]` "Those are the only two supplements I'll think about
when I'm sick and letting my body take care of it."

## The routine

`[00:00]` — Named triggers for getting sick: **airports, travel, and poor sleep**. When sick, the
host's stated priorities: sleep quality (getting extra sleep), sunlight exposure, exercise "if I
have energy" (explicitly scaled back when low-energy), and continuing to eat nutrient-rich foods —
vitamin C from orange juice, meat, and organs specifically named. The only two supplements
mentioned: **extra colostrum**, described as "immunoglobulin-rich," and **desiccated thymus** when
available.
