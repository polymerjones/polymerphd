# My favorite laundry hack

## Central claim

**The host argues mainstream laundry detergents contain phthalates and 1,4-dioxane at levels high
enough that multiple products are illegal to sell in New York state, and recommends replacing
detergent entirely with two to three tablespoons of white vinegar per load.** `[00:00]` "Mainstream
laundry detergents [are] full of garbage chemicals, full of phthalates... almost all of them are
full of 1,4-Dioxane."

## The claim and the fix

`[00:00]` — **Phthalates**: described as fragrance-related hormone disruptors present in "almost
all" mainstream detergents. **1,4-Dioxane**: the host states multiple detergent brands are illegal
to sell in New York specifically because they exceed that state's 1,4-dioxane limit — a specific,
checkable regulatory claim, though no brand is named. **The fix**: two to three tablespoons of
white vinegar per laundry load, used in place of detergent. The host addresses the obvious
objection directly — clothes don't end up smelling like vinegar or acidic.
