# Whiten your teeth with this toothpaste

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates a five-ingredient homemade whitening toothpaste — coconut oil, bentonite
clay, diluted (3%) food-grade hydrogen peroxide, baking soda, and peppermint essential oil — as an
alternative to "garbage fluoride filled toothpaste."** `[00:00]` "Let me show you how to make a
natural toothpaste that will whiten your teeth like crazy."

## The recipe

`[00:00]` — Three heaping tablespoons **coconut oil** (credited with antimicrobial properties), two
tablespoons **baking soda** (a gentle abrasive), two tablespoons **bentonite clay** (its negative
charge said to pull metals and toxins from the mouth and gums), mixed with a **wood, not metal,
spoon** — the concern being that the clay could pull trace metals out of a metal spoon during
mixing. Diluted **hydrogen peroxide** (to ~3%) is added as the whitening agent, and **peppermint
essential oil** for flavor.
