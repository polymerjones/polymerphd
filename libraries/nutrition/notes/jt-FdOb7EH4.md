# Why would I take cheat days? #shorts #health #carnivorediet

## Central claim

**The host states he never takes cheat days, arguing that since his regular diet (organs, meat,
fruit, honey, raw dairy) already consists of foods he considers the most sought-after by humans
throughout evolution, a cheat day would only trade lasting wellbeing for a brief "dopaminergic,
drug-like" effect followed by feeling worse.** `[00:00]` "It's not worth it to me to sacrifice the
way that I feel for something that has a dopaminergic, a drug-like effect with the food for a few
minutes, and then leaves me feeling shittier afterwards."

## The behavioral argument

`[00:00]` — Cheat days are reframed as a sign of being "stuck" in a dietary change rather than a
reward: **"I think they're holding you back... Make an intentional decision with your diet and
don't use food as entertainment. That's how you take the first step toward behavioral change and
thriving."**

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Feeling "shittier" after eating cheat-day food**, contrasted with how the host reports feeling
  on his regular diet `[00:00]`.
