# This is the world's OLDEST SUPERFOOD #animalbased

## Central claim

**The host argues liver is "the original superfood" — the food that concentrates bioavailable
nutrients most densely — describing a first-hand experience with the Hadza where liver was treated
with reverence ("placed on a rock like a piece of gold... treated as sacred"), and contrasts this
with modern practice, where liver is often discarded to pets, arguing muscle meat alone (iron, zinc,
B12, K2) is missing nutrients — biotin, riboflavin, copper, bioavailable vitamin A — found mainly in
liver.** `[00:00]` "If you truly want to thrive, remember what your ancestors were doing, and get
some organs to go with your muscle meat."

## The Hadza account and the nutrient case

`[00:00]` — First-hand account: during time spent with the Hadza, **"no liver went wasted... every
liver was treated with care,"** placed on a rock and shared among the tribe. Nutrient comparison:
**red meat** provides iron, zinc, vitamin B12, and vitamin K2, but **liver specifically** is needed
for **biotin, riboflavin, copper** (to balance zinc intake), and **bioavailable vitamin A** — framed
as nutrients "really only found in liver and a few other foods."
