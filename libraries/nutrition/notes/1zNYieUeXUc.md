# Avoid these "healthy" brands at all costs

## Central claim

**Citing a UC Davis study, the host reports 89% of tested avocado oil products — including chips,
mayonnaise, and salad dressings from named brands — were adulterated with seed oils rather than
being pure avocado oil, consistent with two earlier studies (2020, 2023) finding 70-80% of shelf
and private-label avocado oils similarly cut, and recommends avoiding avocado oil entirely for now
in favor of butter, tallow, ghee, or extra virgin olive oil.** `[00:00]` "89% of them looked like
they were cut with seed oils, sunflower, safflower, etc."

## The cited studies and named brands

`[00:00]` — **UC Davis study** (most recent, unnamed date in this clip): tested "many of the brands
that a lot of us use," specifically naming **Siete, Primal Kitchen**, and **Chosen Foods** among
avocado oil and mayonnaise brands tested, finding **89%** showed a fatty-acid composition
consistent with seed oil adulteration (sunflower, safflower named). **Two earlier studies (2020 and
2023)**, also unnamed by author, found **70-80%** of shelf and private-label avocado oils cut with
seed oils. The host's interim recommendation: avoid avocado oil and use butter, tallow, ghee, or
extra virgin olive oil instead "until we get the full results."

## Source-stated confidence

Moderate. The UC Davis Olive Center (also cited elsewhere in this library for olive oil testing) is
a real, named, checkable institution, and the specific brands named (Siete, Primal Kitchen, Chosen
Foods) make this a concrete, falsifiable claim rather than a vague generalization. The two earlier
studies are described but not attributed to specific authors or journals in this transcript.

## Conflicts with other sources

`JpW1utNfQM0` ("Avoid These Olive Oils") independently names both **Primal Kitchen** and **Chosen
Foods** avocado oil among brands tested for a different contaminant (phthalates, via plastic
contact during pressing/storage) — that note gives Chosen Foods roughly 1,000+ ng phthalates and
Primal Kitchen roughly 2,000 ng, figures that are a distinct concern from this short's seed-oil-
adulteration finding, but concern the same two brands. Together the two notes suggest these
specific avocado oil brands may carry multiple, independent quality issues rather than a single
one. `_TO-GoZo8Lw` ("How to shop for extra virgin olive oil!") separately cites the same UC Davis
Olive Center for olive oil quality testing, suggesting this is an ongoing testing program covering
multiple oil categories.
