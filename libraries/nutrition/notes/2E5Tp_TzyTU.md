# Animal-based restaurant in Texas

## Central claim

**The host visits and endorses a seed-oil-free animal-based restaurant chain in Austin — cooking
chicken in butter, using fresh organic ingredients with no seed oils in the fryers, and offering a
Wagyu burger and raw milk — framing its growth to 10 Austin locations as evidence of a broader
consumer shift away from seed-oil restaurants.** `[00:00]` "I'm stoked that animal-based restaurants
are taking over."

## What the restaurant offers

`[00:00]` — Chicken grilled with butter, no seed oils used anywhere including the fryers, "fresh
organic ingredients," Greek and Mexican-style sauces, a Wagyu burger, raw milk, and smoothies. The
owner (per the host, secondhand) reports receiving emails from customers who say they're switching
from other chains — **Chipotle named specifically** — that cook with seed oils.
