# Nighttime blue light iPhone hack

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates a triple-click iPhone shortcut that toggles a low-blue-light, low-
brightness display filter on and off, used in the evening to protect circadian rhythm and wind
down for sleep.** `[00:00]` "I take the blue light and the brightness off my phone to protect my
circadian rhythm."

## How to set it up

`[00:00]` — Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Color Filters, turned on with
intensity set to "one notch from all the way." Then, back in Accessibility → Accessibility
Shortcuts, selecting Color Filters enables a triple-click of the side button to toggle the low-
blue-light, low-brightness mode on and off at will — used specifically "when the sun is setting and
at night."

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Difficulty winding down / getting to sleep** — attributed to blue light and phone brightness in
  the evening, addressed by dimming/filtering the display before bed `[00:00]`.
