# Use sunscreens based in zinc and animal fats #shorts #health #sunscreen

## Central claim

**Most commercial sunscreens are argued to be harmful rather than protective: their chemical
active ingredients absorb through skin into the bloodstream and are excreted in urine/feces,
several are named as endocrine disruptors, and seed oils in some formulations are argued to
increase sun damage via linoleic acid incorporation into skin cell membranes — with zinc- and
animal-fat-based sunscreen offered as the alternative.** `[00:00]` "Most sunscreen is pure
bullshit... Protect your skin by using sunscreens based in zinc and animal fats."

## The named compounds

`[00:00]` — Absorbed-and-excreted, associated with cancer risk and endocrine disruption: homosalate,
octocrylene, avobenzone, and a fourth compound transcribed as "octabenzone" (likely oxybenzone).
Also named: parabens ("other endocrine disruptors, xenoestrogens") and seed oils high in linoleic
acid, argued to incorporate into cell membranes and increase sun-damage susceptibility.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

None named directly — the claim is about ingredient absorption and hormonal disruption risk, not a
specific symptom.

## Source-stated confidence

Low — the compounds are named specifically, but no study, agency, or specific cancer/hormonal
finding is cited for any of them, and "octabenzone" does not match a standard sunscreen ingredient
name (likely a transcription artifact for oxybenzone or octinoxate).

## Conflicts with other sources

`4G7i5d7LBgs` ("Regular sunscreen is a scam") independently makes the same core claim citing a
specific study finding sunscreen chemicals (avobenzone, oxybenzone, octocrylene, and a fourth
compound) present in over 80% of sunscreens and absorbed into skin and blood — a fuller, cited
version of this clip's uncited claim, naming three of the same four compounds and recommending the
same non-nano zinc oxide alternative.
