# Regular sunscreen is a scam

## Central claim

**Citing a study, the host argues four common chemical sunscreen ingredients — avobenzone,
oxybenzone, octocrylene, and a fourth compound (transcribed "escamsol"/"scamsol," likely
ensulizole or a similarly named UV filter) — absorb into skin and bloodstream and are present in
over 80% of sunscreens, all flagged as potential endocrine disruptors, while explicitly not
dismissing sunburn as a real risk — recommending mineral sunscreens with non-nano zinc oxide as the
safer choice.** `[00:00]` "More than 80% of sunscreens contain potential hormone-disrupting
chemicals that are absorbed through the skin."

## The cited findings

`[00:00]` — A study (unnamed by author/journal) found avobenzone, oxybenzone, octocrylene, and a
fourth chemical absorb into skin and bloodstream after sunscreen application. All four are named as
**potential endocrine disruptors**, present in **over 80%** of commercially available sunscreens,
and stated to be excreted in urine and feces after absorption. The host is explicit this isn't an
argument against sun protection generally — **"sunburn is a real thing that is harmful for humans
that we should not take lightly"** — but a call to read ingredient labels and prefer **mineral
sunscreens with non-nano zinc oxide**.
