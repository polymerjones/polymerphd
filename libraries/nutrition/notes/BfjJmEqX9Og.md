# Animal-based at Sam’s Club

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates that an affordable, high-quality animal-based diet is achievable at Sam's
Club — grass-fed beef at $4.80/lb, raw cheese, Parmigiano-Reggiano, garlic pickles, fruit, and maple
syrup — while flagging garlic as a potential gut irritant for some people and explaining why
non-organic avocados and bananas are lower-risk choices due to their peels.** `[00:00]` "Even at
Sam's Club, you can find a really good animal-based diet. You can eat this way anywhere, very
affordably."

## The haul and the caveats

`[00:00]` — Meat: grass-fed beef burger patties at $4.80/lb. Dairy: raw cheese, Parmigiano-Reggiano.
Other: garlic pickles (with an explicit caveat — "if you've got gut issues and garlic is going to
irritate your gut, maybe don't do it," noting the garlic only flavors the pickle brine and isn't
directly eaten), organic strawberries, non-organic avocados and bananas (justified as lower pesticide
risk since their skins aren't eaten), organic maple syrup. Explicitly skipped: Kodiak Power Cakes,
due to gluten and wheat content.
