# Animal-based diet = optimal health

## Central claim

**Addressing people who say they "could never" eat the way he does, the host argues that most
people never attain their potential energy, mental clarity, libido, or happiness because they
haven't recognized diet as the single biggest lever — bigger than sleep, community, sunlight, or
environment — for reducing "neural inflammation" and reaching optimal health.** `[00:00]` "I
strongly believe that the biggest lever for all of this for humans is what we eat... an
evolutionarily consistent diet of organs, meat, fruit, honey that aligns your genetics [with] where
you come from for millions of years. That is how humans thrive."

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

  linked to diet `[00:00]`.
