# Reversing Crohn's disease!

## Central claim

**A patient with 20 years of Crohn's disease describes a severe flare requiring an ileostomy after
a doctor-prescribed liquid diet (which the patient says was full of low-quality ingredients) failed
to help — followed by switching to eating meat, after which the ileostomy was surgically
reversed.** `[00:00]` "I had an ileostomy in August... and then went on the diet, ate meat, and
they were able to do a reversal."

## The account

`[00:00]` — 20 years with Crohn's disease. A recent flare caused a 50-pound weight loss. The
patient's doctor prescribed a **three-month liquid diet**, which the patient describes critically
as containing low-quality ingredients ("filled with all these bullshit... so many garbage things").
The disease progressed to the point where the large colon was severely diseased, requiring an
**ileostomy**. After switching to eating meat, the ileostomy was **surgically reversed**. The
patient expresses surprise that this outcome is possible and states doctors never discussed this
option.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Severe Crohn's flare requiring ileostomy** — self-reported as resolved to the point of surgical
  reversal after switching to a meat-based diet `[00:00]`.
- **50 lbs of weight loss** — reported during the disease flare, prior to the dietary change
  `[00:00]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Ileostomy** — a surgical procedure diverting the small intestine to an opening in the abdominal
  wall, mentioned here as both required by disease progression and later reversed `[00:00]`.
