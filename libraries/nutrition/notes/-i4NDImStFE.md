# You crave meat for a reason…

## Central claim

**Citing a study using brain electrodes, the host argues vegans and vegetarians consciously reject
meat but still show a positive, evolutionarily rooted unconscious response to it and to
nutrient-rich foods like organs — which he offers as the explanation for why the most successful
plant-based brands make products that visually mimic meat.** `[00:00]` "It's okay to eat meat...
Please do not fear meat and organs. These are the foods that help humans thrive."

## The cited study and its use

`[00:00]` — Study description: electrodes placed on vegan/vegetarian subjects showed a **conscious
aversion to meat**, but **"at a deep-seated evolutionary level, they still have a positive response
to things like meat and nutrient-rich foods like organs."** The host uses this to explain why
plant-based meat companies specifically design products to *look like* meat and steak — targeting
an unconscious craving he says persists "at some unconscious level" even among people who
consciously avoid the real thing.
