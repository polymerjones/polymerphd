# NEVER give baby formulas to your children - here is WHY

## Central claim

**The host argues the top-selling U.S. infant formula brand contains high-linoleic-acid seed oils
(safflower and soybean oil), which he says accumulate in babies' fatty tissue and cell/mitochondrial
membranes, predisposing them to obesity, childhood diabetes, and chronic illness — and calls for
seed oils to be removed from children's diets entirely.** `[00:00]` "This is the way that we
handicap our children and predispose them to obesity, childhood diabetes, and a host of chronic
illnesses."

## The specific product and mechanism

`[00:00]` — The **number one selling infant formula brand** (unnamed by brand in this clip) is shown
containing **safflower oil and soybean oil**, described as "some of the worst seed oils when it
comes to high levels of linoleic acid." The proposed mechanism: linoleic acid accumulates directly
in babies' fatty tissue and in cell membranes, including mitochondrial membranes, "setting them up
for possible obesity and metabolic dysfunction in childhood."

## Symptoms and body signals addressed
