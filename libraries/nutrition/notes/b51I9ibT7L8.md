# Stop using sunscreen: here is why

## Central claim

**The host argues mainstream sunscreens are problematic because multiple studies have found their
chemical ingredients in blood plasma — ingredients that are potentially carcinogenic, already
outlawed in Europe, and potentially hormone-disrupting — and offers a simple heuristic: avoid any
sunscreen with ingredients other than zinc.** `[00:00]` "If you see a sunscreen, it has any funny
words other than zinc. Don't use that one."

`[00:00]` — Cited concern: "multiple studies" showing mainstream sunscreen ingredients detected in
