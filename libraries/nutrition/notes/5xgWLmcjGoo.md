# Is saturated fat healthy?

## Central claim

**The host argues whether rising cholesterol from eating more saturated fat (and less seed oil)
matters for cardiovascular risk depends on metabolic health — citing multiple studies showing the
cholesterol/cardiovascular-disease relationship is "massively attenuated or not even present" in
metabolically healthy people, and a specific study finding no LDL/cardiovascular-disease
relationship in people with a coronary artery calcium score of zero.** `[00:00]` "I don't think
there's a shred of evidence in the medical literature to suggest that's going to increase your rate
of cardiovascular disease [in metabolically healthy people]. Eat your steak, be proud of it."

## The cited evidence and the critique of standard care

`[00:00]` — **Coronary artery calcium score study** (unnamed): among people with a **calcium score
of zero**, no relationship was found between LDL cholesterol levels and cardiovascular disease.
**Broader claim**: "multiple studies" (unnamed) show the cholesterol-cardiovascular link is
substantially weaker or absent in metabolically healthy people generally. **Critique of standard
practice**: doctors typically check cholesterol alone without checking fasting insulin, fasting
blood glucose, or broader metabolic health markers — which the host calls "an incomplete story."
