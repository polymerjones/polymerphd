# What's happening to Americans?

## Central claim

**The host lists a set of declining US population health statistics — obesity, fertility, sperm
count, and autism prevalence — and attributes all of them collectively to over 5,000 largely
untested chemical substances in the food supply, framing whole, unprocessed food as the
straightforward fix.** `[00:00]` "We are being poisoned."

## The statistics cited

`[00:00]` — **Obesity/overweight**: over 70% of American adults, almost 40% of kids. **Fertility**:
down 50% in the last decade. **Sperm counts**: down 60% (timeframe not specified in this clip).
**Autism**: 1 in 32 children, up from 1 in 150 a decade earlier. **Food chemicals**: "over 5,000
substances in our food that the FDA really isn't paying attention to," described as never fully
tested. None of the five statistics is attributed to a specific named source in this transcript.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Obesity/overweight, infertility, low sperm count, and autism** — all listed as population-level
  trends attributed collectively to food chemical exposure, without distinguishing which trend
  might connect to which specific exposure `[00:00]`.
