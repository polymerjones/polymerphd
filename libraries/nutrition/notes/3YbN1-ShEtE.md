# What is beef tallow? #animalbased

## Central claim

**The host explains beef tallow as rendered beef fat (trimmed and boiled down to separate out
connective tissue), crediting it with fat-soluble nutrients (vitamin E, vitamin K2, choline) and
specifically stearic acid, which he says triggers fat burning in human trials and leads to leanness
in animal studies — concluding tallow can help with hunger and weight loss.** `[00:00]` "I believe
there's a good amount of evidence to suggest that eating more beef tallow is a good way to be less
hungry and to lose weight."

## The rendering process and the nutrients

`[00:00]` — Beef fat is trimmed from below the skin, above the muscle, or around the kidneys,
boiled down, and separated from connective tissue to yield tallow. Nutrients named: **bioavailable
vitamin E, vitamin K2, and choline**. **Stearic acid**, an 18-carbon saturated fat, is singled out
as "in human trials triggers fat burning, and in animal studies, leads to leanness of animals" — no
specific study is named in this transcript.
