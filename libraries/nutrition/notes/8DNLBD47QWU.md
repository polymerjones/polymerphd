# How Takis are made 😳

## Central claim

**The host describes Takis as ultra-processed, pesticide-exposed grains fried in "completely
rancid, damaged" seed oils and coated in heavy artificial seasoning (three tons per day, per the
manufacturer, including Red 40), arguing these ingredients dysregulate attention and cause
hyperactivity and misbehavior in kids who eat them.** `[00:00]` "If you wonder why your kids are
misbehaving and they're eating Takis, I have an answer for you."

## The manufacturing process and the behavioral claim

`[00:00]` — Process described: ultra-processed grain baked, then fried in seed oil, then coated
with heavy seasoning — **three tons of seasoning used per day**, containing artificial colorings
including **Red 40**. The host calls these seasoning ingredients "mutant alien ingredients that are
not even food for humans," and attributes attention deficit, hyperactivity, and misbehavior in kids
directly to this ingredient combination.
