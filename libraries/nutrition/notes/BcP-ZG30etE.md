# Are these heart healthy?

## Central claim

**The host critiques the American Heart Association for placing a "heart healthy" approval label on
French fries whose second ingredient is seed oil (canola and sunflower oils), arguing seed oils raise
LDL oxidation and Lp(a) — making the endorsement contradictory.** `[00:00]` "How seed oils can be
heart healthy when they raise oxidation of your LDL and raise LP little a is beyond me... complete
garbage."

## The label critique

`[00:00]` — Reading the product label: canola and sunflower oils listed as the second ingredient in
AHA-approved "heart healthy" French fries. Named mechanism of concern: seed oils raising **LDL
oxidation** and **Lp(a)** (lipoprotein(a)), both cardiovascular risk markers — directly at odds with
a "heart healthy" endorsement. Recommendation: avoid seed-oil-cooked fries; cook fries in tallow
instead.
