# Homemade ground beef with organs #animalbased

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates grinding grass-fed chuck steak with 10% heart and 10% liver into a
homemade "primal burger," arguing the combination covers a broader nutrient profile — muscle meat,
heart, and liver each contributing distinct nutrients — than plain ground beef alone.** `[00:00]`
"I challenge you to find anything more nutritious than that primal burger with heart, liver, and
ground beef that I made myself."

## The recipe and nutrient breakdown

`[00:00]` — Base: chuck steak (a cut "not great for steak," from a grass-fed, grass-finished,
regeneratively raised cow), ground with **10% heart and 10% liver**, mixed by hand, cooked on the
grill. Nutrients attributed to each component: **muscle meat** — zinc, iron, vitamin B12;
**heart** — coenzyme Q10, taurine; **liver** — riboflavin, folate, biotin, vitamin K2, choline,
zinc, and copper (added "to balance" the zinc from the other components).
