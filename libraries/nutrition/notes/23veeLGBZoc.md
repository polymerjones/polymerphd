# What are the best cooking utensils?

## Central claim

**The host argues plastic spatulas contain PVC, brominated compounds, and heavy metals that
transfer into food when heated on a pan, and recommends switching to metal or wood spatulas
instead, framing a melted or burned plastic edge as visible evidence of this transfer.** `[00:00]`
"When you put this on a pan and heat it up next to your food, all of those things can go into your
food."

## What's in a plastic spatula

`[00:00]` — Named components: **PVC**, called "a probable carcinogen"; **brominated compounds**,
described as hormone disruptors; and **heavy metals** — lead, mercury, and antimony. The host points
to a spatula with "a melted, burned edge" as a visible sign that the material degrades under heat
and transfers into food. Recommended replacement: a metal or wood spatula, costing "five or $10."
