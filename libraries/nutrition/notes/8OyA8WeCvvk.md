# Would you eat this?

## Central claim

**The host tears down McDonald's Big Arch burger ingredient by ingredient, finding 16 ingredients
in the sauce (multiple seed oils, sugar, unspecified "natural flavors"), 24 in the bun (versus the
three — wheat, water, salt — that bread "is supposed to have"), and deep-fried, sugar-dusted
battered onions marketed as "crispy onions," concluding this reflects the same pattern of
ingredient complexity found in McDonald's other menu items.** `[00:00]` "Eat single ingredient
foods and eat food with ingredients your great-grandmother would recognize."

## The ingredient breakdown

`[00:00]` — **Sauce**: 16 ingredients, including multiple types of seed oil, sugar, and "natural
flavors" the host says nobody can identify. **Pickles**: questioned for reportedly containing 10
ingredients when "vinegar, salt, and cucumbers" should suffice. **Bun**: 24 ingredients, compared
against a simple bread recipe of wheat, water, and salt. **Crispy onions**: revealed as deep-fried,
battered onions with added dextrose and sugar, rather than a simply prepared topping.
