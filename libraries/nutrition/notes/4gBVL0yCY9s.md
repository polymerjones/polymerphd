# Stop using this for your coffee

## Central claim

**The host argues brewing coffee through plastic Keurig components exposes the coffee to PFAS,
BPA, and BPS as boiling water passes through plastic, and recommends switching to a stainless steel
Keurig basket with organic, mold-free coffee, spring water instead of tap water, and avoiding water
sitting in the plastic reservoir for long periods.** `[00:00]` "Why would you ever put hot water
through a plastic container? Everything from this plastic is now in the coffee."

## The recommended fix

`[00:00]` — Replace the plastic Keurig pod with a **stainless steel Keurig basket**, filled with
organic, mold-free coffee grounds of choice. Use **spring water** rather than tap water. Avoid
letting water sit in the machine's plastic reservoir for extended periods, since it's still in
contact with plastic even with the basket swap. The host discloses he personally doesn't drink
coffee because it disrupts his sleep, but frames this advice for those who do.
