# 🤯 Your Protein Bar Is Made Of WHAT?!?

## Central claim

**Walking through the actual industrial manufacturing process for a conventional protein bar's
ingredients — an indigestible synthetic fat, glyphosate-grown soy processed into lecithin with a
petroleum solvent, and artificial flavor synthesized from a petroleum byproduct — the host argues
these bars are built from chemistry, not food, and contrasts this with his own real-food-ingredient
bar.** `[00:00]` "Let's make your favorite protein bars from scratch at home."

## The synthetic build, step by step

`[00:00]` — Four components, each traced to its industrial origin:
- **Fake fat** — glycerol reacted with propylene oxide gas and a fatty acid, producing a fat "not
  digestible by humans," used specifically to lower the bar's stated calorie count.
- **Soy protein/lecithin** — from soybeans genetically modified to be glyphosate-resistant "so we
  can heavily spray it with a synthetic herbicide," despite (per the host) **Monsanto having paid
  out over $11 billion in legal settlements linking glyphosate to cancer**; the soybeans are then
  washed with **hexane**, "a petroleum-derived solvent," heated, and skimmed to yield soy lecithin.
- **Artificial flavor** — synthesized from **glycol**, described as "a petroleum by-product," via
  "glyoxylic acid oxidation decarboxylation."
- **Everything else** — cheap protein powder ("palmore" in the transcript, unclear spelling),
  artificial sweetener, maltodextrin, xanthan gum, tapioca starch, and unnamed fillers, emulsifiers,
  and binders.

## The alternative

`[01:01]` — The host contrasts this with his own **Lineage** protein bar, made "entirely from real
food ingredients," citing **20 grams of protein, under 200 calories**, and a taste endorsement
("Paul, you guys nailed it. It's absolutely delicious") from an unnamed second voice.
