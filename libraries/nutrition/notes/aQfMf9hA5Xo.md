# I tried this crazy Oreos experiment…

## Central claim

**Testing a viral claim, the host shows an Oreo takes an unusually long time to burn — attributed by
fact-checkers to its sugar content taking time to caramelize — and uses this as a visual illustration
that ultra-processed food, full of seed oils and high fructose corn syrup, behaves differently from
real food (like steak or milk), which he says would burn instantly.** `[00:00]` "That is not food.
You should not be eating this. We are being poisoned by ultra-processed junk food. And this is a
really incredible visual illustration of that."

## The demonstration and its explanation

`[00:00]` — The host attempts to burn an Oreo and finds it takes much longer than expected. He notes
a prior fact-check attributed the slow burn to sugar content requiring time to caramelize rather than
combust immediately. He contrasts this with real food (steak, milk), which he says "would burn
instantly," and names seed oils and high fructose corn syrup as the Oreo's problematic ingredients.
