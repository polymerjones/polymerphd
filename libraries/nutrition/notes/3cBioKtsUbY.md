ranks it as slightly better than coffee (a "burned plant seed") but still recommends most people
avoid it, and separately flags its caffeine content for causing reactive hypoglycemia.** `[00:00]`
"Tea is bullshit... most people are going to do much better without tea."

(shared with coffee) is separately flagged as causing **reactive hypoglycemia**. The host
