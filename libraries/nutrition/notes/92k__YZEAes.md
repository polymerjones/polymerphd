# The worst idea for fertility

## Central claim

**Reacting to Clavicular's fertility-recovery stack — HCG, HMG, and Clomiphene to restart
pituitary/testicular function after years of exogenous testosterone starting as a teenager, plus
supplements (CoQ10, carnitine, zinc, folate, vitamin D, omega-3) — the host argues those same
nutrients are available simply from eating steak and eggs, and that Clavicular's fast food habit is
undermining any fertility benefit from the supplements themselves.** `[00:00]` "You could get all
of those from just eating steak and eggs if you didn't insist on eating Burger King every single
meal of the day."

## The stack and the critique

`[00:00]` — **Fertility-restoration drugs**: HCG, HMG, and Clomiphene, described as necessary to
restart pituitary and testicular function after prolonged exogenous testosterone use — the host
states Clavicular currently has **zero sperm production**. **Supplements**: CoQ10, carnitine, zinc,
folate, vitamin D, and omega-3. **The history**: the host states Clavicular was on **400 milligrams
of testosterone at age 16**. **The core critique**: rather than relying on supplements while
continuing to eat Burger King and McDonald's "every single meal," the host argues real food (steak,
eggs, animal fats, sunlight for vitamin D) would supply the same nutrients more effectively.
