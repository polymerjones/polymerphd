# Don't make this mistake when brushing your teeth

## Central claim

**The host argues correct tooth-brushing timing matters more than people realize: brush before
breakfast (not after, since eating spikes overnight bacteria), wait 30-60 minutes after any meal
before brushing (since post-meal acid softens enamel), always floss before brushing, and always
brush right before sleep — while avoiding fluoride toothpaste, which he says consistently lowers
IQ in children in studies.** `[00:00]` "I bet you're brushing your teeth wrong and you are damaging
your teeth."

## The timing rules

`[00:00]` — **Morning**: brush before breakfast, not after — overnight mouth bacteria "explode" once
food is introduced, and brushing after breakfast means brushing while that bacterial spike is
present. **After any meal**: don't brush immediately — post-meal acid softens tooth enamel, so
immediate brushing wears it down; rinsing with water is fine, but brushing should wait **30-60
minutes**. **Before bed**: the single most important brushing time, to avoid "leav[ing] your mouth
full of bacteria for eight hours." **Floss before brushing**, not after.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Lowered IQ in children** — attributed to fluoride toothpaste use, cited to unnamed studies
  `[00:00]`.
- **Enamel erosion** — attributed to brushing too soon after eating, while enamel is softened by
  post-meal acid `[00:00]`.
