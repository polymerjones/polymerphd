# I wouldn't put it on my wrist…

## Central claim

**Citing a study, the host reports three types of Apple Watch bands contain fluoroelastomer, a
PFAS-contaminated synthetic rubber, worn against skin for over 11 hours a day on average — a
meaningful absorption route given evidence that skin contact with PFAS results in bodily exposure —
and states he personally wouldn't wear one, citing both this and RF-EMF concerns.** `[00:00]` "I
would never wear one of these for the RF-EMF or for the potential exposure to forever chemicals in
these bands."

## The cited study and the broader pattern

`[00:00]` — **Fluoroelastomer**: a synthetic rubber derivative found in three types of Apple Watch
bands, contaminated with PFAS (per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances, "forever chemicals").
**Exposure duration**: the majority of wearers keep an Apple Watch on for **more than 11 hours a
day**, in direct skin contact. **Mechanism**: cited evidence that skin contact with PFAS can result
in absorption into the body. **Broader pattern**: the host extends the concern to synthetic
clothing generally, naming **Lululemon** (sports bras, leggings) as another product line found
contaminated with PFAS.
