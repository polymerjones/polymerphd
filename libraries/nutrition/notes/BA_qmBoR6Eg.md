# Fruit is amazing

## Central claim

**The host describes two years on a strict carnivore diet (meat, organs, fat) that improved his
eczema but caused electrolyte problems, heart palpitations, sleep disturbances, and declining
testosterone — all of which resolved after adding fruit and honey back to his animal-based diet —
and argues fruit's carbohydrates specifically aid electrolyte conservation and support carbohydrate
needs for exercise without the plant toxins found in grains and roots.** `[00:00]` "All of those got
better when I added fruit and honey back to my diet."

## The personal timeline and the mechanism

`[00:00]` — **Two years of strict carnivore** (meat, organs, fat only): eczema improved, but the host
developed electrolyte problems, heart palpitations, sleep disturbances, and declining testosterone.
**After adding fruit and honey back**: all of these issues resolved. **Proposed mechanism**: fruit
carbohydrates aid the body in "conserving electrolytes and maintaining proper ratios of sodium,
chloride, potassium, and magnesium." Additional named benefits: vitamin C, boron (in many fruits),
and carbohydrate fuel for exercise, without the "excess plant toxins" the host associates with grains
and roots as carbohydrate sources.
