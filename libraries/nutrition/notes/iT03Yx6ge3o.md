# You're probably not running correctly #running #fitness #carnivorediet

## Central claim

**Walking uses a heel-strike motor pattern, while jogging, running, and sprinting are argued to be
inherently forefoot-strike patterns — using a heel strike while jogging or running is framed as
incorrect form that damages the knees and joints.** `[00:00]` "Walking is a heel strike motor
pattern. Jogging, running, sprinting are [a] four[-]foot strike motor pattern[s]... If you are not
using a four[-]foot motor pattern, when you are running or jogging, you are doing it wrong, and you
are hurting your knees and your joints."

## The stated preference and rule

`[00:00]` — The host states a personal preference against jogging generally, preferring to walk or
sprint instead. For those who do jog, the stated rule is: "always run on the balls of your feet" —
described as "the proper way to run," applying even to slow jogging.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Knee and joint pain/damage** — attributed specifically to using a heel-strike pattern while
  jogging or running rather than a forefoot strike `[00:00]`.

None — "four foot motor pattern" in the transcript is very likely a caption/transcription artifact
for "forefoot motor pattern," given the context distinguishing it from "heel strike."
