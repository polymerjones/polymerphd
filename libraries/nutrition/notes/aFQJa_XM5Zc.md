# Is Western medicine only treating symptoms?

## Central claim

**Tracing his own training — four years pre-med at William & Mary, two years PA school at George
Washington University, four years practicing as a cardiology PA, four years of medical school at the
University of Arizona, and four years of residency at the University of Washington — the host argues
he received essentially zero nutrition education and no focus on root causes of disease at any
stage, with training instead centered on pharmaceutical symptom management.** `[00:00]` "I've come to
believe that this lack of focus on nutrition in medical education is hurting millions and millions of
people."

## The training timeline

`[00:00]` — **Pre-med**: four years, College of William & Mary. **PA school**: two years, George
Washington University (Washington, D.C.). **Clinical PA practice**: four years in cardiology, with
"zero conversation about the root cause of cardiovascular illness." **Medical school**: four years,
University of Arizona, "zero courses in nutrition," training centered on "pharmaceutical drugs and
how to use them to treat symptoms." **Residency**: four years, University of Washington, again "zero
courses in nutrition."
