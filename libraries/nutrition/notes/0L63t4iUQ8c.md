# What's the best way to cook eggs?

## Central claim

**The host gives two cooking rules for eggs, independent of how they're prepared (hard-boiled,
scrambled, sunny side up): never use a non-stick pan, because of PFAS ("forever chemicals") that
can disrupt hormones, and never cook in oils — including olive or avocado oil, which oxidize under
heat — favoring tallow, butter, or ghee instead.** `[00:00]` "Don't cook your eggs in non-stick
garbage pans... don't cook your eggs in seed oils... don't cook your eggs in avocado or olive oil."

## The two rules

`[00:00]` — **Pan**: avoid non-stick cookware, which the host calls "full of forever chemicals,
PFAs," and says will "disrupt your hormones." **Fat**: use tallow (rendered beef fat), butter, or
ghee rather than any oil — the host is explicit that even olive oil, often assumed to be a "safe"
cooking fat, "is going to be oxidized and broken down if you heat it for cooking."
