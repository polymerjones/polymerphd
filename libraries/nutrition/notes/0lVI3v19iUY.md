# Is Detoxing from Seed Oils Possible?

## Central claim

**Citing a 680-day half-life for linoleic acid (the dominant omega-6 fat in seed oils) in cell
membranes, the host argues there's no way to actively accelerate its clearance — full return to a
normal cell membrane composition would take over 8 years — so the only real "detox" is simply
stopping seed oil intake and prioritizing high-stearic-acid animal fats like tallow, butter, and
ghee.** `[00:00]` "We don't really know how to speed that process up, but the best I can think about
this is stop eating seed oils."

## The half-life figure

`[00:00]` — Corn, canola, sunflower, safflower, and soybean oil are named as the high-linoleic-acid
seed oils in question. The host states omega-6 fatty acids have **"a half-life of 680 days in your
cell membranes,"** which he extrapolates to mean **"more than 8 years"** to fully clear excess
linoleic acid from cell membranes after stopping intake — no active method to speed this up is
offered, only removal of the ongoing source.

this short's presentation of the same number, which states it without qualification.
