# Energy drinks?? Really?

## Central claim

**Energy drinks are rejected ingredient-by-ingredient: caffeine is argued to spike cortisol,
activate the sympathetic nervous system, and disrupt blood sugar and sleep; the B vitamins,
carnitine, and taurine they contain are argued to be more bioavailable when sourced from meat and
organs instead; and stevia is argued to disrupt gut bacterial "quorum sensing."** `[00:00]` "Why
would you drink an energy drink for your energy? Why not get the nutrients... from food?... B
vitamins, oh those are in meat and organs, even more bioavailable in those foods... carnitine and
taurine, magical substances that are also found in meat and organs in much more bioavailable
forms."

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Blood sugar disruption, sleep disruption** — attributed to caffeine `[00:00]`.
- **Gut disruption** — attributed to stevia via quorum-sensing interference `[00:00]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Quorum sensing** — used without full definition; the communication mechanism between bacteria,
  here said to be disrupted by stevia in the gut `[00:00]`.
