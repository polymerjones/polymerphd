# Blue Zones eat meat?!?

## Central claim

**The host argues the popular narrative that Blue Zones longevity is linked to plant-based eating is
"almost entirely false," naming specific traditional meat dishes eaten in four of the five Blue Zone
regions (Sardinia, Icaria, Okinawa, Nicoya), and noting that Loma Linda — the one region where meat
is not eaten in significant quantities — shows research indicating poor sperm numbers and motility
among its vegan and vegetarian residents.** `[00:00]` "In the blue zones, people eat significant
amounts of meat, contrary to what you've been told... It's a nice story, but it's almost entirely
false."

## The region-by-region breakdown

`[00:00]` — **Sardinia**: porcedu, a prized dish made from a special local pig lineage. **Icaria**:
gamopilafo, a traditional dish made with free-range goat. **Okinawa**: pork described as "a
significant component of the diet," with historical centenarian research finding none were vegan or
vegetarian. **Nicoya (Costa Rica)**: meat described as playing a significant role in the diet and
"often sought after and craved." **Loma Linda**: the one exception where meat is not eaten in
significant quantities — cited research there shows vegans and vegetarians with "very poor sperm
numbers and sperm motility."
