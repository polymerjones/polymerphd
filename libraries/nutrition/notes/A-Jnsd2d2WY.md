# Lululemon = forever chemicals

## Central claim

**The host warns that PFAS ("forever chemicals") are present in the crotch region of Lululemon
leggings and similar products from other companies, and that because women commonly wear these
leggings without underwear, the chemicals can be absorbed directly through skin in a concentrated
area — with PFAS linked to hormone disruption, cancer, and thyroid, kidney, and liver damage.**
`[00:00]` "These chemicals are associated with hormone disruption, with cancers, with thyroid,
kidney, and liver damage in humans. They're horrible for us and they're in our clothing."

## The mechanism and the specific concern

`[00:00]` — **PFAS**: perfluoroalkyl compounds, called "forever chemicals" for being persistent in
the environment and difficult for the body to eliminate. **Route of exposure highlighted**: skin
absorption from the crotch region of leggings, specifically flagged as concerning because "women
don't wear underwear with these commonly," concentrating direct skin contact in that area. **Named
health associations**: hormone disruption, cancers, thyroid damage, kidney damage, liver damage —
listed without individual studies cited in this brief clip.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Hormone disruption** — attributed to PFAS exposure `[00:00]`.
- **Thyroid, kidney, and liver damage** — attributed to PFAS exposure `[00:00]`.
