# Chicken or beef: Which one is healthier?

## Central claim

**The host prefers red meat (beef, bison, lamb, venison) over chicken, arguing that even the best
organic chicken is typically fed corn, soy, and grains — foods not evolutionarily appropriate for
chickens — making it harder to find quality chicken than quality grass-fed red meat, though he
considers any chicken still preferable to processed plant foods.** `[00:00]` "I think that the way
for humans to thrive is to make the majority of your diet red meat, beef, grass-fed beef, bison,
things like this, lamb, venison."

## The comparison

`[00:00]` — Chicken is affirmed as meat, lacking the "defense chemicals" of plant foods, and
preferable to processed plant foods generally. But the host's specific preference for red meat over
chicken rests on feed quality: "even the best chickens, organic chickens, are going to be fed corn
and soy... foods that are not evolutionarily appropriate for the chickens." Red meat animals (beef,
bison, lamb, venison), by contrast, more commonly get access to a grass-fed diet. The host allows
occasional chicken consumption as fine, while recommending it not form the majority of the diet.
