# Don't make this mistake when buying sourdough bread

## Central claim

**The host argues most sourdough bread — despite being better than regular bread — is made with
enriched wheat flour containing folic acid, a synthetic folate form the body doesn't fully
metabolize, cited to studies finding over 90% of people consuming supplements or enriched flour
have unmetabolized folic acid in their blood, associated with reduced immune function, some
cancers (colon cancer named), and potential neurologic development issues in children or fetuses —
recommending sourdough made only from wheat flour, water, and salt instead.** `[00:00]` "We do not
want to be eating folic acid."

## The claim and the fix

`[00:00]` — **Folic acid**: described as "a synthetic form of folate. It does not occur in nature,"
present in enriched wheat flour used in most commercial sourdough. **Prevalence**: cited studies
find **over 90%** of people who consume supplements or enriched flour have detectable
**unmetabolized folic acid** in their blood. **Health associations**: reduced immune function, some
cancers (colon cancer specifically named), and potential neurologic development issues in children
or fetuses — none tied to a specific named study in this clip. **The fix**: seek sourdough made from
just wheat flour, water, and salt, with no enriched flour.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Reduced immune function** — associated with unmetabolized folic acid `[00:00]`.
- **Neurologic development issues in children or fetuses** — associated with unmetabolized folic
  acid exposure `[00:00]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Unmetabolized folic acid** — synthetic folic acid detectable in blood because it isn't fully
  converted to its active form, cited here at a >90% prevalence among supplement/enriched-flour
  consumers `[00:00]`.
