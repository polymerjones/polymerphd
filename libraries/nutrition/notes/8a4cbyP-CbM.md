# Should there be warning labels on junk food?

## Central claim

**The host argues junk food should carry cigarette-style graphic warning labels — pictures of
health consequences (an obese person with an amputation or on dialysis) and text stating regular
consumption can lead to diabetes, heart disease, cardiovascular disease, depression, poor libido,
and "an overall miserable life" — arguing this is "medical clear fact" that consumers deserve to
see as plainly as smokers do.** `[00:00]` "Why these foods do not have warning labels like
cigarettes is bullshit."

## The proposal

`[00:00]` — Modeled directly on cigarette package warnings (graphic imagery plus text), the
proposed junk food label would show consequences like amputation or dialysis from diabetes, with
text naming diabetes, heart disease, cardiovascular disease, depression, reduced libido, and
overall reduced quality of life as outcomes of regular consumption. No specific study is cited for
this causal chain in the clip — it's framed as an already-settled medical consensus rather than a
claim needing fresh evidence.
