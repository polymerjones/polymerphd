# Vegan vs carnivore UFC fight

## Central claim

**Using a UFC fight result (the meat-eating fighter won against a plant-based opponent) and a
prior boxing example (Canelo Alvarez going vegan before a loss to Dmitry Bivol), the host argues
elite athletic performance requires animal meat, citing higher bioavailability and unique nutrients
not found in plants.** `[00:00]` "High-level athletic performance requires animal meat. There's no
question. It is essential for optimal human performance."

## The evidence offered

`[00:00]` — Two anecdotal cases: **Ian Machado Garry**, described as following a plant-based diet,
lost to **Shavkat Rakhmonov**, whose post-fight meal was "real meats" versus Garry's beans,
chickpeas, and Beyond Meat. **Canelo Alvarez** is cited as a prior example — reportedly going vegan
before his fight with Dmitry Bivol, which "didn't go that well for Canelo either." The host's
stated mechanism: **"animal protein, meat, more highly bioavailable... so many unique nutrients in
meat that you can't get from plants."**
