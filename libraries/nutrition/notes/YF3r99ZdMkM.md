# Avoid seed oils and processed sugar for HPB

## Central claim

**High blood pressure is argued to be caused by insulin resistance in the vast majority of cases —
a root cause the host says was never discussed across his cardiology PA practice, medical school,
and residency training, which instead focused on medication algorithms (beta blockers, diuretics,
calcium channel blockers) that lower blood pressure without addressing the underlying cause —
with removing processed sugars and seed oils offered as the actual fix.** `[00:00]` "High blood
pressure is caused by insulin resistance in the vast majority of cases... While beta blockers,
diuretics, or calcium channel blockers may lower your blood pressure, they do not treat the root
cause... Remove processed sugars and seed oils from your diet, and your insulin resistance will
improve. I guarantee it."

## The stated medical background

`[00:00]` — PA school, practicing cardiology as a PA, medical school, and residency at the
University of Washington — across all of it, the host states insulin resistance "was never talked
about" in relation to blood pressure management.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **High blood pressure** — the central condition addressed, framed as fixable via insulin
  resistance reversal rather than only manageable via medication `[00:00]`.

## Source-stated confidence

Low-to-moderate — insulin resistance is a genuinely recognized contributor to hypertension in the
broader medical literature, though "the vast majority of cases" is a stronger, more absolute claim
than mainstream cardiology typically makes (hypertension has multiple recognized causes and risk
factors beyond insulin resistance); the claim that removing processed sugar and seed oils will
"guarantee" improvement is asserted without a cited trial. The personal medical-training account is
a first-person claim not independently verifiable from this clip.
