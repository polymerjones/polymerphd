# Seed oils are in everything!

## Central claim

**Reading ingredient labels on the fly, the host shows seed oils (canola, sunflower, soybean — "the
evil trifecta of vegetable oils") present in products not typically flagged as unhealthy, including
gluten-free tortilla wraps, goldfish crackers, and yellow curry paste, arguing this pervasiveness
explains widespread poor health.** `[00:00]` "Gluten free tortilla wraps. You thought you were doing
good because you were gluten free? Nope. Soybean oil... The evil trifecta of vegetable oils. Canola,
sunflower and soybean... They're in everything. No wonder everyone is so fat, sick, and unhealthy."
