# Doctors aren't taught this..

## Central claim

**Filmed at the University of Arizona Medical School, the host reiterates that his medical training
covered anatomy and pharmaceutical treatment appropriate to diagnoses, but never challenged students
to investigate root causes of illness or the therapeutic power of food for conditions like chronic
illness, autoimmune disease, inflammation, cancer, and dementia — arguing this needs to change.**
`[00:00]` "We need to do better for our doctors so that so many more people can find freedom from
suffering. If we don't train doctors differently, how will any of this ever change?"

## The critique

`[00:00]` — What medical training did cover: anatomy, and pharmaceutical drugs appropriate for
specific diagnoses. What it did not cover: investigating what causes illness, or food as "a powerful
lever" for chronic illness, autoimmune disease, inflammation, cancer, and dementia. The host frames
this as a systemic training gap requiring structural change, not an individual failing of any one
doctor.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Chronic illness, autoimmune disease, inflammation, cancer, and dementia** — named as conditions
  where food's therapeutic potential is left untaught in medical training `[00:00]`.
