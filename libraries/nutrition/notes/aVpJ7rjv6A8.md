# Paul Saladino’s Animal-Based Breakfast

## Central claim

**The host shows his animal-based breakfast — raw goat's milk with honey, orange juice, liver
(credited for folate, riboflavin, biotin, K2, choline), heart (coenzyme Q10, taurine, anserine,
carnitine), a grass-fed New York steak, microplastic-free sea salt, and coconuts — framing it as an
example of prioritizing the most nutrient-rich foods with the least toxins.** `[00:00]` "I like to
focus on the most nutrient-rich foods with the least amount of toxins. And I feel like an
animal-based diet is a great starting place for that."
