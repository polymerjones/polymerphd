# Why are you using deodorant…

## Central claim

phthalates, synthetic fragrances, and aluminum, and that diet quality — not deodorant — is the
real determinant of body odor, while offering diluted apple cider vinegar as a compromise for
professional settings that require it.** `[00:00]` "There's no reason for me to use garbage
