# I had to quit carnivore…

## Central claim

**The host explains his dietary evolution: strict carnivore (meat, fat, salt only) for about a
year and a half starting in 2018-2019 resolved his eczema but caused other unspecified issues that
improved once carbohydrates were reintroduced, leading to his current animal-based diet (meat,
organs, raw dairy, fruit, honey) — which he recommends as a starting baseline, especially for
autoimmune issues or figuring out food reactions.** `[00:00]` "I don't think everyone needs to eat
exactly like me, but I do think an animal-based diet... is a good starting point for people,
especially if they have autoimmune issues."

## The dietary timeline

`[00:00]` — **2018-2019, ~18 months**: strict carnivore (meat, fat, salt), which "helped me
understand what was causing my eczema, fixed the eczema," but led to unnamed issues that "got a lot
better when I included carbohydrates." **Current diet**: animal-based — meat and organs as the
majority, plus raw cheese, raw butter, raw milk, fruit, and local raw organic honey. A sample day:
honey and raw milk on waking; meat and maybe fruit juice after surfing; cheese and fruit (squash,
avocado, olives) as an afternoon snack; meat, organs, and carbohydrates (fruit, honey, dairy) at
dinner.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Eczema** — resolved by strict carnivore, self-reported `[00:00]`.
- **Unspecified issues from strict carnivore** — improved specifically once carbohydrates were
  reintroduced, though the issues themselves aren't named in this clip `[00:00]`.
