# Do this before eating rice

## Central claim

**Although he personally doesn't eat rice, the host outlines a preparation protocol for those who
do — choosing organic white rice over brown (for lower arsenic), rinsing repeatedly, soaking
overnight in warm water with apple cider vinegar, and pressure cooking — to reduce arsenic and
lectins, while disclosing that even with all these steps, rice still gives him brain fog.**
`[00:00]` "I don't eat rice, but if I did, here's how I would do it."

## The protocol

`[00:00]` — **White over brown**: brown rice is said to carry "much higher levels of heavy metals
like arsenic" than white rice. **Preparation steps**: rinse multiple times; soak overnight in warm
water with apple cider vinegar "to help with the further breakdown of problematic compounds";
pressure cook to further break down lectins. **Serving**: with grass-fed butter and salt. The host
is explicit this is conditional advice — he personally still experiences brain fog and feels "a
little funny" from rice even after all these steps, but offers the protocol for people who do
include rice in their diet.
