# I don't worry about melanoma…

## Central claim

**Responding to concern about his tan, the host argues he isn't worried about melanoma because
gradual, non-burning sun exposure (as in outdoor workers) is associated with lower melanoma risk,
while excess linoleic acid from seed oils accumulating in fat tissue is positively correlated with
melanoma rates — and since he avoids seed oils and tans gradually without burning, he considers
himself low-risk.** `[00:00]` "We know that gradual exposure to sunlight, like outdoor workers who
get a little bit of sun every day, is actually protected against melanoma. We also know that
conversely, excess linoleic acid, the polyunsaturated fatty acid from seed oils in your fat tissue,
is positively correlated with melanoma rates."
