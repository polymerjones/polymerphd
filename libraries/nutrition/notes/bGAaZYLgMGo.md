# Cheesecake Factory = seed oils??

## Central claim

**Through a direct conversation with Cheesecake Factory kitchen staff, the host establishes that the
restaurant's flat top grill — used for tortillas, buns, chicken, and bacon — is seasoned with canola
oil, while the burger, steak, and fish broilers use no oil (fish gets a light oil brush only for
appearance), and the salad dressing contains canola oil — concluding that asking specific questions
lets a customer avoid seed oils at this restaurant.** `[00:00]` "At Cheesecake Factory, there's seed
oils on the flat top, not on the steaks or the burgers, but you have to know. And if you ask, you can
avoid it."

## The staff interview

`[00:00]` — **Flat top grill** (canola oil, continuously seasoned): tortillas, buns, chicken, bacon.
**Broilers** (no oil): burger broiler, steak broiler, fish broiler — though the fish may get a light
oil brush for visual appeal ("if they want to look like a peeling"). **Salad dressing**: contains
canola oil. Staff also raised a cross-contamination caveat: even requesting butter instead of oil
doesn't guarantee avoidance, since shared cooking surfaces carry residue.
