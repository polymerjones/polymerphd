# STOP giving THIS to your kids

## Central claim

**The host argues Nutella combines seed oil (its second ingredient) with hazelnuts — themselves
seeds carrying lectins, digestive enzyme inhibitors, phytates, and oxalates — making it doubly
concerning, and recommends replacing it and other nut butters entirely with regular butter plus
honey or maple syrup for sweetness.** `[00:00]` "Get rid of the Nutella. In fact, get rid of like all
of the peanut butters and nut butters and just use regular butter."

## The two-part concern

`[00:00]` — **Seed oil**: named as Nutella's second listed ingredient. **Hazelnuts**: classified as
seeds, and therefore carrying the same category of anti-nutrients this host applies to other seeds
elsewhere — lectins, digestive enzyme inhibitors, phytates, and oxalates — without naming a specific
quantity or study for hazelnuts specifically. The recommendation extends beyond Nutella to nut
butters generally, in favor of cow butter with honey or maple syrup as a sweet spread alternative.
