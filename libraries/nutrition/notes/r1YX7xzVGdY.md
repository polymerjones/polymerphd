# Beef + fruit = thrive

## Central claim

**Offering a deliberately simplified version of the animal-based diet, the host recommends beef
(muscle meat and organs) plus fruit as accessible to anyone, reasoning that grass-fed/grass-finished
beef is easier to source reliably than species-appropriate chicken or pork, that most fish is
contaminated, and that fruit carries the least plant defense chemicals of any carbohydrate
source.** `[00:00]` "Eat beef and fruit. This is the simplest version of an animal-based diet.
Anyone can do this... Beef, muscle meat, and organs, and fruit. Similar version of an animal-based
diet. So many people will benefit."

## The reasoning for each choice

`[00:00]` — **Beef:** cows are ruminants raised on grass, making grass-fed/grass-finished
availability easier to find than species-appropriate chicken or pork; most fish is "contaminated
with heavy metals and microplastics." **Fruit:** framed as the food plants actually "want" eaten
(sweet, colorful), carrying "the least amount of defense chemicals" compared to vegetables,
grains, or beans.

Low — the claim that grass-fed/finished beef is more findable than species-appropriate chicken or
pork is a practical market observation rather than a nutritional claim; the fruit-versus-grains/
beans defense-chemical comparison is stated without a specific study.
