# Ground beef without beef??

## Central claim

**The host argues Trader Joe's "beefless ground beef" is a poor substitute for real ground beef,
pointing to its ingredients — textured soy protein, canola oil, guar gum, and caramel color — and
contrasting them with real ground beef's bioavailable iron, zinc, manganese, vitamin B6, and
vitamin B12, none of which the plant-based product contains.** `[00:00]` "This can't be good for
humans."

## The ingredient breakdown

`[00:00]` — Flagged ingredients: **textured soy protein** (called "not great for humans"),
**canola oil**, **guar gum** ("we know [is] harmful for the human gut"), and **caramel color**
(described as "associated with irritable bowel syndrome"). Real ground beef's nutrients, named by
contrast: bioavailable iron, zinc, manganese, vitamin B6, and vitamin B12 — "none of which are in
beefless ground beef."
