# You think nicotine is healthy!?

## Central claim

**The host argues nicotine pouches deliver far more nicotine than people realize — up to 90mg/day
from a near-full pack, with roughly 50mg actually absorbed, more than 30 cigarettes' worth — are
addictive and hard to withdraw from, and raise cortisol, and recommends creatine plus sleep and
meat as a better path to the energy and focus people are actually seeking from nicotine.** `[00:00]`
"That's more than 30 cigarettes worth of nicotine per day."

## The dosage math

`[00:00]` — Each packet: **6mg of nicotine**. Near-daily use of most of a pack: up to **90mg of
nicotine per day**. Absorption rate: **45-75%** of the nicotine in a pouch, yielding roughly **50mg
of absorbed nicotine per day** — compared directly to "more than 30 cigarettes worth." Separately,
nicotine is said to **increase cortisol**, a stress hormone. The host's alternative recommendation
for energy/focus: **creatine**, called "the best nootropic," alongside adequate sleep and eating
meat.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Difficult withdrawal** — attributed to the high daily nicotine dose from pouch use `[00:00]`.
- **Elevated cortisol** — attributed to nicotine use generally `[00:00]`.
