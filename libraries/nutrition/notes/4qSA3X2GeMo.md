# My protocol for longevity

## Central claim

**The host walks through a day built around several longevity-oriented habits: not sitting too
long, looking at distant views to support the parasympathetic nervous system, Wi-Fi on during the
day but off at night, whey protein post-surf, red lighting throughout the house after sunset to
protect circadian rhythm, and deliberately prioritizing community and social connection.** `[00:00]`
"I'm going to take everything I do in a day to maximize health span and lifespan."

## The routine

`[00:00]` — Morning: shoulder/hip mobility work before surfing; standing up periodically and
looking at a far-away view, framed as supporting the **parasympathetic nervous system**; Wi-Fi
turned on for daytime work use. `[01:08]` — Evening: Wagyu flat iron steak for dinner; **all house
lights switched to red after sunset** to protect circadian rhythm, with watching the sunset itself
described as a cue that "it's time to wind down." The host explicitly names **community** — his
dog, friends, a fire — as "a big part of my life" and, implicitly, this longevity protocol, even
though he says he doesn't always show it in his videos.
