# Calling out McDonalds…

## Central claim

**The host argues McDonald's switched its french fries from beef tallow to seed oils in 1990 due
to a smear campaign by businessman Phil Sokoloff (who blamed saturated animal fat for his own heart
attack), resulting in fries that now use four seed oils, 19 total ingredients, and toxic
combustion-adjacent byproducts (aldehydes, acrolein) when heated — and publicly challenges McDonald's
to switch back.** `[00:00]` "I'm challenging you, McDonald's. Cook your fries in tallow again."

## The history and the chemistry

`[00:00]` — **Phil Sokoloff**, described as a wealthy American businessman who had a heart attack in
the 1960s, is credited with mounting a public campaign against saturated animal fats and McDonald's
fries specifically, convinced saturated fat caused his heart attack. McDonald's "caved under the
pressure" and switched to seed oils by 1990. The host names **aldehydes and acrolein** — "found in
cigarettes when you heat them" — as toxic byproducts of heating seed oils for frying. Because
customers missed the beef flavor from tallow, McDonald's now adds **beef flavoring derived from
wheat and milk ingredients** rather than reintroducing tallow itself.
