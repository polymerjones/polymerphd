# Animal-based pasta recipe

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates preparing spaghetti squash as a grain-free, gluten-free substitute for
pasta — halved, seeded, roasted at 400°F for about an hour, then shredded with a fork — arguing
avoiding grains, especially wheat, is "a huge step in the right direction for most people" for
overall health.** `[00:00]` "This is what I eat instead of pasta on an animal-based diet."

## The recipe

`[00:00]` — Preheat oven to 400°F. Cut spaghetti squash in half, remove seeds, roast for
approximately one hour. Shred the cooked flesh with a fork to produce spaghetti-like strands. The
host notes spaghetti squash is botanically a fruit, and suggests topping it with tomato sauce and
meat, a fettuccine-style cheese sauce, or simply butter and salt.
