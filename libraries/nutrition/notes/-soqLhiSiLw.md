# Beating Crohn's with meat & fruit

## Central claim

**Relaying someone else's story (Oliver), the host describes an eight-month animal-based diet
(meat, eggs, fruit) resulting in medication-free Crohn's disease remission, resolved anxiety and
psoriasis, and full recovery of lost muscle mass and training capacity — offered as evidence that
diet quality can be a root-cause intervention for chronic illness that "the Western medical system"
overlooks.** `[00:00]` "By just changing the quality of the foods he's eating... Oliver was able to
completely reverse his Crohn's, lower his inflammation, get off his medications, and get back to
training."

## The reported outcome

`[00:00]` — Oliver's stated diet: **10-15 eggs a day, a kilo and a half (1.5 kg) of beef, and
fruit** "to fuel his training." Reported outcomes over eight months: **medication-free Crohn's
management, resolved inflammation, no more anxiety or psoriasis**, running a marathon, and
regaining **seven kilograms of muscle** lost during the Crohn's flare. The host is explicit this is
secondhand ("this is an incredible story," "I love hearing the stories of these people") and adds
an important hedge of his own: **"I don't think it's the only way to do this, but it clearly worked
for Oliver."**

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Crohn's disease flare, inflammation, anxiety, and psoriasis** — all reported by Oliver as
  resolved after eight months on the described diet `[00:00]`.
- **Muscle loss during illness** — reported as fully regained (seven kilograms) once back to
  training on the new diet `[00:00]`.
