**The host argues junk food (chips, cookies, Cheetos, M&Ms, candy) triggers brain reward patterns
similar to cigarettes, and that "cheat days" on an animal-based diet function like a smoker lighting
up every weekend — reigniting cravings that undermine progress — recommending a strict, cheat-day-
free 30-day animal-based commitment instead.** `[00:00]` "What makes you think you're not going to
have cravings for junk food during the week that are going to short circuit your progress with an
