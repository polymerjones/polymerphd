# What about Olive Oil?

## Central claim

**The host ranks extra virgin olive oil as clearly better than seed oils — unheated, unrefined,
unbleached, undeodorized, and not hexane-extracted — but still ranks animal fats (butter, tallow,
ghee) above it because of their lower linoleic acid content, and states he personally doesn't use
olive oil at all (no salad, no cooking with it).** `[00:00]` "I prefer animal fats like butter and
tallow or ghee because they're much lower in linoleic acid. And I want the lowest amount of
linoleic acid possible in my diet."

## The ranking and the caveat

`[00:00]` — Extra virgin olive oil is credited as minimally processed relative to seed oils —
**no hexane extraction**, no heating, refining, bleaching, or deodorizing — making it "way better
than seed oils" for anyone who wants an alternative. But the host states his own preference is
animal fats specifically for their lower linoleic acid content, and adds a practical caveat: he
**wouldn't heat olive oil** or use it as a cooking oil, consistent with concerns about its heat
stability raised elsewhere in this library.
