# “Red meat is inflammatory” - Harvard 😂

## Central claim

**Responding to a Harvard list naming steak alongside white bread, margarine, soda, and french fries
as inflammatory foods, the host argues there's no good evidence red meat is harmful, citing an
interventional study where replacing grain-based carbohydrates with red meat reduced inflammation,
and pointing to his own low hs-CRP after eating red meat daily (twice daily) for over five years.**
`[00:00]` "There's no good evidence that red meat is harmful for you. On the contrary, there are
many good interventional studies... which show that replacing grain-based carbohydrates with red
meat actually reduces inflammation."

## The rebuttal

`[00:00]` — Harvard's list is characterized as grouping steak with clearly processed, refined foods
(white bread, margarine, soda, french fries) — "one of these things is not like the others." Cited
counter-evidence: an interventional study showing replacing grain-based carbohydrates with red meat
reduces inflammation. Evolutionary framing: meat "at the center of the human diet for hundreds of
thousands of years," prized by hunter-gatherers who show "exemplary health and freedom from so many
of the chronic diseases that plague us in the Western world." Personal data point: the host reports
a "super low" hs-CRP (a standard inflammation marker) after eating red meat daily, twice a day, for
over five years, referencing bloodwork he shares on his podcast.
