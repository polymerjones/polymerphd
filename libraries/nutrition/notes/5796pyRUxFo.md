# How to make kefir

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates making kefir at home from kefir grains and raw milk, fermented a couple of
days in a warm environment, and states he personally is lactose intolerant and can't drink raw milk
without issues, but can tolerate kefir once it's fermented because fermentation lowers its lactose
content.** `[00:00]` "I'm lactose intolerant. I can't drink raw milk without getting lactose issues,
but I can totally drink kefir once it ferments."

## The recipe

`[00:00]` — Kefir grains (strained from a bag, purchased online) are placed in a glass jar with raw
milk, then left to ferment for a couple of days — warmer temperatures speed fermentation, so the
host recommends leaving the jar somewhere warm or outside. The result is fermented milk (kefir)
with substantially lower lactose than the raw milk it started from.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Lactose intolerance** — self-reported by the host as an issue with raw milk directly, resolved
  for him by drinking kefir instead `[00:00]`.
