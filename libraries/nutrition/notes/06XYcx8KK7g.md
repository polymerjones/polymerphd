# How to shop for meat at Costco

## Central claim

**The host argues Costco offers real budget-friendly paths to quality meat — buying a ribeye
primal and cutting it yourself instead of pre-cut steaks, grass-fed ground beef under $5/lb, and
mostly grass-fed Australian lamb — pushing back on the idea that eating well is too expensive.**
`[00:00]` "Don't tell me eating healthy is too expensive. Less than $5 a pound for grass-fed, ground
beef at Costco."

## The specific prices and cuts

`[00:00]` — **Ribeye primal**: USDA Choice grade, **$12.49/lb**, versus Costco's regular pre-cut
ribeyes at **$18-19/lb** — buying the whole primal and cutting it yourself is the stated savings
strategy. Costco is noted as **not carrying grass-fed, grass-finished steaks**. **Ground beef**:
American-raised, grass-fed, grass-finished, 85% lean, **$4.97/lb** — the host adds, "I love animal
fat. I don't fear it," referencing the 85% (versus leaner) lean ratio positively. **Lamb**: Costco's
rack of lamb and lamb leg are cited as roughly 90% Australian, mostly grass-fed and grass-finished.
