# Healthy soda recipe

## Central claim

**The host offers a homemade sparkling honey-lime drink as a soda substitute and argues raw,
organic, glyphosate-free honey — despite being sugar — is not equivalent to the processed sugar,
phosphoric acid, and caramel color in Coca-Cola, citing "multiple studies" showing honey is a
healthy food.** `[00:00]` "Cutting all of the soda out of your diet, out of your life, is one of the
single best, easiest things you can do to improve your health."

## The recipe and the honey argument

`[00:00]` — Recipe: sparkling water (glass preferred), cut lime, about a tablespoon of honey, and a
pinch of sea salt. On honey specifically: **"there are multiple studies showing that honey is a
healthy food for humans"** (unnamed), with an explicit caveat to consume it "in the context of how
active you are." The core distinction drawn: raw, organic, glyphosate-free honey "does not have the
same effects in the human body" as Coca-Cola's processed sugar, phosphoric acid, and caramel color —
framing this as a difference in food quality and context, not just sugar content.
