# What's inside Dunkin Donuts'?

## Central claim

**The host reads through the 70+ ingredients in a Dunkin' Donuts Boston Cream Donut — processed
sugars, seed oils, preservatives, and several gums including carrageenan — arguing gums generally
irritate the gut and carrageenan specifically is well studied in both humans and animals as gut-
irritating and inflammatory, and connects this single donut to a broader statistic that Western
diets now derive over 60% of calories from ultra-processed foods like it.** `[00:00]` "That is why
we are all fat-sick and unhealthy."

## The ingredients and the broader statistic

`[00:00]` — Ingredients named: processed sugars, corn syrup, artificial flavors, sodium benzoate,
sodium pyrophosphate, seed oils, and a cluster of gums — **cellulose gum, guar gum, xanthan gum,
acacia gum, and carrageenan**. Carrageenan specifically is called "well studied in both human and
animal models" for gut irritation, inflammation, and damage. The host connects this one product to
the claim that **over 60% of calories in Western diets** now come from ultra-processed foods,
framing incremental exposure ("little bits... every once in a while") as how this becomes the
majority of a diet over time.
