# Humans crave meat and organs!

## Central claim

**Citing the Hadza hunter-gatherer tribe's own stated preferences — meat as their favorite food, a
big kill as their happiest day, hunting skill as what makes a good husband — the host argues this
reflects an intuitive human draw toward meat and organs, eaten nose to tail including the fat, as
the diet humans are naturally suited to.** `[00:00]` "It's just very clear meat and hunting is the
center of their life."

## The Hadza evidence cited

`[00:00]` — Three specific claims about the Hadza: their **favorite food is meat**; their
**happiest day** is described as the day of a big kill, brought back to the tribe; and when asked
what makes a good husband, **women reportedly answer "a good hunter."** The host uses these to argue
that in an unmediated (non-agricultural, non-modern) context, humans gravitate toward meat and
organs as the primary source of nutrient density.
