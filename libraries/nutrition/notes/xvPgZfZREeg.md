# Use honey as a sweetener!

## Central claim

**Rather than treating all sweeteners as equally bad, the host ranks them: processed sugar and
artificial sweeteners (stevia, Splenda) are rejected outright — both disrupt gut microbiome quorum
sensing, and stevia is separately noted for use as a contraceptive in indigenous countries — agave
and maple syrup rank as an acceptable-but-not-ideal middle tier (better than processed sugar in
animal models), with raw, unheated honey (or fruit) as the clear preference.** `[00:00]` "Both
Stevia and Splenda have been shown to disrupt the gut microbiome. They disrupt quorum sensing,
which is how bacteria talk in the gut. But Stevia is also used as a contraceptive in indigenous
countries... My preference, raw, organic, glyphosate-free honey or fruit as your sweetener."

## The tier structure

`[00:00]` — **Rejected:** processed sugar (sucrose), stevia, Splenda. **Middle tier** (heated plant
syrups, "not as good... as raw, unheated honey" but "better than processed sugar in animal
models"): agave, maple syrup. **Preferred:** raw, organic, glyphosate-free honey; fruit.
