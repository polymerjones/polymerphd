# Being in nature positively changes your brain chemistry!

## Central claim

**Urban environments — characterized by straight lines, right angles, concrete, and steel — are
argued to activate the sympathetic (fight-or-flight) nervous system, keeping city-dwellers
chronically stressed without their awareness, while natural environments with curved lines and
fractal patterns are argued to calm the body and activate the parasympathetic system, with the host
recommending 5-10 minutes daily in a natural landscape for anyone living in a city.** `[00:00]`
"With their straight lines, right angles, concrete and steel, cities have been called landscapes of
despair... being in cities with their right angles and straight lines activates the sympathetic
nervous system in humans... a natural environment... calms our body, turns on the parasympathetic
nervous system."
