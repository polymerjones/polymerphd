# Is kombucha BS?

## Central claim

**Kombucha itself is not rejected, but nearly all store-bought kombucha is, on the grounds that it
retains too much unfermented processed sugar — with the host citing research that fermented foods
like kombucha and kefir benefit the gut microbiome, while recommending homemade, longer-fermented,
lower-sugar kombucha instead.** `[00:00]` "I believe that most store-bought kombucha is way too
high in sugar, way too high in unfermented sugar to be good for you... Make your own kombucha and
get it low in sugar and that can be beneficial for your gut."

## The fermentation mechanism

`[00:00]` — Kombucha is made by adding processed cane sugar (sucrose) to a culture; sufficient
fermentation time converts that sugar into fermentation products. The host's objection is
specifically that commercial kombucha is often not fermented long enough to convert most of the
added sugar.

Moderate for the general fermented-foods-benefit-gut-microbiome claim — described as supported by
