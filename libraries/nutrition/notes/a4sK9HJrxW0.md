# How to make kefir

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates two ways to make kefir from raw, grass-fed milk sourced from a local
producer: adding kefir milk grains to a jar of raw milk, or adding some already-fermenting kefir
directly to fresh raw milk — both methods yielding kefir after one to two days depending on
temperature.** `[00:00]` "Two ways that are super simple... this raw milk will become kefir, either
because of the kefir grains or because you added some kefir that was already fermenting."

## The two methods

`[00:00]` — **Method 1**: add kefir milk grains to a glass jar, then pour in raw grass-fed milk.
**Method 2**: once an existing kefir culture is established, add some of that kefir directly to
fresh raw milk instead of using grains. Either method ferments into kefir within one to two days,
with fermentation speed depending on ambient temperature.
