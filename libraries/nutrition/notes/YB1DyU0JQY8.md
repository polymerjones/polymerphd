# Almond milk vs. raw milk

## Central claim

**Almond milk is argued against on the basis of its carrageenan content, processed sugar, and
almonds' own oxalate/digestive-enzyme-inhibitor content — citing a specific three-child case series
where excess almond product consumption caused blood in urine, painful urination, and kidney
stones that resolved on elimination — while raw cow's milk is argued for, citing European studies
associating it with lower rates of asthma, eczema, atopy, and hay fever in children.** `[00:00]`
"In this case series of three children, blood in the urine, pain with urination, and kidney stones
were all associated with excess consumption of almond milk and almond products that resolved when
it was eliminated... consumption of raw milk is associated with significantly decreased rates of
asthma, eczema, atopy, and hay fever in children throughout Europe."

## The two named study types

`[00:00]` — **Against almond milk:** a specific case series of three children with kidney-stone-
related symptoms tied to almond product consumption, symptoms resolving on elimination. **For raw
milk:** European population studies ("this study and many others") associating raw milk consumption
with lower rates of four specific conditions (asthma, eczema, atopy, hay fever) in children.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Blood in urine, painful urination, kidney stones** — cited in three children linked to excess
