# What's in the Whole Foods HOT BAR??

## Central claim

**Checking labels at the Whole Foods Smokehouse Hot Bar, the host finds canola and/or soybean oil
in nearly every meat item, including chicken wings — arguing seed oils "sneak into" foods people
assume are just meat, and framing seed oils generally as the single greatest driver of metabolic
dysfunction, insulin resistance, diabetes, and eventually obesity.** `[00:00]` "Even though it's
meat, has seed oils."

## The label check

`[00:00]` — Multiple hot bar items checked in sequence, each showing canola oil, with at least one
item also listing soybean oil. Chicken wings are specifically singled out as a food people
commonly eat without realizing it's cooked in seed oil, even at a retailer (Whole Foods) with a
health-focused reputation. The recommended alternative: tallow, butter, or other animal fats for
cooking.
