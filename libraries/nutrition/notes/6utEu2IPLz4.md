# Why do so many kids get diabetes?

## Central claim

**Filmed at the University of Arizona pediatric diabetes center where he attended medical school,
the host argues rising childhood type 2 diabetes is driven by processed sugar and seed oil
consumption and childhood obesity, and that even type 1 diabetes (an autoimmune condition) could be
diet-linked — criticizing his own medical training for never prompting him to ask why children get
diabetes rather than only how to treat it.** `[00:00]` "The roots of chronic illness for children
lies in diet. We cannot ignore this any longer."

## The claim and the setting

`[00:00]` — Filmed at "the angel wing for children with diabetes" at the University of Arizona
Pediatric Center — the same institution where the host attended medical school and did clinical
rotations. He states type 2 diabetes in children is rising "massively" due to childhood obesity,
itself driven by processed sugars and seed oils. He extends the diet-connection hypothesis to
**type 1 diabetes**, an autoimmune condition, suggesting it "could be linked with diet" as well,
though without elaborating a specific mechanism. He frames his own training there as never having
challenged him to investigate root causes rather than pharmaceutical treatment.
