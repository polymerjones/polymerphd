# Seed oils at "healthy" vegan restaurant

## Central claim

**Asking staff directly at a restaurant branded as healthy and vegan, the host finds seed oils —
grapeseed oil (sauteing) and rice bran oil (frying) — are used throughout, with olive oil reserved
mainly as a finishing oil, undercutting the restaurant's "healthy" positioning by the host's own
seed-oil standard.** `[00:00]` "A lot of seed oils, even at a healthy vegan restaurant."

## What the staff said

`[00:00]` — In response to a direct question about food-sensitivity-relevant oils: **fryer** — rice
bran ("gluten-free") oil; **saute** — grapeseed oil; **dishes/toppings** — olive oil. The host adds
specific linoleic acid content for the two seed oils: **grapeseed oil ~60% linoleic acid**, **rice
bran oil ~30-40% linoleic acid** — figures stated directly rather than attributed to a specific lab
analysis of this restaurant's actual oil.
