# 3 more kitchen hacks

## Central claim

**The host names three kitchen practices he follows: filtering tap water with a countertop reverse
osmosis system, using only wood or bamboo cutting boards and utensils instead of plastic, and
avoiding seed oils entirely in favor of liberal use of animal fats.** `[00:00]` "Doing these simple
three things in your kitchen will vastly improve the quality of your health and the quality of the
food that you are preparing."

## The three practices

`[00:00]` — **Water**: a countertop reverse osmosis filter to remove "chemicals and pollutants"
from tap water. **Cutting surfaces and utensils**: wood only, never plastic, to avoid microplastics,
phthalates, or xenoestrogens shedding into food — the host notes he often eats directly off wood
cutting boards. **Cooking fat**: no seed oils ("these are garbage. They have no place in your
food"), using tallow, ghee, or butter liberally instead.
