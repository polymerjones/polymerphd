# Eat 80/20 ground beef

## Central claim

**The host argues fattier ground beef (80/20, or at least 85/15) is both more enjoyable to eat and
more nutritious than lean 95/5 ground beef, rejecting the premise that animal fat is unhealthy and
recommending fattier cuts as "most of what I eat."** `[00:00]` "You've been told animal fats are
bad for you. I say animal fats are essential for optimal human health."

## The comparison

`[00:00]` — **95/5 (95% lean, 5% fat)**: described as dry, unenjoyable, requiring "a ton of
butter" to make palatable — the host says he doesn't even consider it food. **85/15 or 80/20**
(15-20% fat): described as more delicious and containing "many more of those good animal fats." The
recommendation: seek out ground beef "as fatty as possible," at minimum 85/15.
