# How raw butter is made

## Central claim

**Touring how Raw Farm's cultured raw butter is made (raw cream cultured with probiotic bacteria,
then churned and packaged), the host argues this specific product is exceptionally beneficial for
gut health because of the concentration of immunoglobulins, peptides, and other compounds in raw
milk fat.** `[00:00]` "It's difficult for me to think of anything that's better for your gut than
the fat from milk."

## The process and the claim

`[00:00]` — Process: raw cream from "a truly raw farm" is cultured with bacteria, churned into
butter, cut into sticks, wrapped, and packaged by machine. The health claim: raw milk fat
concentrates **immunoglobulins, peptides, and "healing compounds"** that the host says make cultured
raw butter specifically beneficial for gut health — stated as a recommendation particularly for
people with existing gut issues.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Gut issues generally** — the host frames cultured raw butter as something people with gut
  issues are "missing out" on if they're not eating it `[00:00]`.
