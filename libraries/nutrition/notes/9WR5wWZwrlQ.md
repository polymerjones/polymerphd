# How to cook the perfect steak

## Central claim

**The host recommends using a meat thermometer to cook steak to precise doneness — pulling it off
the grill at 118°F for rare, 123°F for medium-rare, or 127-128°F for medium, a few degrees below the
final target, then resting it about five minutes before cutting.** `[00:00]` "Do not fear red meat,
use a meat thermometer on the grill, pull it off a few degrees shy of what you want it, let it sit
for about five minutes, cut into it and enjoy one of the most nutritious healthy foods on the
planet."

## The temperature guide

`[00:00]` — Pull temperatures given: **118°F** for rare, **123°F** for medium-rare, **127-128°F**
for medium. The host states a personal preference for ribeye cooked medium-rare. The technique:
insert the thermometer while grilling, pull a few degrees below the target (carryover cooking during
the five-minute rest brings it to final temperature), then rest before cutting.
