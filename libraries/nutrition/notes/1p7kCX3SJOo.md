# KIND bar is not so kind…

## Central claim

**The host questions how a KIND bar can carry a "heart-healthy" claim (based on its peanut
content) when its second and third listed ingredients are both forms of sugar (glucose syrup and
sugar), calling the labeling "total bullshit."** `[00:00]` "How exactly are peanuts heart-healthy,
and how can you even write heart-healthy on a snack bar when the second ingredient is glucose
syrup... and the third ingredient is sugar?"

## The ingredient-order argument

`[00:00]` — The host's case rests on ingredient list ordering (ingredients are listed by descending
quantity): peanuts are the marketed "heart-healthy" ingredient, but glucose syrup and sugar occupy
the second and third positions, meaning they make up a larger share of the product than any single
health claim would suggest. No specific gram amount or percentage is given.
