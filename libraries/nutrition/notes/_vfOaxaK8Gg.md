# Avoid folic acid…

## Central claim

**Citing a study on umbilical cord blood, the host argues folic acid — the synthetic form of
folate used in most prenatal vitamins and enriched foods — is linked to a significantly higher rate
of autism spectrum disorder in Black children when unmetabolized folic acid levels are elevated,
and recommends checking prenatal vitamin labels for L-5-methylfolate instead.** `[00:00]` "Folic
acid and unmetabolized folic acid are potentially very harmful for humans and for babies."

## The cited study and the fix

`[00:00]` — The study is described as finding **"higher levels of unmetabolized folic acid... in
umbilical cord blood in Black children... associated with a significantly higher rate of autism
spectrum disorders"** — a specific population and exposure marker, though the study isn't named by
author or journal. A Whole Foods 365 prenatal multivitamin is checked on camera as an example
containing folic acid despite its "healthy" branding. The recommended fix: **only consume folate
added as L-5-methylfolate**, described as "the natural form of folate."

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Autism spectrum disorder** — associated with elevated unmetabolized folic acid in cord blood,
  in the specific population (Black children) the cited study covered `[00:00]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Unmetabolized folic acid** — synthetic folic acid that hasn't been converted to its active
