# What's in a Pop Tart?

## Central claim

**The host runs through Pop-Tarts' ingredient list and names four specific concerns — corn syrup/
HFCS, seed oils, caramel color, and Red 40 — arguing these make it one of "the worst things you
could be eating or feeding your kids," and recommends jerky, fruit, or yogurt as substitutes.**
`[00:00]` "What the heck is in Pop-Tarts? Let's find out."

## The four flagged ingredients

`[00:00]` — Stated in sequence, each without a named study: **corn syrup and high-fructose corn
syrup**, said to be "clearly linked to obesity in both animal and human trials"; **soybean and palm
oil** (seed oils), which the host calls "the single greatest driver of obesity and chronic illness
in humans" — his own standing position, not unique to this product; **caramel color**, "a synthetic
additive linked to cancers in animal models"; and **Red 40**, "a dye that is commonly associated
with ADHD and anxiety behaviors in kids and adults."

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **ADHD and anxiety behaviors in kids and adults** — attributed specifically to Red 40 `[00:00]`.
