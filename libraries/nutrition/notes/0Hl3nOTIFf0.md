# Is the medical system broken?

## Central claim

**A psychiatrist (unnamed in this clip, speaking in first person) describes leaving clinical
practice because psychiatric residency training offered no ability to address diet despite
neuroinflammation being, in their view, the root cause of most psychiatric illness — and extends
this to a broader pattern of medicine dividing "brain and body," citing gastroenterologists telling
IBD patients there's no diet connection to their disease.** `[00:00]` "There's no ability to treat
anything with diet. It's all medications... that was really why I left medicine."

## The claim, and the career decision behind it

`[00:00]` — Training context: **residency at the University of Washington**, described as "one of
the most preeminent universities in the country." The speaker states psychiatric illness is rooted
in **neuroinflammation**, itself triggered by something dietary ("it's something coming from
here"), but that dietary intervention was institutionally unavailable as a treatment option —
**"medications, don't worry about the side effects."** This is stated as the direct reason for
leaving clinical practice: **"I've had this just complete loss of religion"** — while remaining a
board-certified, licensed physician who now works "educationally" rather than in practice. The
speaker extends the pattern beyond psychiatry: patients with **Crohn's and ulcerative colitis**
seeing gastroenterologists are reportedly told **"there's no connection with diet. It's literally in
your gut."**

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Psychiatric illness generally** — attributed to neuroinflammation with a dietary trigger,
  rather than addressed as a standalone brain condition `[00:00]`.
- **Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis** — cited as conditions where gastroenterology, in the
  speaker's account, denies any diet connection despite occurring "literally in your gut"
  `[00:00]`.
