# McDonald’s French fries = anxiety??

## Central claim

**Citing a newly released study, the host reports fried foods — especially those fried in seed oils
like McDonald's French fries — were associated with a 12% increase in anxiety and a 7% increase in
depression, proposing that heated, oxidized polyunsaturated seed oils break down into harmful
byproducts causing neuroinflammation as the mechanism.** `[00:00]` "French fries are McDonald's
number one selling food and they could be contributing to a huge amount of mental illness."

## The study and the proposed mechanism

`[00:00]` — Cited findings: fried foods associated with a 12% increase in anxiety and 7% increase in
depression. The host explicitly notes this is **observational research** and "correlation is not
causation" before proposing a mechanism: French fries are fried in corn, canola, soybean, and
partially hydrogenated soybean oil; when these polyunsaturated seed oils are heated, they break down
into products the host states "clearly cause inflammation in the brain," or **neuroinflammation**.
He frames the fried-food-to-mental-illness link as "a very compelling hypothesis" rather than an
established fact.
