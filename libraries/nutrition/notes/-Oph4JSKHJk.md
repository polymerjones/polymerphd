# Don't buy THIS chicken

## Central claim

**The host argues that unless a chicken's label specifically says "air chilled," it has likely
been through a chlorine-bath chilling process that leaves retained chlorinated water — and chemicals
along with it — in the meat, citing figures from up to 12% down to roughly 3-10% depending on the
specific product, even for organic chicken.** `[00:00]` "Unless your chicken says it's air chilled,
it is full of chemicals and chlorine that are absorbed into the chicken when it is chilled."

## The specific figures cited

`[00:00]` — Checked directly against product labels: **organic Costco chicken (not air-chilled)** —
up to 7% retained water; **party wings/drumsticks** — up to 3%; **chicken thighs** — up to 9%;
**a second party-wings example** — almost 10%. The host frames all of this retained water as
"potentially full of chlorine and other chemicals they're using to sterilize the chicken," and
recommends specifically seeking out chicken labeled both **organic and air-chilled**, noting it's
"a little hard to find."

## Source-stated confidence

Moderate. The retained-water percentages are read directly from product labels on camera, which is
stronger sourcing than most claims in this library, though the leap from "retained water" to "full
of chlorine and other chemicals" isn't independently quantified — the label discloses retained
water percentage, not chlorine concentration specifically.

figure for organic Costco chicken specifically, suggesting the percentage varies by product/batch

mechanism, differing only in the specific percentage cited per product.
