# Why are you chewing gum??

## Central claim

**Chewing gum is rejected on ingredient-list grounds — the first four ingredients are sugar-related
(gum base, dextrose, corn syrup), and even sugar-substitute varieties are argued to disrupt gut
flora — with the suggestion to simply chew food instead.** `[00:00]` "The ingredients, processed
sugar, gum base — who knows what that is — dextrose, corn syrup, the first four ingredients are
sugar. And even the ones that are not sugar, they're sugar substitutes, are gonna mess up your gut
flora."

in this clip.
