# Yes, I was once a raw vegan…

## Central claim

**The host recounts three distinct dietary phases — seven months of raw veganism that caused
digestive distress, fatigue, eczema, and significant muscle loss; a transition to strict carnivore
that resolved the eczema but caused electrolyte imbalance and muscle cramps; and his current
animal-based diet, which he describes as the phase where he is "truly thriving."** `[00:00]` "It
was horrible for me. I had terrible gas and bloating... I had fatigue. I had eczema. I was 25
pounds of lean muscle mass lighter... Then I transitioned to a strict carnivore diet. That helped
with my eczema... but I developed many issues with electrolyte imbalance and had muscle cramps.
I've ended up on an animal-based diet... and now I... am truly thriving."

## The three phases

`[00:00]` — **Raw vegan (7 months):** two heads of kale/day, broccoli, radishes, tomatoes, oat
milk, some nuts, a little fruit. **Strict carnivore:** meat, organs, fat only. **Current
animal-based:** organs, meat, fruit, honey, raw dairy.
