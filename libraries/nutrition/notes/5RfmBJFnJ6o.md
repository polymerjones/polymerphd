# Avoid these items in your bathroom

## Central claim

**The host shows his deliberately minimal bathroom routine — no facial products or lotions beyond
tallow sunscreen when surfing — and explains his specific choices for PFAS-free floss and a
plant-based (castor oil bristle) toothbrush to avoid PFAS absorption and microplastic exposure,
while flagging an unresolved annoyance: a blue LED light built into the toothbrush that undermines
his red-light nighttime setup.** `[00:00]` "This is all I use on a daily basis, guys."

## The specific choices

`[00:00]` — **Floss**: avoids PFAS-containing ("Gore-Tex"-style) floss, citing a study finding
women who used **Glide floss** had significantly higher PFAS levels in their bodies, with the
mechanism being absorption through gum tension or swallowing during flossing. Uses a **PFAS-free
floss** instead. **Toothbrush**: has used a boar's-hair toothbrush and recently upgraded to a
**plant-based bristle toothbrush** made from **castor oil**, specifically to avoid microplastics
from plastic bristles. **Unresolved issue**: the plant-based toothbrush has a built-in blue LED
light, which he says disrupts his otherwise red-light nighttime environment — "why would you do
that?"
