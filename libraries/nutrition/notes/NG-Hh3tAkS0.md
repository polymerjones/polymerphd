# Not so "Whole Foods"..

## Central claim

**A Whole Foods hot bar is characterized as "a train wreck" on two separate fronts: almost every
item is cooked in expeller-pressed canola oil, and the serving packaging itself (plastic-lined
cardboard containers, butcher paper) contains PFAS — extending the concern from food content to
packaging.** `[00:00]` "Almost every single one of the things in this hot bar has seed oils. But
that is not the only problem... the cardboard containers have a plastic lining, which has PFASs in
it... even the butcher paper has a plastic lining on it that's going to have PFASs."

## The two named exposure routes

`[00:00]` — **Seed oils:** expeller-pressed canola oil used across nearly the entire hot bar.
**PFAS:** named in plastic-lined food containers, butcher paper, popcorn bag linings, and (in an
aside) dental floss.
