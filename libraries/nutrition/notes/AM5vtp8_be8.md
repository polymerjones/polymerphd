# Here is how I would drink coffee…

## Central claim

**Despite not drinking coffee himself, the host lays out how he would do it: organic coffee brewed
with spring or reverse osmosis water using a French press or pour-over (avoiding plastic coffee pots
due to possible hormone-disrupting leaching from hot-water condensation), waiting 90 minutes after
waking and stopping by noon, using raw cream/milk and raw honey as additions, and replenishing lost
minerals with sea salt, coconut water, or fruit juice.** `[00:00]` "I would wait 90 minutes after I
woke up to drink the coffee and not drink the coffee after 12 p.m."

## The full protocol

`[00:00]` — **Coffee quality**: organic. **Water**: spring water or reverse osmosis water only.
**Brewing method**: French press or pour-over, explicitly avoiding a plastic coffee pot due to
condensation potentially leaching "hormone disrupting components from the plastic" into the coffee.
**Timing**: 90 minutes after waking before the first cup; no coffee after 12pm. **Additions**: raw
cream or milk, raw honey if sweetened. **Mineral replenishment**: natural sea salt, coconut water, or
orange/other fruit juices, to offset minerals lost from caffeine consumption.
