# The history of raw kefir!

## Central claim

**Resurfacing a clip originally removed from YouTube, the host argues raw milk kefir is an
incredibly nutrient-dense, gut-microbiome-supportive whole food with a history spanning thousands
of years across cultures (Maasai, Mongolian, European) and species (cattle, sheep, horses,
reindeer, camels, water buffalo), originating as an accidental byproduct of unwashed milking
containers acting as a natural starter culture.** `[00:00]` "It's so important and so helpful that
I really want you guys to see this."

## The history

`[00:00]` — Kefir's origin is framed as incidental rather than deliberate: milking containers
weren't cleaned between uses, so residue from the prior week's milk functioned as a local starter
culture, fermenting fresh milk into kefir within **six to ten hours**. This pattern is described as
occurring wherever humans cohabitated with milk-producing mammals — cattle, sheep, horses,
reindeer, camels, water buffalo — across cultures including the Maasai, Mongolians, and Europeans.
The host frames raw milk kefir's bacterial diversity as a key benefit for gut microbiome health.
