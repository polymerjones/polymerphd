# Creatine Makes Vegans Smarter 💪

## Central claim

**Citing randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials showing creatine supplementation
improves memory in vegetarian women and cognition in the elderly, the host argues creatine should
come from meat and organs rather than supplements — using the optimal daily creatine amount to
back-calculate a recommended daily meat-and-organ intake.** `[00:00]` "I don't think we should be
supplementing with creatine. I think we should be getting this from our food, from meat and organs.
And that the optimal amount of creatine for humans is a clear indication of the amount of meat and
organs that we should be eating every day."

## The quantified recommendation

`[00:00]` — **~5g creatine/day for a 170 lb male** ≈ "a little more than 2 pounds of meat and
organs per day." **~3g creatine/day for a 115 lb female** ≈ "a little more than a pound of meat per
day."

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Memory** — reported improved in vegetarian women given creatine, per a cited RCT `[00:00]`.
- **Cognition** — reported improved in elderly subjects given creatine, per cited RCTs `[00:00]`.

Moderate for the underlying research claim — described as coming from "randomized, double-blind,
