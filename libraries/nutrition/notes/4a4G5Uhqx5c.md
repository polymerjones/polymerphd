# Are humans broken??

## Central claim

**The host argues medical school teaches, implicitly, that humans are broken and can't fix
themselves, and rejects this — arguing movement, sunlight exposure, sleep, and diet are "the best
medicine" capable of resolving a wide list of conditions including rheumatoid arthritis, eczema,
psoriasis, depression, anxiety, insomnia, infertility, and menstrual problems, which would make
pharmaceuticals and mainstream medicine unnecessary for many people.** `[00:00]` "If you are
empowered to understand that you can fix your rheumatoid arthritis, your eczema, your psoriasis,
your depression, your anxiety, your insomnia, your infertility, your menstrual irregularities, your
painful periods, your PMS, then what do you need a pharmaceutical for?"

## The claim's scope

`[00:00]` — A notably long list of conditions is claimed fixable through "the way we move, the way
we see the sun, the way we sleep and what we eat": **rheumatoid arthritis, eczema, psoriasis,
depression, anxiety, insomnia, infertility, menstrual irregularities, painful periods, and PMS.** No
individual mechanism or study is given for any specific condition in this short — it's delivered as
a sweeping philosophical position about medicine's framing of the body rather than condition-by-
condition evidence.
