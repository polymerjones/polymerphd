# How I cut a pineapple

## Central claim

**A straightforward technique demonstration: storing pineapple upside down so juice redistributes
downward, then cutting off the top, bottom, and sides, quartering it, removing the fibrous core,
and finishing with a low-microplastic sea salt.** `[00:00]` "That is an upside down pineapple... all
the juice goes down."

## The technique

`[00:00]` — Store pineapple upside down. Cut off the top and bottom, then the sides, then quarter
it lengthwise. Remove the fibrous center core (composted rather than eaten). Optionally finish with
salt — the host specifies "low microplastic sea salt."
