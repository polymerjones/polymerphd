# How to shop for butter 🧈

## Central claim

**The host demonstrates a butter-shopping hierarchy: raw (unpasteurized) butter first, grass-fed
organic butter as a second choice, and ghee (clarified butter with milk solids removed) recommended
specifically for cooking to avoid smoking.** `[00:00]` "Raw is my favorite, but these are my second
choices."

## The hierarchy and the label distinctions

`[00:00]` — **Raw butter**: unheated, unpasteurized — top choice. **Grass-fed vs. organic**,
explicitly distinguished: grass-fed means cows eat only grass; organic means no pesticide-sprayed
feed contact — the two don't always overlap. Examples shown: a pasture-raised, organic butter that
"might not be entirely grass-fed"; a truly grass-fed butter imported from Ireland; a butter not
certified organic but judged likely good anyway. **Ghee**: recommended for cooking specifically to
avoid butter's tendency to smoke at high heat, since milk solids (the component that burns) are
removed.
