# Boost your kids immune system

## Central claim

**Reacting to a video of Danish kids playing in mud, the host argues studies show children exposed
to pets, daycare centers, or farm environments have lower rates of asthma, eczema, and allergies,
and that the US is overly obsessive about cleanliness — including hand soaps containing triclosan,
a potential endocrine disruptor and gut microbiome disruptor.** `[00:00]` "Being a little more dirty
than we are in the United States with our super hygienic environment is good for kids' immune
systems."

## The claim and the caveat

`[00:00]` — Cited pattern: kids exposed to pets, daycare, or farm environments show "lower rates of
asthma, eczema, and allergies." Named concern: soaps containing **triclosan**, flagged as a
potential endocrine disruptor and gut microbiome disruptor, plus general over-sterilization of
countertops. The host explicitly caveats this is not an anti-hygiene stance — "I believe in hygiene.
I think that we should wash our hands" — but argues outdoor, less-sheltered play should be added
back in.
