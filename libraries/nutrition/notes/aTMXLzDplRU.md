# My thoughts on methylene blue

## Central claim

**The host argues methylene blue, despite trending in the health space as a mitochondrial booster,
actually decreases ATP production for most people (those without a genetic block in the
mitochondrial electron transport chain), and that its "feel better" effect comes instead from its
action as a strong MAOA inhibitor raising brain serotonin — which can cause serotonin syndrome when
combined with antidepressants or other serotonergic compounds.** `[00:00]` "For most people,
methylene blue is worsening your energy production, but you are being misled by the fact that the
MAOA inhibition is raising serotonin, making you feel a certain way that is not reflective of
increased functioning mitochondria or better energy production."

## The mechanism, split in two

`[00:00]` — **How it works**: methylene blue moves electrons down the mitochondrial electron
transport chain — genuinely useful for someone with a specific genetic block in that chain, but the
host states "most of us don't have that." For everyone else, he argues methylene blue **decreases**
ATP production rather than improving it. **Why people feel better anyway**: methylene blue is a
strong **MAOA (monoamine oxidase A) inhibitor**, raising serotonin levels — this subjective effect is
mistaken for improved mitochondrial function. `[01:12]` — **Drug interaction risk**: combining
methylene blue with antidepressants, 5-HTP, rhodiola, SAMe, or St. John's wort risks **serotonin
syndrome** due to compounded serotonergic effects. **Physical side effects noted**: blue tongue,
and the host states it can also turn the brain and heart blue. **Alternative recommendation**: for
most people who want to be metabolically healthy, the host recommends meat, organs, fruit, raw
dairy, sunlight, and exercise instead.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Lower energy production** — attributed to methylene blue decreasing ATP output in people without
  an electron transport chain defect `[00:00]`.
- **Serotonin syndrome risk** — attributed to combining methylene blue with other serotonergic
  compounds or antidepressants `[01:12]`.

## Glossary terms introduced

- **Electron transport chain** — the mitochondrial process methylene blue interacts with, moving
  electrons through it `[00:00]`.
- **MAOA (monoamine oxidase A) inhibitor** — an enzyme-blocking mechanism that raises serotonin;
  methylene blue is described as a strong one `[00:00]`.
- **Serotonin syndrome** — a risk from combining serotonin-raising compounds, including methylene
  blue with antidepressants or supplements like 5-HTP `[01:12]`.
