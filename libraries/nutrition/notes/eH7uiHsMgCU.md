# Are you thriving??

## Central claim

**Responding to people who say "I could never do that" about his recommendations (cutting coffee,
sourdough bread, sugar, seed oils, or earbuds), the host reframes his advice as conditional: if
someone is genuinely thriving, he says there's no reason to change anything, but if they are
honestly not thriving, the information is offered as a path forward — while stressing that only the
individual can honestly assess which is true for them.** `[00:00]` "If you are thriving, why change
anything in your life?... I think many of you are not truly thriving, but you are not willing to
address those things that may help you reach a more optimal state. But that's fine. It's your
decision."
