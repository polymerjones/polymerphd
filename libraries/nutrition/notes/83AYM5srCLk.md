# Almond milk vs. raw milk

## Central claim

**The host argues raw milk is "way healthier" than almond milk on multiple grounds — many almond
milks contain seed oils and gut-damaging thickeners (carrageenan), almonds themselves are
oxalate-rich seeds linked to kidney stones, and raw milk provides nutrients (choline, K2,
riboflavin, bioavailable calcium) absent from almond milk — closing with a claim that almond milk
products are consistently associated with kidney and genitourinary issues in kids.** `[00:00]`
"Hands down, raw milk from cows or goats... way healthier than almond milk."

## The case against almond milk

`[00:00]` — **Seed oils**: the example almond milk checked contains sunflower oil. **Thickeners**:
many almond and oat milks contain carrageenan or "gelangun" (likely gellan gum), said to damage the
gut. **Oxalates**: even a pure almond milk (almonds only) would still concern the host, since
almonds are seeds "very high in oxalates," linked to kidney stones. **Kidney/genitourinary claim**:
almond milk and its products are said to be "consistently associated with kidney issues and genital
urinary tract issues in kids" — no specific study named in this clip.

## Symptoms and body signals addressed

- **Kidney stones** — attributed to oxalates in almonds `[00:00]`.
- **Kidney and genitourinary issues in kids** — associated with almond milk and almond milk
  products `[00:00]`.
- **Asthma, eczema, and allergies in kids** — associated instead with raw cow's milk, in a
  favorable direction `[00:00]`.
