# You DON'T need lower cholesterol…

## Central claim

**The host discloses his own cholesterol is high and that doctors have recommended a statin, which
he declines because he considers himself metabolically healthy — arguing LDL cholesterol is a poor
predictor of cardiovascular disease in metabolically healthy people, and that cholesterol itself
plays beneficial roles in immune response, hormone/fertility health, and longevity.** `[00:00]` "The
question you should be asking is are you metabolically healthy... if you are metabolically healthy
[you] don't care if your cholesterol goes up a little bit if you eat red meat."

## The claim and the personal disclosure

`[00:00]` — Personal disclosure: the host's own cholesterol is elevated; multiple doctors have
recommended a statin; he declines, citing his metabolic health as the reason not to worry.
**Test recommended**: fasting insulin, described as an easy way to assess metabolic health.
**Core claim**: in metabolically healthy people, LDL cholesterol is "horribly bad" at predicting
cardiovascular disease. **Cholesterol's other roles**: participation in immune response, healthy
hormone levels and fertility, and association with longevity in old age.
