# Grocery stores: modern hunting grounds for humans #animalbased

## Central claim

**The host frames grocery stores as modern hunting grounds where humans have lost their intuitive
sense of what to select, resulting in the selection of foods full of artificial flavors, sweeteners,
thickeners, pesticides, and vegetable peel coatings — and argues remembering our evolutionary
origins points clearly toward meat, organs, and in-season sweet foods.** `[00:00]` "We're seeking
meat, organs with the meat, and we're seeking sweet things that are in season. That's how you
remember how to hunt in a place like this."
