# Is “dead” dairy negatively affecting our kids?

## Central claim

**The host argues raw milk — illegal to obtain in 37 US states — is one of the most healing foods
for children, citing anecdotal reports from hundreds of parents describing improvements in gut
discomfort, diarrhea, constipation, bloating, inflammatory bowel disease, eczema, and sleep issues
after switching children from pasteurized ("dead") dairy to raw dairy, and calls for those states to
legalize raw milk access.** `[00:00]` "I'm calling on those 37 states to wake up and to allow you to
be able to get raw milk and raw dairy because this food is healing for humans."

## The claim and the legal landscape

`[00:00]` — **Legal status**: raw milk described as illegal in 37 US states. **Anecdotal pattern**:

inflammatory bowel diseases, eczema, and sleep issues. **Framing**: pasteurized milk described as
