# Kodiak Cakes are deceiving

## Central claim

**The host argues adding whey or wheat protein isolate to grain-based foods like Kodiak Cakes
pancake mix or Magic Spoon cereal doesn't make them healthy, because the base ingredient is still
whole grain wheat — carrying gluten (a lectin that disrupts the gut lining and causes leaky gut),
digestive enzyme inhibitors, and phytic acid (which blocks mineral absorption) regardless of added
protein.** `[00:00]` "Just adding protein from wheat or from whey protein to a food that is
basically wheat flour plus sugar... doesn't make it healthy at all. It just makes it a fake healthy
food."

## The mechanism

`[00:00]` — Named problems inherent to whole grain wheat, independent of added protein: **gluten**
(described as itself a lectin, disrupting the "gut intestinal lining, causing leaky gut"),
**digestive enzyme inhibitors**, and **phytic acid** ("which prevent the absorption of minerals").
The host names the base ingredients as whole grain wheat plus wheat protein isolate, sugar, dextrose,
and cane sugar, arguing the protein addition is a marketing overlay rather than a nutritional fix.
