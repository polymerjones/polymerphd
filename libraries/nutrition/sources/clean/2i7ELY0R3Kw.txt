[00:00] I don't think any of us believe there's a good way to die, but one of the scariest ways to end your life is probably the thought of dementia. Losing memory, losing a remembrance of who people are in your life who are important, what you've done in your life. But I believe dementia, Alzheimer's, are completely preventable. We know very strongly that Alzheimer's is connected with insulin resistance. It's called type 3 diabetes. Well, guess what? Diabetes is preventable. Insulin resistance is preventable and reversible. But why is no one talking about dietary interventions for Alzheimer's or the prevention of Alzheimer's? Why is no one talking about how to be metabolically healthy, treat or prevent this disease? How do you claim metabolic health? Eliminate seed oils, eliminate processed sugars. If more people did that, I believe our epidemic of dementia would vanish.
