Okay, I don't have any kefir grains right now, but once you have a kefir culture already going, this is kefir, you can also take raw milk and just add the kefir directly to it, and then in a day or two, depending on the temperature, this raw milk will become kefir, either because of the kefir grains or because you added some kefir that was already fermenting.
