[00:00] What's in a Nutri-Grain bar? Let's find out. These are supposed to be a healthy snack, but is that really true? We'll see. So in the crust of the Nutri-Grain bar, you have wheat with gluten, a lectin, which can damage the human gut, and you have seed oils like soybean oil in the crust, and you also have carrageenan, a sulfated polysaccharide from algae that is well-known to cause inflammation and damage to the gut. In animal models, invert sugar induces obesity and prediabetes quickly. The filling also contains corn syrup, so you're getting more of that glyphosate that comes along with any corn product. These are definitely not a healthy snack. In fact, these are an unhealthy snack. I would never eat them or feed them to kids.
