[00:00] Kombucha. You guys ask me about this all the time, bullshit or not. I don't drink nearly any store-bought kombucha because I think there's too much sugar in it. In order to make kombucha, you're adding sugar. Almost all kombuchas are going to have a processed cane sugar, a pure sucrose, to a culture. If you let it ferment long enough, that culture will turn the sugar into a fermentation product. Probiotics like kombucha have been studied in studies like this one, which show that fermented foods like kombucha or kefir are good for the gut microbiome. But I believe that most store-bought kombucha is way too high in sugar, way too high in unfermented sugar to be good for you. It still has processed sugar in it. Make your own kombucha and get it low in sugar and that can be beneficial for your gut. Like fermented foods like kefir, not vegetables or fiber. That's not what you need for your gut. So most store-bought kombucha, bullshit in my opinion, way too much sugar.
