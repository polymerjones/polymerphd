[00:00] Never mix your creatine with caffeine. It might abolish the positive effects of creatine. Hear me out on this, guys. There have been a couple of interesting studies done on this topic, one from 1996 and one more recently from 2022. The 1996 study looked at maximal torque for knee extension and found that when participants took creatine, maximal torque was increased with knee extension. But when that creatine was combined with caffeine, there were no performance benefits in terms of knee extension torque. Similarly, a more recent 2022 study also looked at knee extension but measured muscle thickness. They found that with creatine supplementation, muscle thickness increased. When that creatine was combined with caffeine, there was no change in muscle thickness. What could be going on here? Creatine shortens muscle relaxation time, allowing you to generate maximal force more quickly. Caffeine does the opposite, causing the release of calcium from the sarcoplasmic reticulum in your muscles, which lengthens muscle relaxation time. These are opposed, so it's possible that creatine and caffeine might cancel each other out a little bit when it comes to muscle relaxation time. So here's the solution. Separate the caffeine and the creatine. Research

[01:03] suggests three to four hours of separation is probably optimal. If you want the maximal benefits of the creatine, which has been repeatedly shown to be very beneficial for humans.
