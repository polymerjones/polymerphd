[00:00] You would have to eat over a pound of carrots to get the amount of bioavailable vitamin A found in one ounce of beef liver. Vitamin A is one of the most important micronutrients for humans, and half of the U.S. population is deficient in vitamin A, probably because we're not eating enough liver and we're eating way too many processed foods. But getting enough vitamin A in your diet is critical because it helps with immune function, reproductive function, healthy hormones, pregnancy, breastfeeding, healthy skin. All kinds of important things are associated with vitamin A. The vitamin A in things like carrots is beta-carotene. That's hard for the body to use and not very bioavailable. Your body has to convert that into the usable form of vitamin A, retinol vitamin A, which is what is found in liver. Like so many nutrients, vitamin A is more bioavailable and easier to get from animal meat and organs than it is from plants. The takeaway here is simple. Eating more meat and organs and not fearing these foods is the way to become optimally healthy. If you want to eat some carrots every once in a while, go ahead, but make sure you include liver in your diet if you truly want to thrive.
