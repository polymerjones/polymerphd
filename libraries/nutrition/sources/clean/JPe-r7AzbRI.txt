[00:00] It turns out that good old baking soda is the best way to get pesticides off of your produce. A 2017 study from the University of Massachusetts found that soaking your produce for 15 minutes in a 1% solution of baking soda, two teaspoons of baking soda per liter of water, reduced 99% of the pesticides on the surface of your fruit and vegetables. It doesn't get rid of all the pesticides that are inside the fruit, but this was significantly better than what you can achieve with vinegars. And there seems to be a trend now of adding vinegar to baking soda to rinse your produce. Don't do that. That neutralizes these because vinegar is acidic. Baking soda is alkaline. What you want is an alkaline solution to rinse your produce, to soak them, to deactivate the pesticides, to clean the pesticides off. So don't mix vinegar and baking soda to clean your produce. Baking soda, super cheap, super available. Use this to get rid of the pesticides on your fruit. 15 minutes soak, 1% solution, two teaspoons per liter. Super easy way to decrease these pesticides on your fruit and vegetables.
