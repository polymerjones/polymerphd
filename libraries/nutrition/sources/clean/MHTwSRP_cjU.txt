[00:00] Being in nature positively affects your brain chemistry. If you live in a city or urban environment, I challenge you to get into a natural landscape for 5-10 minutes a day. Here's why. With their straight lines, right angles, concrete and steel, cities have been called landscapes of despair. And it's true. We know from scientific research that being in cities with their right angles and straight lines activates the sympathetic nervous system in humans, the fight or flight nervous system. If you live in a city, you may not even appreciate how turned on your sympathetic nervous system is the majority of the time. You may be chronically stressed because of your surroundings. In contrast, a natural environment like this, with curved lines and fractals, calms our body, turns on the parasympathetic nervous system. This is what we need to truly thrive as humans.
