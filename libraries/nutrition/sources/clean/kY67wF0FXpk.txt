But a lot of those minerals are not bioavailable in plant foods. They're much more bioavailable in animal foods. If you listen to plant-based advocates, they will tell you that there's just as much zinc in lentils as there is in steak. What they're not telling you is the zinc in lentils or beans is not bioavailable. Meaning when you put that thing into your body and it goes to your stomach, you can't get the zinc out of the beans or out of the lentils and into your body. It just goes right out in your poop.
