You're heating the oil anyway if you're using it for cooking. Coconut oil is good. My preferred oils for cooking, if I'm going to heat them in a pan, are tallow, which is rendered beef fat, or raw butter. And if you're going to be heating it hot, use ghee because it's clarified butter and it won't burn. The smoke point of an oil is not the same as its peroxidation index. Peroxidation index tells you when the oil is going to become damaged and oxidized. Smoke point just tells you when it's going to start burning in the pan. So if you're going to cook with an oil, my preferred is tallow, coconut oil, butter, or ghee. Not this stuff.
