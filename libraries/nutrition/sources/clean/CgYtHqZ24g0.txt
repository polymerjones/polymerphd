[00:00] French fries are some of the most addictive foods on the planet. When you take potatoes and you deep fry them in seed oils, like peanut oil here at Five Guys, those seed oils break down. The polyunsaturated fatty acids break down into a compound called 4-HNE. There are studies like this one which clearly show that the levels of HNE in French fries is off the charts. In your body, HNE is directly linked to inflammation, cell membrane damage, mitochondrial dysfunction, and sabotaging your appetite, making you more hungry. French fries are delicious, I get it, but these are the worst foods that you can possibly eat if you want to lose weight and be healthy. They're only going to make you hungrier and lead to long-term health problems.
