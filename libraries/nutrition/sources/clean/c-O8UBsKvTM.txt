[00:00] This is why scrambling is the worst way to cook an egg. Eggs are amazing, but when you scramble an egg like this, all of the good nutrients in the yolk get a little bit denatured from the heat. Eating eggs is super healthy for humans, but scrambling is the worst way to eat your eggs. What's better? Anything that preserves the yolk a little bit. Anything that leaves the yolk soft and even a little bit runny. You can do soft, boiled, you can do poached, you can do sunny side up. Those are all better ways to cook your egg. The yolk of an egg is treasure. It's full of so many good nutrients. B vitamins, vitamin A, choline, biotin, folate, cholesterol. There's a lot of stuff in the egg yolk that's really good for you. It gets a little bit broken down when you scramble an egg like this. And look, if scrambling is all you've got time for, fine, great. A scrambled egg is better than no egg. But if you really want to be the best egg eater that you can be, try soft boiling, poached, or sunny side up to preserve that precious yolk.
