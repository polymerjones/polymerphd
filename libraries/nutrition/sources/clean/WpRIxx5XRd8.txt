Glyphosate is the most widely used herbicide in the United States today, and it is sprayed on so many of the foods we eat. It's probably in your house right now. In 2015, the International Agency on the Research of Cancer classified glyphosate as a group 2A carcinogen, stating that it was probably carcinogenic, cancer-causing in humans, and connecting it to non-Hodgkin's lymphoma. So what's the answer here? Choose organic, non-GMO food when you can. With things like honey, make sure that it's certified glyphosate-free. If you're eating meat, opt for grass-fed, grass-finished, regeneratively-raised beef, preferably from small local farms and farmers that you know. Those changes aren't always easy, but doing those things will almost entirely reduce your exposure to glyphosate in your diet.
