Farm-raised salmon is anything that says Atlantic salmon, but doesn't say wild-caught or wild-raised. These salmon are raised on farms. They're fed horrible food. They're fed grains. They're fed fish food, which is full of things like perfluoroalkyl compounds, PFAs, which are also known as forever chemicals, hormone disruptors. It's full of BPA, PCBs, which are polychlorinated biphenyls. It's full of microplastics. And all of those things get concentrated in an unhealthy fish raised in a pen, eating garbage food, eating basically the fish equivalent of processed food, and the flesh of that salmon is white, but they add a dye to convince you, to trick you into thinking that it's something like a wild salmon. So when it comes to fish, be careful. A lot of fish is very high in heavy metals. Look for fish that is low in heavy metals and preferably not farm-raised. Interestingly, there are a couple of farm-raised fish operations like Seatopia that are trying to do this better.
