[00:00] Diamond kosher salt versus Celtic sea salt. I've definitely used a lot of Celtic sea salt in my life, and I used to think this was good, but unfortunately, I was wrong. In a recent independent analysis, Celtic sea salt was full of heavy metals, aluminum, arsenic, cadmium, and lead. In fact, Celtic sea salt had lead that was so high that an average consumption of salt per day, around nine grams of salt, you would get almost 10 times the tolerable upper limit for lead in your salt. So contrast that with Diamond kosher salt. This had essentially no heavy metals at all. This was much cleaner than I expected, so I've changed what kind of salt I use. I don't use this anymore, and I've started using this. I wanna see some more tests to back all this up, but it's really striking how many heavy metals can be in salt, and this is important to know for your health.
