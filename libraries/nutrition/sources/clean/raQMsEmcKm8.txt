[00:00] Steak for diabetes? A new study suggests that the amino acid taurine found in red meat, chicken, turkey, and other animal foods is clearly beneficial for people with metabolic syndrome. This was a meta-analysis looking at 25 randomized controlled trials studying more than 1,000 humans who supplemented with taurine, this amino acid. Good sources of taurine include steak, hamburger, red meat in general, organ meats like heart and liver, as well as chicken, turkey, and fish. There are so many nutrients in these foods like taurine, which has also been studied and found to be beneficial for longevity that are vital for our health and help us live our best lives. In this study, doses of taurine from 0.5 grams per day to six grams per day improved human health, including systolic blood pressure, diastolic blood pressure, fasting blood glucose, and triglycerides, all indicators of metabolic syndrome, a condition directly connected with diabetes. Clear evidence that taurine is yet again beneficial for humans, another win for animal foods.
