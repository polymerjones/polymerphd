[00:00] Is your salt full of heavy metals like lead? There was a recent independent analysis of heavy metals and sea salts that blew my mind. Three of these sea salts had levels of heavy metals like lead that were pretty darn high. I used to use Celtic sea salt, but in nine grams of this salt, the average of most people consume in a day, you would get almost 10 times the limit from the California Prop 65 guidelines, which is 0.5 micrograms of lead per day. Now, a lot of people feel like that level of lead is pretty darn low, but the FDA tolerable limit of lead in a day is 12 micrograms. So to get almost 5 micrograms of lead in a day from 9 grams of salt is a pretty significant contribution to your lead intake. This salt had some of the highest lead in it of any of these. Unfortunately, Redmond Real Salt also had a significant amount of lead and other heavy metals. Know what is in the salt you're eating, and you can significantly decrease your intake of heavy metals like lead.
