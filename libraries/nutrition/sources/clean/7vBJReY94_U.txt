[00:00] I don't eat rice, but if I did, here's how I would do it. I would choose an organic white rice rather than a brown rice because we know that brown rice has much higher levels of heavy metals like arsenic than white rice. And then I would take that white rice and do a lot of things to prepare it before I cooked it. I would rinse it many times. Then I would soak it overnight in warm water with apple cider vinegar to help with the further breakdown of problematic compounds in the rice. And then I would pressure cook it to break down any more lectins even further. And then I would eat it with a good grass-fed butter and good salt. For me, rice doesn't work. Even when I do all those things, it still kind of gives me brain fog and makes me feel a little funny. But if rice works for you as a form of carbohydrates, then great. Think about how you're preparing your rice. And if you take those steps and you're more careful with it, I think you'll get the most benefits with the least problems from your white rice.
