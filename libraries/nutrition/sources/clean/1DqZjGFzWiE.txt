[00:00] I prefer eating a whole egg rather than eating egg whites. But it wasn't always this way. When I was in college, I ate egg whites because I bought into the mainstream idea that egg yolks are bad for you because they contain cholesterol. But this notion has been widely debunked now, and we know that egg yolks are not bad for humans, even though they contain cholesterol, and that egg yolks contain so many valuable nutrients that you want to get. You want vitamin A, you want choline, you want riboflame, and you want folate. All of these are rich in the yolk of an egg, and you're going to miss out on a lot of these valuable things if you only eat egg whites. So to me, it makes the most sense to obviously eat the whole egg. In the white, there's a lot of good bioavailable protein and some nutrients. In the yolk, there are even more unique nutrients that aren't found in the white in a lot of cases, and the combination of those makes it incredibly healthy food for humans. Don't fear egg yolks because they contain cholesterol. Dietary cholesterol has a very minimal effect on serum cholesterol, and if you are metabolically healthy, I don't even think it's a big deal anyway. So don't get egg whites. Just get the whole egg.
