Avocado and olive oil. I think a lot of you guys probably cook in these, but I would not cook in these oils. They're better than seed oils, but they're going to oxidize when you cook in them. What I would prefer in terms of a plant oil is something like coconut oil. You would want a virgin organic coconut oil, but my favorites are animal fats. Animal fats are where the real nutrients are. Fat-soluble vitamins, vitamins A, D, E, K2. And in butter and ghee, you're going to get butyric acid, which is an anti-inflammatory compound even used to treat inflammatory bowel disease. If you're going to cook in oil, use tallow or ghee. Coconut oil is a third, and I would not cook in olive oil or avocado oil.
