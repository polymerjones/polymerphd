[00:00] Don't make this mistake when you're shopping for sourdough bread. A lot of sourdough bread contains an ingredient you do not want to be eating. So sourdough bread is definitely better than regular bread. But most sourdough breads are made with enriched wheat flour. Enriched wheat flour contains folic acid. What is folic acid? Folic acid is a synthetic form of folate. It does not occur in nature. And so this leads to unmetabolized folic acid in your body, in your blood, when you consume folic acid in supplements or in breads and in flours. In fact, studies show that 90 plus percent of humans consuming supplements or enriched flours have unmetabolized folic acid in their body. It can lead to reduced immune function. It's actually associated with some cancers, like colon cancers in some people. And it can lead to potentially issues with neurologic development in children or fetuses. We do not want to be eating folic acid. So be careful when you are shopping for sourdough. The simple solution here is finding a sourdough from a baker that doesn't use an enriched flour. It should just have a wheat flour, water, and salt. That's it. Send this to someone you know who is eating the wrong type of sourdough.
