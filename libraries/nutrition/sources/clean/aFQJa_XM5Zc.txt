[00:00] Western medicine doesn't teach doctors anything about nutrition, and this is hurting so many people. I spent four years at the College of William & Mary doing my undergraduate pre-med education. After that, I spent two years at George Washington University in Washington, D.C. to get my master's to become a physician assistant. And I practiced for four years as a PA in cardiology. And during those four years, really, zero conversation about the root cause of cardiovascular illness or how we help patients correct the root of their cardiovascular disease. After that, I went to medical school at the University of Arizona for four years. Zero courses in nutrition. Again, zero focus on the root cause of disease. Almost all of our education in medical school was about pharmaceutical drugs and how to use them to treat symptoms. Finally, I spent four years at the University of Washington in residency. Again, zero courses in nutrition. No focus on what could be causing patients' illnesses. I've come to believe that this lack of focus on nutrition in medical education is hurting millions and millions of people.
